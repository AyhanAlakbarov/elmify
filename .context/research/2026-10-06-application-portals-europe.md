- **[K]** means background knowledge that was NOT re-checked this session. Treat every [K] claim, and every host marked [K], as UNVERIFIED and re-fetch before the product relies on it as a recognition rule.

## Patterns

1. **Central single-step:** Sweden (`universityadmissions.se`, confirmed) and Finland (`studyinfo.fi` / `opintopolku.fi` joint application for English master's, confirmed; document follow-ups UNVERIFIED).
2. **Two-step chains:** Netherlands (Studielink then the university's Osiris-type system, confirmed); Italy (university platform first, then Universitaly pre-enrolment for the visa, confirmed); France (Études en France then the university or Mon Master; Azerbaijan is on the list, confirmed; Mon Master hand-off UNVERIFIED); Germany (uni-assist then sometimes the university portal; only some universities); Hungary and Russia via scholarship portals.
3. **University-own (the default):** Denmark (confirmed: optagelse.dk is bachelor's only), Austria, Belgium, Czechia, Poland, Switzerland, Norway (Søknadsweb per university), the Baltics, Balkans, Caucasus and most remaining states. All UNVERIFIED except Denmark.
4. **Shared SaaS / vendors:** uni-assist (Germany, many universities); PAC (Ireland, some universities); Osiris, DreamApply (Estonia, others), CAMPUSonline (Austria), Esse3 (Italy), eCandidat (France), SITS/Slate (UK). Host-pattern rules for each are UNVERIFIED and need a fetch.
5. **Scholarship-forced separate portals:** Stipendium Hungaricum (`apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu`), Türkiye Bursları (`turkiyeburslari.gov.tr`), Russian Government quota (`education-in-russia.com`); all [K], UNVERIFIED.
