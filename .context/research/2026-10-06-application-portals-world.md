## Patterns

1. University-own portal on the university domain: the majority. Canada (UBC, Waterloo), Australia (UQ), NZ (Massey, Auckland, Otago), Singapore (NUS GDA3, NTU), Brunei (UBD), Korea (KAIST, SNU), China (Tsinghua), Vietnam (VNU), Saudi (KFUPM, KAUST), UAE, Qatar (QU), Bahrain, Kuwait, Africa (Ghana, Uganda, Kenya, South Africa), US in-house systems (Georgia Tech).
2. Shared SaaS where the host may be a vendor domain: US (Slate, hosted under `apply.*`/`applygrad.*.edu` or the vendor domain; CollegeNET Admit and ApplyWeb; Liaison GradCAS), Qatar (HBKU on `hbkuqa.elluciancrmrecruit.com`), NZ/Massey (SITS vision under `massey.ac.nz`), Ontario (OUAC for TMU and Guelph). Slate usually appears on the university's own domain, so host matching alone will miss it. Watch for `technolutions.net`.
3. Central systems: OUAC (Ontario graduate, only some universities), UAC and QTAC (Australia, optional and programme-limited), Study in Korea (GKS-G only). No country has a mandatory central system for a self-funded master's.
4. Scholarship-forced separate portals (two-step chains): China (CSC `studyinchina.csc.edu.cn` plus the university form), Korea (GKS-G at `studyinkorea.go.kr` plus university admission), Japan (MEXT embassy track plus university), Mexico (AMEXCID/Conahcyt plus university, UNVERIFIED), Ontario/TMU (OUAC then university upload portal, a non-scholarship two-step).
5. Paper/email/post: Japan (EMS/DHL for some programmes, supervisor consent by email), Brazil/USP (exchange by email), Vietnam (VNU-IS email), Korea ("online or by mail" per university). Everywhere else it is rare.
6. Agent route: Australia (agents common; UQ forces agents for certain countries, list unconfirmed for Azerbaijan), NZ (agents offered, optional). Elsewhere no.

Gaps to close before relying on this: exact hosts for Unimelb, Auckland, Otago, McGill, UofT SGS, Slate/ApplyWeb example hosts, Kuwait, Saudi (King Saud), UAE application hosts, Korea university track after GKS, Japan MEXT university-track procedure, and all of South America and Mexico beyond one example.
