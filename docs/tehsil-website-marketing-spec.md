## Executive Summary

Tehsil is a paid platform that finds master's scholarships you can actually afford—verified against official sources—and applies to them for you. We serve master's applicants worldwide seeking scholarships and application support, and education agencies seeking a white-label platform to extend their capacity.

Our core differentiator is the Funding Intelligence Engine: verified funding with receipts (official sources, deadlines, last-checked dates), city-specific realistic affordability modeling, and discovery of obscure funding routes—departmental support, industrial sponsorships, research-ecosystem pathways—that listing sites miss. We do not promise admission or outcomes; we promise verification, transparency, and work saved.

## 1. Business Summary

### What We Do

Tehsil automates and verifies the master's application journey for students worldwide:

1. **Verified discovery**: Find scholarships and programs checked against official sources, with affordability modeled by city and funding gap calculated explicitly.
2. **Application strategy**: Get a recommended pack optimized for your budget, funding requirement, deadlines, and personal goals—with reasons explained for every inclusion.
3. **Documents**: Tailored CV and motivation letters, generated from your verified facts and reviewed by a counselor before use.
4. **Application execution**: Forms completed, portals navigated, documents uploaded, fees tracked, statuses monitored—by consultants initially, by agents progressively.
5. **Dashboard**: One place to see recommendations, deadlines, costs, approvals, and next actions.

### Market Position

The real competitor is not other agencies—it is a student with 40 browser tabs, a spreadsheet, and ChatGPT. That approach is free, feels fully in their control, and works for some people. We win by:

- **Stopping information rot**: Official sources change silently; we re-check deadlines, fees, and eligibility rules.
- **Proving affordability**: Listing sites say "scholarship available." We calculate what still needs to be paid and whether it's realistic for your situation.
- **Finding the hard-to-find**: Famous scholarships are findable by anyone. We surface departmental support, industry partnerships, research-linked funding, and program-specific routes.
- **Doing the work**: Completing forms, handling portals, tracking payments, and following up—work that compounds across 10–20 applications.

### Commercial Model

- **Consultation fee**: Small upfront fee. Pays for a versioned Recommendation Report you keep even if you stop there. Credited toward the platform fee if you continue.
- **Platform fee**: Covers discovery, strategy, document generation, application execution, monitoring, and dashboard access for one complete application cycle.
- **Application fees**: Separate ledger. You fund it, we track it, unspent balance returned at the end.

We serve both direct students and education agencies through one multi-tenant, white-label platform. Agencies present us under their brand and domain; we handle the work.

## 2. Value Proposition

### Core Promise

Find scholarships you can actually afford—verified from official sources—and apply to them for you, so you stop missing deadlines and funding opportunities.

### Five Concrete Benefits

| Benefit | What It Solves |
|---------|---|
| **Verified funding, with receipts** | Official source, governing rule, last-checked date, and eligibility check on every scholarship claim. No guessing, no stale information. |
| **Total affordability modeling** | City-specific realistic living costs, itemized by category, minus verified funding, leaving the uncovered gap you actually need to pay. |
| **Funding we find that listings miss** | Departmental support, industry partnerships, lab-linked routes, and research-ecosystem pathways that aggregators don't index. Typically 20%+ more verified opportunities than an aggregator baseline. |
| **Application work done for you** | Forms, portals, document uploads, fee tracking, and deadline monitoring handled by consultants (now) and agents (progressively). One less thing to procrastinate on. |
| **One dashboard, full visibility** | Recommendations, deadlines, costs, document approvals, application status, and receipts in one place. No more spreadsheet shuffling. |

### Trust Anchors

We operate in a category students associate with scams. Our trust strategy is restraint:

- **No outcome promises**: We never guarantee admission or scholarships. That claim is legally false and we refuse it.
- **Verification-first**: Every material claim has an official source, a check date, and a reasoning trail. You can verify or refute every recommendation.
- **Transparent money**: Platform fee is separate from application fees. Your application budget sits in a per-student ledger, itemized with receipts, and unspent balance is returned.
- **You approve everything**: You approve the application pack before we submit. Counselors approve every CV and motivation letter before it goes anywhere. You do identity verification, signatures, and any personal MFA yourself.
- **Full audit trail**: Every action—automated or human—is logged and exportable. You own the record.

## 3. Target Audience

### Primary: Master's Applicants (Direct-to-Consumer)

**Who**: Ages 18–30, seeking master's programs worldwide, typically applying to 8–15 programs simultaneously.

**Motivation**: 
- Fear of missing scholarships or deadlines
- Uncertainty about affordability and whether programs are actually affordable for them
- Time-consuming manual work across 10+ applications
- Doubt about application quality across that volume
- Anxiety about scams disguised as agencies (primed against deceptive claims)

**Psychographics**:
- Financially aware (they're comparing total cost, not just scholarship amount)
- Diligent (they'd do this themselves if they could, but it's too much)
- Evidence-driven (show sources, explain reasoning, let them verify)
- Skeptical of agencies (default assumption is that agents overpromise)
- Mobile-first (they research and apply on phones)

**Key insight**: They don't need a cheerleader; they need a *proof*. Testimonials and logos don't convert this audience. Showing the instrument working—a real evidenced program card with actual numbers—does.

### Secondary: Education Agencies (B2B)

**Who**: Established consultancies, immigration agencies, and education brokers serving master's applicants.

**Motivation**:
- Counselor capacity is the bottleneck; research work scales poorly
- Competing agencies offer similar outcomes
- Risk: a student discovers funding the agency missed

**Buy**:
- Research automation (affordability modeling + lead discovery)
- White-label platform under their brand/domain
- Audit trails and data isolation (legal requirement for student data handling)
- CTA: Book a demo, not a consultation

**Key insight**: Sell counselors, not just owners. If the tool slows their workflow down, adoption fails. Emphasize assigned queues, pre-researched leads with evidence, and side-by-side document review.

### Tertiary: Parents (Decision Influence)

**Who**: Funding the master's, often in a different country, more skeptical than the applicant.

**Motivation**: 
- "Is this actually legitimate?" (scam risk)
- "Can my child really afford this?" (affordability proof)
- "Will they actually help?" (delivery proof)

**Key insight**: This is unvalidated; the website proposal flags it as an open question. If parents carry decision weight, a separate evidence section or trust-building pathway may be needed. Validate in pilot phase.

## 4. Key Features

### Phase 1 (Launch MVP)

These features are live and verified:

1. **Funding Intelligence Engine**
   - Hybrid discovery across government, Erasmus Mundus, university, departmental, industrial, and research-ecosystem sources
   - Eligibility screening against hard gates (teaching language, deadline, confirmed ineligibility)
   - Evidence-backed claims with official source, last-checked date, and confidence level
   - City-specific affordability modeling with itemized costs, benefit deduction, and uncovered gap

2. **Recommendation Pack**
   - Personalized program recommendations optimized for your budget, funding dependency, and personal goals
   - Balanced-fit vs. funding-first ranking modes (choose which matters more)
   - Proof for each recommendation: academic fit, funding coverage, affordability, deadline, risks
   - Ability to reject, replace, or request programs
   - Downloadable, timestamped Consultation Recommendation Report

3. **Application Strategy & Documents**
   - Tailored CV and motivation letters generated from your verified facts
   - Side-by-side counselor review with source highlights, fact-checks, and flagged claims
   - Revision rounds (two style revisions, unlimited fact corrections)
   - Approval requirement before use
   - Version history and audit trail

4. **Application Management**
   - Per-program tracking: status, deadline, documents needed, fees, next action
   - Platform-managed email for applications and communication
   - Document upload once; agent posts it to the relevant portals
   - Consultant or agent execution of forms and portals
   - Application fee ledger: itemized, receipts, unspent balance returned

5. **Dashboard & Visibility**
   - One-page profile of goals, constraints, and preferences
   - Recommendation pool and highlighted pack
   - Per-program roadmap and status
   - Deadline calendar
   - Cost summary and budget tracking
   - Decision history and readable activity log
   - Agency-branded, exportable application dossier

6. **White-Label Agency Platform**
   - Tenant isolation: strict data boundaries between agencies
   - Branded domain, logo, colors, support identity, legal links
   - Separate roles: platform admin, agency owner, counselor, student
   - Agency-owner view: assigned students, approvals, audit exports
   - Counselor view: student queue, recommendations (read-only), document approvals, application stages
   - Audit trail and export controls

### Future Phases (Not Launch)

- **Phase 2**: Standalone document services (CV Polish, motivation letters for any program)
- **Phase 3**: Autonomous application execution (agent-submitted forms, escalation on CAPTCHA/identity verification)
- **Phase 4**: Live mock interviews and interview guidance
- **Phase 5**: Automated lead discovery (replacing manual consultant research)

## 5. Messaging Pillars (Core Messages)

### Pillar 1: Verified Funding with Receipts
**"Official source, last-checked date, eligibility verdict, on every scholarship claim."**

Every funding opportunity shows exactly where the information comes from, when we last verified it, and whether you qualify. No vague "opportunity available" claims. No stale information. This is the single strongest differentiator—no aggregator, no chatbot, and no agency brochure shows this level of proof.

**Evidence**: The evidence card showing real program data, real sources, real last-checked dates.

---

### Pillar 2: Affordability You Can Actually Calculate
**"Realistic living costs, itemized. Minus verified funding. Leaving the gap you actually need to pay."**

Scholarship listings tell you the award amount. They don't tell you whether it covers tuition, living costs, and travel—or what remains your responsibility. We model affordability by city with itemized costs (housing, food, transport, etc.), apply verified funding, and show the uncovered gap. That's the number that decides whether you can attend.

**Evidence**: A worked example showing tuition + fees + city living costs - verified funding = actual affordability.

---

### Pillar 3: Funding Others Miss
**"Departmental support, industrial partnerships, lab-linked routes—the scholarships listing sites don't index."**

The famous Erasmus Mundus and government scholarships are findable by anyone. We surface the obscure routes: departmental support within university budgets, industry partnerships with master's programs, research-group stipends, and lab-linked pathways. Typically 20%+ more verified opportunities than an aggregator baseline.

**Evidence**: One worked example of an obscure route with its evidence and source. Honest about the evidence strength if it's an ecosystem clue.

---

### Pillar 4: Application Work Done for You
**"Documents, forms, portals, payments, monitoring. So you don't miss deadlines or funding."**

Applying to 10+ programs is grunt work: form filling across different portals, document tailoring, payment tracking, following up on missing pieces, and monitoring for new requirements. We do that work, with counselor oversight and your approval at every step.

**Evidence**: The roadmap showing stages (consultation, pack approval, documents, applications, monitoring, outcomes), who does each step, and what you do yourself.

## 7. Visual Direction & Tone

### Visual Approach

**Principle**: The instrument is the credibility. Design serves proof, never decoration.

**Hero Visual**:  
- A real evidenced program card (the core product output), not an abstract gradient or stock photo.
- Legible at mobile width. Real data, real source URL, real numbers.
- Establishes immediately: *this is what you get*, not *this is a brand*.

**Evidence Card**:  
- Central proof on the homepage. Shows one full program: institution, funding, costs, living-cost breakdown, affordability gap, source, check date, eligibility verdict.
- Static or light interactive (field/country picker is the high-value upgrade, not required at launch).
- Real data only. Never placeholder shapes. Never "data-viz interpretations." If data is shown, it's real.

**Overall Aesthetic**:  
- Clean, evidence-forward, sober. No ambient motion, no decorative loops. No particle fields, no floating geometry.
- Money and deadlines must be legible—no animation obscuring numbers.
- Information-hierarchy-first. Reduced-motion mode works perfectly.

**Color & Typography**:  
- Tenant `brand_primary_color` as the only variable color. Derived `--brand-tint` for secondary contrast (per `apps/tenancy/services.py`).
- Typography hierarchy clear. No large display type that pushes content below the fold.
- High contrast on evidence (AA WCAG minimum).

### Tone & Voice

**Register**: Honest, precise, evidence-driven. Not cheerleading or hyperbolic.

**Principles**:
1. **Specificity over reassurance**: Don't say "your data is safe." Say what exactly you control: "You approve the pack before submission. Counselors approve every document. You do identity verification yourself. Everything is logged and exportable."
2. **Concede, then differentiate**: "You can do this yourself. Plenty do. Here's why we're worth the fee." (Concession buys credibility.)
3. **Name hard truths plainly**: No outcome guarantees. Scam risk is real. Parents worry about affordability. Say it, then prove we're different.
4. **Show, don't tell**: Don't say "verified funding." Show a program card with official source and check date.

**Language Ban** (from product source):
- ❌ "Guarantee," "guaranteed admission," "we'll get you in" (legally false)
- ❌ "Dream school," "unlock your future," "life-changing" (agency-scam register)
- ❌ "Effortless," "instant," "zero work" (untrue, reads as scam)
- ❌ "Revolutionary," "AI-powered," "cutting-edge" (assert what the interface should show)
- ❌ "Hack the system," "insider trick" (implies manipulating admissions, which we refuse)
- ❌ Jargon on student-facing pages: "tenant," "pack version," "hard gate," "Funding Intelligence Engine" (internal only)

**Tone Examples**:
- ✅ "We find scholarships others miss—departmental, industry, research-linked. With proof."
- ✅ "No admission guarantee. Ever. We don't work that way."
- ✅ "Funding covers tuition. But living costs? That's separate, and we calculate it."
- ✅ "You approve the pack, the documents, and everything before we submit. That's not a feature—that's the point."

## 8. Information Architecture

### Pages (Minimal, Content-Driven)

```
/                       Home — the primary pitch and proof
├── /how-it-works       Process deep-dive (optional at launch; can be a homepage section)
├── /pricing            Fee structure explained (not a homepage band)
├── /agencies           B2B white-label pitch and demo booking
├── /start              Consultation entry (conversion destination)
├── /faq                Objection long-tail
├── /about              Founder/team legitimacy
└── Legal
    ├── /privacy        Privacy policy
    └── /terms          Terms of service + outcome disclaimer
```

**Page Priority**:
- High: Home, How it works, Pricing, Start, Agencies
- Medium: FAQ, About
- Low: Privacy, Terms

**Why each page**:
- **Home**: Student-facing primary argument. 100% conversion focus.
- **How it works**: Remove process uncertainty. Link from home.
- **Pricing**: Fee structure is genuinely unusual. Compressing into a band reads as obfuscation.
- **Agencies**: B2B route. Quiet on home; dedicated page and demo CTA.
- **Start**: Consultation signup. Handles payment, profile intake, and expectation-setting.
- **FAQ**: Objection long-tail. Low priority; build if traffic data shows need.
- **About**: In this category (scam-adjacent), "who are these people" is a live objection.
- **Legal**: Necessary. Low friction. Required disclaimer about outcomes.

### Navigation

**Header**: `Logo · How it works · Pricing · For agencies · [Start — €X]`

**Footer**: Product (How it works, Pricing, For agencies) · Company (About, Contact) · Legal (Privacy, Terms) · Outcome disclaimer as a principle, not small print.

## 9. Content Strategy

### Homepage Sections (Narrative Order)

1. **Hero**: State the promise. Show the instrument. Defuse scam suspicion.
2. **The Problem**: Why this is hard. The information environment, not the person.
3. **Evidence Card** ★: One real program, fully evidenced. This is the proof.
4. **What You Control**: Answer the anxiety forces (upfront payment, handing over documents, agents submitting wrong, can't undo it).
5. **Funding Others Miss**: One worked example of an obscure funding route.
6. **How It Works**: Five steps. Who does each. What you still do.
7. **What It Costs**: Fee structure clear. Anchored against cost of one missed opportunity.
8. **Objections**: Top five answered plainly (DIY, fake funding claims, scam risk, AI-written CV, if you don't get in).
9. **Final CTA**: Restate promise. Same CTA, same price.

(Optional: Agencies band after primary CTA—quiet, subordinate, never competing with student conversion.)

### Key Content Pieces

| Content | Purpose | Location | Effort |
|---------|---------|----------|--------|
| Evidence card | Proof | Home §3 | Medium (pre-build ~20 field×country cards, or 1 template + live research) |
| Funded example | Differentiation | Home §5 | Low (one worked example with real source) |
| Process diagram | Clarity | Home §6 + `/how-it-works` | Low (5-step flowchart) |
| Pricing explainer | Transparency | `/pricing` + Home §7 | Low (structured breakdown: consultation, platform fee, application fees, ledger, refunds) |

## 10. Launch Blockers (Non-Blocking for Spec)

These are marketing questions that do not block this spec but must be validated before launch:

1. **Consultation fee amount**: Not set. Anchor against cost of one wasted application fee, but the actual figure must be decided.
2. **Evidence card data**: Which program(s) and funding routes will be shown as proof? Must be real, verified, current.
3. **Founder/team identity**: Who is "Orkhan" and who else? Names, photos, brief bios needed for `/about`.
4. **Parent decision-weight**: Do parents carry significant decision weight? (Flagged as unvalidated in proposal.) May require secondary argument thread.
5. **Live vs. static evidence cards**: High-leverage conversion upgrade to show personalized cards based on student input. Cost vs. value trade-off to be decided.
6. **Payment processor & legal**: Consultation and platform fees require payment processing, terms, privacy agreements, and jurisdiction-specific consumer law.

These metrics track whether the website is selling, not just educating:

| Metric | Target | Why |
|--------|--------|-----|

| **Agency demo booking** | Track separately from student conversion | Different funnel, different KPIs. |

## 12. Design Guardrails (Anti-Drift Rules)

These are pass/fail checks, enforced before ship and again at QA.

### The Central Rule
**Every visual element must name the conversion job it performs.** Illustrate the promise, earn trust, differentiate, or reduce anxiety. A device with no job is cut regardless of craft.

**The one-line test at QA**:  
*"Does this page sell the service, or showcase the developer?"*

### Hard Rules

| # | Rule | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hero visual is the product**, not decoration (no gradients, particle fields, floating 3D, stock photos) | Decoration says "we have nothing to show." |
| 2 | **Evidence card has real data, real numbers, real source URL**, never placeholder shapes | It is the only proof. Style it away and nothing remains. |
| 3 | **Legibility beats effect on money and deadlines** | These numbers are the product. |
| 4 | **No scroll-jacking, no pinned full-screen chapters, no horizontal scroll** | Nine sections + scrolling = visitor leaves before the price. |
| 5 | **Motion only reveals or directs attention**; no loops, no decoration, no motion on money figures | Ambient motion signals portfolio, not instrument. |
| 6 | **Page fully works with reduced motion and no JS-driven reveals** | If the argument only works as scroll experience, it isn't an argument. |
| 7 | **Price in the CTA button** | Hiding it is a trust violation. |
| 8 | **No testimonial-shaped placeholders** | Empty slots advertise the absence. Design for zero social proof as permanent state. |
| 9 | **Evidence card legible at 375px, no horizontal scroll** | 18–25 audience is mobile-first. |
| 10 | **No portfolio tells** (custom cursors, letter-by-letter animation, page-load animation >400ms, huge display type) | Each signals "look at the site" instead of "read the argument." |

### Grayscale Test

Screenshot the finished page. Strip all color and imagery. Leave the text.  
**If the argument still converts, the design is serving it.**  
**If it collapses, the design was carrying the page.**

## 13. Competitive Differentiation

### vs. Aggregators (ScholarshipsAI, MastersPortal, etc.)
- ❌ Aggregators index listings. We verify official sources.
- ❌ Aggregators show what exists. We calculate whether you can afford it.
- ❌ Aggregators suggest applications. We do the work.

### vs. Education Agencies
- ❌ Agencies promise admission. We don't.
- ❌ Agencies offer vague support. We show exactly what you control and approve.
- ❌ Agencies are expensive and scam-adjacent. We're transparent about money and verification.

### vs. DIY (Spreadsheet + ChatGPT)
- ✅ You save time on 10+ applications (hours saved, not days).
- ✅ We catch deadlines and funding changes you miss.
- ✅ We find departmental and research-linked funding a spreadsheet search won't.
- ✅ Professional documents and forms reduce rejections from poor execution.

### vs. Pure AI Agents (GPT, Claude, etc.)
- ✅ We verify claims; AI makes up percentages and odds.
- ✅ We model real affordability; AI hallucinates benefits.
- ✅ We know portal mechanics and handle CAPTCHA; AI cannot.
- ✅ Counselors review documents; AI alone shouldn't.

## 14. Rollout & Validation

### Pre-Launch Validation

**Pilot Phase (Before Public Launch)**:
1. Five to ten real students use the platform end-to-end.
2. Founder reviews pack quality and application outcomes (not as a gate, as input).
3. Measure: recommendation precision >90%, recall >80%, official-source support 100%.
4. Validate: three different student profiles, two different language preferences, one funding-dependent applicant.

**Website Validation (Before Public Site)**:
1. Art direction: three divergent design candidates, judged against §12 (guardrails) and §7 (tone).
2. Copy testing: top five objections validated against pilot student feedback.
3. Evidence cards: real programs pre-built or research cost modeled.
4. Founder review: outcomes-disclaimer tone, pricing clarity, process transparency.

### Post-Launch Monitoring

- **Weekly**: Consultation conversion rate, homepage scrolling behavior, pricing page abandonment.
- **Monthly**: CRO audit. Section-by-section scroll depth, CTA clicks, form completion.
- **Quarterly**: Pilot student feedback on messaging accuracy, landing page trust signals, objection handling.
