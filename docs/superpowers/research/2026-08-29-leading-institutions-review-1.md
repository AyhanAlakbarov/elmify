# Funded International Master's Places Outside the High-Income Core: Türkiye, Azerbaijan, the Turkic World, and Phase-2 Asia

## TL;DR
- **Yes, funded international master's places are abundant across all Tier 1–3 regions, and the single most valuable pattern — AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION funding — is real at Sabancı University (Türkiye), Nazarbayev University (Kazakhstan), Khazar University (Azerbaijan), Bilkent University (Türkiye), and ADA University (Azerbaijan):** an admitted international applicant is considered for a tuition waiver (often plus stipend and housing) with no separate scholarship competition, and all teach in English. The priority nationalities (Azerbaijani, Turkish, Indian, Pakistani, Vietnamese, Indonesian, Georgian, Armenian, Kazakh, Uzbek) qualify almost everywhere, subject to the "not a citizen of the host country" rule.
- **The biggest structural finding for the requester's discovery system: funding splits roughly 45% funding-shaped URLs / 55% programme-, admissions-, PDF- or dynamic-search-embedded — and several of the most valuable automatic awards (Sabancı, ADA's graduate assistantship, IIT Delhi's IMSP) have their operative "no separate application" sentence sitting inside an admissions/programme page or a brochure, not on a /scholarships/ path.** A path-substring triage will miss a material fraction of the best awards.
- **A genuinely enumerable list exists in exactly one place: India's Study in India portal publishes a browsable table of named institutions with per-institute fee-waiver counts.** No equivalent register enumerates funded institutions for Türkiye, Azerbaijan, or Central Asia — those publish either a dynamic search tool (Türkiye), a single-grant programme list (Azerbaijan), or an exchange-oriented member list (TURKUNIB). This confirms the expected "nothing systematic across the region" answer, with India the lone exception.

## Key Findings

**1. Full state scholarships cover the priority nationalities everywhere.** Türkiye Scholarships, the Heydar Aliyev grant (Azerbaijan), ICCR (India), KNB (Indonesia), and the Vietnamese (VIED) and Uzbek state schemes are all open to the priority nationalities and are genuinely fully funded (tuition + stipend + often flights/accommodation). These are NOMINATED or DIRECT central-government schemes, not institution-level.

**2. The highest-value category — AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION full or near-full funding — is concentrated in private/autonomous English-medium universities.** Sabancı, Bilkent, Nazarbayev, Khazar, and ADA all consider every admitted international applicant for funding automatically. Sabancı, Nazarbayev, Bilkent, and ADA add living support (stipend and/or accommodation); Khazar's automatic award is tuition-only.

**3. Several systems are affordable even unfunded.** METU (Türkiye) charges international master's students only a few hundred USD per year because it is a public university; Uzbek and Armenian public universities and the Indian IITs (M.Tech ~INR 5,06,150 total) are low-cost. A tuition-only waiver plus low living costs can constitute a genuine floor in these systems, unlike in the high-income core.

**4. Teaching language is the decisive filter.** English-taught: Sabancı, Bilkent, METU, Koç, Nazarbayev, Khazar, ADA, IIT Delhi, IIT Madras, ICCR/Study-in-India programmes. Mixed/other: Türkiye Scholarships places many students into Turkish-taught programmes (with a mandatory year of Turkish even for English-track placees); KNB and Darmasiswa (Indonesia), VIED (Vietnam), and much Uzbek/Armenian public provision are wholly or partly in the national language.

## Details

Legend for Application route: DIRECT = apply directly to a named scheme; NOMINATED = requires government/embassy nomination; AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION = considered automatically once admitted, no separate scholarship application.

- Scheme: Sabancı University Graduate Scholarships (full/partial tuition waiver + stipend + accommodation)
- Route: **AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION**. Operative (official iro.sabanciuniv.edu): "All international applicants who meet the application requirements are automatically considered for a scholarship. - Teaching language: **English.**
- Tuition if unfunded: Private-university fees (high relative to public Turkish universities); confirm current figure.
- URL: sabanciuniv.edu; funding on iro.sabanciuniv.edu/en/scholarships (funding-shaped) AND evaluation described on graduate-admission pages.

**5. Türkiye Scholarships (national scheme, placing into ~100+ Turkish universities)**

### PART B — The structural question (does funding live on funding-shaped URLs?)

Observed pattern across the ~20 institutions/schemes above:

- **Funding-shaped URL** (/scholarships/, /funding/, /financial-aid/, /admission/financial-aid): Bilkent (/bilkent/scholarships/), Koç (international.ku.edu.tr/scholarships/), Nazarbayev (nu.edu.kz/admissions/scholarships), ADA (ada.edu.az/en/admission/financial-aid), IIT Madras (/academics/academic-services/scholarships-and-financial-assistance), Khazar (international.khazar.org/international-scholarship-program/), Study in India (/scholarships/FeeWaiversandConcessions), Sabancı (iro.sabanciuniv.edu/en/scholarships). **≈ 8.**
- **Inside programme/admissions pages, PDFs, dynamic search, or scheme subdomains a /scholarships/ filter would miss:** ADA graduate (the operative "no separate application" sentence lives inside /admission/graduate), METU (aid on programme/admissions and oidb fee pages), IIT Delhi (intladm.iitd.ac.in + PG information brochure PDF), TDTU (programme scholarship pages), KNB participating universities (oia.ugm.ac.id — mixed), Türkiye Scholarships (dynamic search engine, not a static funding page), Heydar Aliyev/SIACAS (/financial-support and /universities — partly funding-shaped, partly not), Uzbek/Armenian public schemes (fragmented). **≈ 9–10.**

**Ratio: roughly 45% funding-shaped / 55% programme-, admissions-, PDF-, or dynamic-search-embedded.** The critical qualitative finding: several of the **most valuable AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION awards** have their operative sentence embedded outside a /scholarships/ path — ADA's graduate assistantship (in /admission/graduate), IIT Delhi's IMSP (in the PG brochure PDF), and, for Türkiye Scholarships, the entire participating-university list is reachable only through a **dynamic search tool populated during the application window**, invisible to any static path crawl. A path-substring triage would therefore miss a material share of the best opportunities. **Discovery must additionally crawl /admissions/ and /international/ paths, index PDF brochures, parse programme-page bodies, and handle dynamic search endpoints** — and should key on the operative phrases "automatically considered," "no separate application," and "considered by default," which reliably flag the highest-value awards regardless of URL path.

### PART C — Is there any list?

**Derivation of who would publish such a list, and what each actually publishes:**

- **A government marketing its own institutions abroad:** India's **Study in India portal** is the ONE body that publishes a genuine enumerable, browsable table of named institutions with per-institute fee-waiver counts (/scholarships/FeeWaiversandConcessions) — the single strongest "list" finding. **Türkiye Scholarships** publishes participating universities/programmes only via a **dynamic search engine and the application system** — not a static list. **Study in Azerbaijan / SIACAS** publishes a universities page plus a separate official document of **435 eligible programmes** under the Heydar Aliyev grant — but this enumerates programmes under one government grant, not "all institutions with funded places."
- **A regional bloc promoting mobility:** The **Organization of Turkic States**, via **TURKUNIB (Turkic Universities Union)**, publishes a **member-university list (~170 universities)** at turkunib.org/en/members and runs the **Orhun** exchange — but Orhun funds only 1–2 semester tuition-free **exchange**, NOT full master's degrees. The list exists but does not map to "funded master's places." **ASEAN** publishes no funded-master's institution register in scope.
- **A development agency funding education:** **ICCR** (India) publishes scheme and eligible-country information but not an "all funded institutions" register; **KNB** (Indonesia) lists participating universities on the KNB portal; **GIZ/JICA/KOICA/TIKA** fund programmes/individuals, not a regional institution register. The **Islamic Development Bank** runs joint scholarship programmes (e.g., IsDB–Türkiye) but not an enumerable institution list in scope.
- **A university network/consortium:** TURKUNIB (above) lists members but not funded-degree places.
- **Accreditation/quality-assurance bodies:** These publish accreditation registers (see Part D), not funding lists.
- **Erasmus+ / Erasmus Mundus:** Publishes **region lists** (Azerbaijan = Neighbourhood East; Kazakhstan/Uzbekistan = Central Asia; India = Asia; Türkiye = an associated Programme country), not a global host-institution list; ICM funds short mobility, full degrees via Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters.

**Verdict: No single body enumerates "institutions offering funded international master's places" across these regions. The closest to a true list is India's Study in India fee-waiver table. Everything else is a dynamic search tool (Türkiye), a single-grant programme list (Azerbaijan), an exchange-oriented member list (TURKUNIB), or a country/region eligibility list (Erasmus+, ICCR).** The expected "nothing systematic exists" answer is confirmed for the region as a whole, with India the lone structured exception.

### PART D — Which are actually worth attending (verifiably published standing)

- **METU (Türkiye):** English official language; ABET-accredited engineering programmes; member of the European University Association (EUA) and CESAER. Publicly funded and low-cost. Among the strongest degrees for recognition abroad in this set.
- **Sabancı and Bilkent (Türkiye):** Well-regarded Turkish research universities with English-medium instruction, recognised nationally by Türkiye's Council of Higher Education (CoHE/YÖK); Bilkent has published bibliometric distinctions (e.g., a high open-access publication share in the CWTS Leiden ranking).
- **Nazarbayev University (Kazakhstan):** English-medium; built with international partner universities; the country's flagship autonomous research university. Standing is institutional/national; subject-level international recognition is emerging rather than long-established.
- **ADA University (Azerbaijan):** Publishes accreditations/rankings; per ADA's own citation, Times Higher Education placed it among the top 400 globally in the Quality Education metric — the highest by an Azerbaijani university in that category. Dual-degree programmes with George Washington University (US) strengthen recognition abroad.
- **Khazar University (Azerbaijan):** Long-established private university teaching in English; nationally accredited. Verify current international register memberships directly.

## Recommendations

**Stage 1 — Target the automatic, English-taught, living-cost-inclusive awards first.** For the priority nationalities, the best risk-adjusted options are **Sabancı** and **Nazarbayev** (automatic consideration, English, tuition + stipend + housing) and **Bilkent** (automatic, English, up to 100% + stipend/housing). These require only a strong admission application. **ADA's International Fellowship and Graduate Assistantship** (English, tuition + stipend + housing) are next, limited to specified programmes. Threshold to move on: if the candidate's field is outside these universities' offerings, go to Stage 2.

**Stage 2 — Use the fully funded national schemes where language fits.** **ICCR** (India, English, fully funded, PG stipend ₹20,000/month) is high value and open to all priority nationalities. **Türkiye Scholarships** is fully funded but frequently Turkish-taught with a mandatory Turkish year — apply only if the candidate accepts that cost. **KNB** (Indonesia) and **VIED** (Vietnam) are fully funded but often national-language — confirm the specific programme is English before committing.

**Stage 3 — Treat tuition-only waivers as viable only where living costs are low.** **Khazar** (tuition-only; Baku living ~$400–1,000/month) and **METU** (near-free public tuition) can be genuine floors. **Study in India** fee waivers are tuition-only — pair with ICCR or self-funded living.

**For the requester's discovery system:** Do NOT rely on path-substring triage alone. Crawl and parse **/admissions/**, **/international/**, and programme-page bodies; index **PDF brochures**; and handle **dynamic search endpoints**. Prioritise detecting the operative phrases "automatically considered," "no separate application," and "considered by default." Ingest India's Study in India fee-waiver table directly as a structured source. Benchmark that would change the approach: if a re-crawl shows >70% of the best awards are on funding-shaped URLs, revert to cheaper path triage; the observed ~45/55 split says do not.

## Caveats
- **Currency/stipend figures fluctuate** (Turkish lira especially); TL amounts (Türkiye Scholarships master's 6,500 TL, Sabancí 32,500 TL, Bilkent ~46,000 TL) are recent-cycle and should be re-verified against the current official page before use.
- **Study in India publishes internally inconsistent figures**: its FeeWaivers page cites ~9,500 waivers across 108 institutes at 100/50/20%, while its Partner-Institute page cites far larger counts and 100/50/25%; the flat cash award appears as up to USD 3,500 on one page. Cite the specific page used and treat exact numbers as provisional.
- **ADA University ROR ID could not be confirmed**; do not substitute the unrelated "Ada Kent University" (N. Cyprus) record.
- **Eligibility under the Heydar Aliyev grant** depends on OIC/NAM/ties status: Armenia's and Georgia's eligibility in particular should be verified against the current call.
- **Teaching language must be verified per programme** for Türkiye Scholarships placements, KNB, VIED, TDTU, and Uzbek/Armenian public universities — several offer both national-language and English tracks, and a funded placement may be in the national language.
- **Pakistan is a source country in this scope**, not a confirmed funded destination for foreign master's students at LUMS/NUST/IBA on the evidence reviewed.
- Standing claims in Part D rely on official/accreditation sources and institutions' own published statements; where only commercial rankings exist, standing is marked unconfirmed per the brief.
