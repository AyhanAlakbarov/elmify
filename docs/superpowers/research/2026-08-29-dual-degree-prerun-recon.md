# Pre-run reconnaissance — Azerbaijan's international dual-degree programmes

**Date:** 2026-08-29. **Status: NOT a research run, and not an input to one.**

A control, like `2026-08-29-outbound-launch-prerun-recon.md`.
`PROMPT-leading-institutions-outside-high-income.md` Part A0 asks whether these exist at master's
level and who pays what. **Do not hand this file to either run** — it exists so the adjudication can
see what each run reaches without it.

Every claim here was fetched from the issuing body and is quoted.

---

## 1. Four of the six are master's — the opposite of what was predicted

The State Agency for Science and Higher Education publishes the set (`eatda.edu.az`, page dated
28.10.24):

> "Hazırda 6 beynəlxalq ikili diplom proqramı (**2 bakalavriat, 4 magistratura** təhsil səviyyəsi
> üzrə) həyata keçirilməkdədir."
> — "Six international dual-degree programmes are currently running (**2 at bachelor's, 4 at
> master's** level)."

| Level | Home institution | Foreign partner | Fields |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Master's** | Azerbaijan State Pedagogical University (ADPU) | George Washington (US) | Education and Human Capital Development |
| **Master's** | ADA University | George Washington (US) | Computer Science & Data Analytics; Electrical & Power Engineering |
| **Master's** | Baku State University (BDU) | Hebrew University of Jerusalem (Israel) | Biotechnology |
| **Master's** | Azerbaijan State Oil and Industry University (ADNSU) | Warwick (UK) | Renewable Energy Sources |
| Bachelor's | Baku Engineering University (BMU) | INHA (Korea) | Civil, Electrical, Electronics Engineering; IT |
| Bachelor's | Azerbaijan State Agrarian University (ADAU) | Ege (Türkiye) | Agronomy, Agro-engineering, Plant Protection, Economics |

**The master's half is the larger half.** The pre-run guess — that the site's bachelor-first
navigation meant these were bachelor's-only, and that Part A0 would close cheaply — was wrong.

ADAU–Ege, for the record, is a four-year bachelor's: three years in Ganja, one in İzmir, 30 places
per speciality (`edu.gov.az/az/announcements/16694-1`).

## 2. Who pays the student — and it is not the state

`dp.edu.az/az/content/66`… the ADA–GWU page (`/az/content/55`) states the terms:

- **Master's level**, two specialities, both institutions' diplomas awarded.
- **Five semesters — three at ADA, two at George Washington.** Two semesters abroad.
- **Taught in English.**
- **Route:** selection on the results of the State Examination Centre's (DİM) master's entrance
  examinations, after which candidates who meet the passing score take part in selection by ADA and
  GWU. Entry is through the national exam, not a nomination.

And then the sentence that answers Part A0's question:

> "Proqram iştirakçılarının **bəzi xərcləri BP və onun tərəfdaşları** tərəfindən maliyyələşdiriləcəkdir."
> — "**Some** of the participants' costs will be financed by **BP and its partners**."

**The student-facing money is a corporate sponsor's, not the state's.** This is consistent with the
financing rule already established: Cabinet decision 450 divides state funding into design costs,
steering-committee costs and local-faculty costs — it pays for the *programme*. The participant's own
costs are met, in part, by BP.

**Three consequences.**

1. **It is not a funded place.** "Some costs", extent unpublished, is not an
   `AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION` full-funding floor and should not be recorded as one.
2. **It is `INDUSTRIAL_SPONSORSHIP` inside a `GOVERNMENT_SCHOLARSHIP` shell.** A scheme announced by
   the state, delivered by two universities, and paid for at the student level by an oil company. The
   third instance this week — after the GKS Overseas Korean track and Kazakhstan's nomination-level
   conditions — of one scheme carrying more than one set of rules, and the first where the *funder
   family itself* differs between the shell and the money.
3. **A funder we would never have reached from the funder side.** No corporate-sponsorship search
   finds BP through a Baku dual-degree page. This is an argument for the channel-indexed method, not
   the funder-indexed one.

## 3. Where these sit administratively

Presidential Order **No. 711 of 16 November 2018** approved the *State Programme on increasing the
international competitiveness of the higher education system in the Republic of Azerbaijan for
2019–2023*; its §3.1 provides for establishing **five** international dual-degree programmes
initially, continuing subject to monitoring. Cabinet decisions **451** (selection of foreign partner
institutions, specialities and levels) and **450** (financing) of 20 November 2019 sit under it.

**This is not the outbound State Programme**, though the same management group — DPİQ — administers
both. That shared administrator is what repeatedly merged adjacent Azerbaijani instruments in earlier
research, and it is now documented twice.

## 4. A currency trap, live on the page

The ADA–GWU page states document intake **closed 01.04.2023**, while a 2026–2027 master's intake for
the same partnership appears to be running. **The programme pages are stale even where the programme
is not.** Anything reading these pages for cycle status will report a live programme as closed — the
mirror image of the failure the "confirm the scheme is currently open" rule usually guards against.

## 5. Not established — deliberately left for the runs

1. **The other three master's programmes.** ADPU–GWU, BDU–Hebrew University and ADNSU–Warwick were
   not opened. Their level is sourced; their funding, duration, split between institutions and route
   are not.
2. **Whether BP-style sponsorship is the pattern or an ADA-specific arrangement.** One page is one
   data point. If each programme carries a different sponsor, that is a materially different finding
   from a single state-brokered corporate scheme.
3. **What "some costs" actually covers** — tuition at the partner, travel, living costs abroad, or
   none of these. Unpublished on the page.
4. **What the student pays** in home tuition for the semesters at the Azerbaijani institution.
5. **Whether equivalents exist outside Azerbaijan** — Türkiye, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, India,
   Pakistan. Nothing was attempted here, and Part A0 states that a route found elsewhere is worth more
   than these six.

## 6. What the adjudication should check

1. **Does either run establish the 2-bachelor's / 4-master's split**, or does it treat all six as one
   kind?
2. **Does either run find the BP sentence**, or report the dual degrees as state-funded because they
   sit inside a State Programme?
3. **Does either run notice the stale intake date** and check the cycle independently?
4. **Does either run keep the competitiveness programme separate from the outbound one?**
5. **Does either run find an equivalent outside Azerbaijan?** That is the finding worth more than
   everything above, and nothing in this note helps them get it.
