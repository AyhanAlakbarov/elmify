# State-Funded Outbound Master's Scholarships: Azerbaijan and Türkiye

## TL;DR
- **Both countries run live, bonded outbound master's schemes.** Azerbaijan's "State Programme for youth study at prestigious foreign universities" (2022–2028) is open — its 2025/2026 cycle ran document intake 10 April–15 June 2025 — and requires 5 years' post-graduation work in Azerbaijan. Türkiye's YLSY (now branded "1416 Bursları", under the 1929 Law No. 1416) is open — per MEB, "In 2025 our Ministry will send 811 students abroad on official scholarship status" — and carries a heavy bond: compulsory service (mecburi hizmet) of TWICE the funded period abroad, secured by joint guarantors and repayable with interest on default.
- **The two Azerbaijani programmes that prior research conflated are opposite instruments.** The outbound State Programme sends Azerbaijani citizens abroad on State Oil Fund money with a 5-year return bond; the inbound "Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant" (decree 3956, 8 July 2023) brings FOREIGN students INTO Azerbaijan on state-budget money with NO return obligation. Different direction of travel, different beneficiaries, different payer.
- **Both outbound awards are effectively bonded products, not free scholarships.** For most students the decisive fact is the service/repayment obligation. Azerbaijan's is a general 5-year in-country work requirement; Türkiye's YLSY is materially more onerous (double-period compulsory service to a named institution, with müteselsil kefil guarantors and interest on default) and should be treated as a career-length public-service commitment rather than a conventional grant.

## Key Findings

### Part A — Azerbaijan (outbound State Programme)
1. **Status: OPEN, and the successor programme has been extended to 2028.** The 2007–2015 programme (President's decree No. 2090, 16 April 2007; funded by the State Oil Fund, executed by the then Ministry of Education) has ended. Its successor — "Gənclərin xarici ölkələrin nüfuzlu ali təhsil müəssisələrində təhsil almalarına dair 2022–2026-cı illər üçün Dövlət Proqramı" (President's decree No. 3163, 28 Feb 2022) — was extended and reset by decree No. 248 of 12 August 2024. Per the Ministry of Science and Education (edu.gov.az): the intake quota was set at 500 persons, and "from 2025, no more than 25% of the quota covers the bachelor's level and no more than 20% the doctoral level" — i.e. roughly 55%+ is master's-level. The most recent evidenced cycle is **2025/2026**: document intake ran 10 April–15 June 2025, drawing 1,147 applicants, with interviews beginning late June 2025. The programme is now generally referred to as the "2022–2028" State Programme.
2. **Administering body: the Ministry of Science and Education** (Elm və Təhsil Nazirliyi), acting through its Dövlət Proqramı İdarəetmə Qrupu (State Programme Management Group), which was created under the separate 2019–2023 higher-education-competitiveness programme. **Funding source: the State Oil Fund of Azerbaijan (ARDNF/SOFAZ)**, plus permitted grants/donations. Responsibility has moved with the education portfolio: the 2007–2015 programme was run by the Ministry of Education; today's programme sits with the merged Ministry of Science and Education, with the Ministry of Economy conducting monitoring/evaluation on the education ministry's order.
3. **Destinations: a published, ranking-based list of approved universities and programmes, updated every academic year.** For 2025/2026 the list covered 15 priority fields, 221 institutions in 32 countries, and 4,405 programmes. Institutions are chosen using the Times Higher Education (THE) World University Rankings and the Academic Ranking of World Universities (ARWU/Shanghai). In addition, per edu.gov.az, candidates may compete "in any specialty offered by institutions in the top ten of the 2025 THE and QS World University Rankings", regardless of the field list.
4. **Fields: 15 priority specialisation areas, published per cycle** (aligned to the country's socio-economic development priorities under decree No. 3163).
5. **Covers** (Cabinet decision No. 218 of 1 June 2022, §2.1): tuition; visa and in-country registration; monthly living costs (per published country-by-country norms — e.g. USD 1,800/month total for the general USA rate, GBP 1,500 for London); medical insurance; international travel (one round trip per academic year, economy class, one bag); and bank/card fees. **Excluded:** family members/dependants (§2.2), MBA programmes, and language/foundation courses.
6. **Return obligation: 5 years' work in Azerbaijan after graduation, contractually bound and financially enforced.** All costs are repayable on breach (the selection Qayda notes participants can be released from the work obligation only by repaying all costs incurred).
7. **Application route: DIRECT electronic application — but the candidate must ALREADY hold admission** to a programme on the approved list. The programme explicitly does not arrange or carry out foreign admissions. Two stages: online document submission, then a mandatory interview (Azerbaijani and/or the language of study; pass mark 40/50). Winners of the "Yüksəliş" competition are exempted from the interview and GPA criterion.
8. **Numbers ARE published:** the 2024–2025 cohort was 500 scholars (125 bachelor, 375 master); 1,147 people applied for 2025/2026; 1,252 students in total have been awarded places since 2022 (across 21 countries / 151 institutions), of whom 186 had graduated.

### Part A (SEPARATE) — Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant (INBOUND, foreign students)
This is a **different programme in the opposite direction** and must not be merged with the outbound State Programme.
- **Legal basis / administrator:** President's decree No. 3956 of 8 July 2023; jointly run by the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Science and Education**. It consolidates earlier separate grants for Organisation of Islamic Cooperation (OIC) and Non-Aligned Movement (NAM) member states.
- **Who is eligible:** FOREIGN citizens — from OIC and NAM member states, Small Island Developing States, and countries with ethnic/historical/cultural ties to Azerbaijan.
- **Levels:** preparatory, bachelor (incl. general medicine), **master's** (incl. medical residency), and doctorate.
- **Covers** (official MFA/MSE call): 100% tuition plus language-preparation; one round-trip international flight per year; a monthly allowance of **800 AZN (≈USD 470) for accommodation, daily expenses and educational materials, paid 10 months each year except July and August**; a **yearly medical allowance of 200 AZN (≈USD 117)**; and visa plus residence permit.
- **Route: NOMINATION by the applicant's home-country government** (ministries/embassies), then registration through the Study in Azerbaijan Centralized Admission Service (SIACAS, portal.edu.az). Nomination does not guarantee an award.
- **Return/repayment obligation: NONE.** No primary source imposes a return-to-home, work-in-Azerbaijan, or repayment condition; the only commitment is compliance with the university agreement.
- **Numbers:** per the official call, "Annually 100 international students will benefit from the Scholarship … offered on an annual basis for a period of 5 years." The first cycle (2024–2025) selected 100 recipients from 35 countries (from applicants across 47 countries) at 10 Azerbaijani universities (mainly Baku State University, Azerbaijan Medical University, Azerbaijan State Oil and Industry University, Baku Engineering University). Most recent evidenced cycle: **2026–2027** (nomination window in spring 2026; SIACAS registration in June 2026).

**Side-by-side (the correction this run exists to make):**

| | Outbound State Programme | Inbound Heydar Aliyev Int'l Education Grant |
|---|---|---|
| Direction | Azerbaijani citizens go ABROAD | FOREIGN citizens come INTO Azerbaijan |
| Who is eligible | Azerbaijani citizens | Citizens of OIC/NAM/SIDS/culturally-linked states |
| Who pays | State Oil Fund (SOFAZ) | State budget |
| Legal basis | Decree 3163 (28 Feb 2022), extended by 248 (2024) | Decree 3956 (8 July 2023) |
| Administrator | Ministry of Science and Education | Ministry of Foreign Affairs + Ministry of Science and Education |
| Master's covered? | Yes (main level) | Yes |
| Return/repayment bond | 5 years' work in Azerbaijan; all costs repayable on breach | None |
| Route | Direct application; admission required first | Home-government nomination + SIACAS |

They differ on **every** dimension except that both bear the Heydar Aliyev association and both fund master's study. The only overlap is the name; there is no shared eligibility, funding, or obligation.

### Part B — Türkiye (outbound)
**Enumeration of Turkish state (and state-linked) bodies that fund a citizen's study abroad — derived from Turkish government sources:**
- **MEB (Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı) — YLSY / "1416 Bursları"**, under Law No. 1416 (Ecnebi Memleketlere Gönderilecek Talebe Hakkında Kanun, 1929) and the 2020 Yönetmelik: the principal outbound graduate scholarship; funds **master's AND doctorate** abroad under one instrument. **In scope.** Run by the Yükseköğretim ve Yurt Dışı Eğitim Genel Müdürlüğü (yyegm.meb.gov.tr).
- **TÜBİTAK** — **2213-A Yurt Dışı Doktora** and **2213-B Yurt Dışı Müşterek Doktora** are **doctoral-only (OUT OF SCOPE)**. TÜBİTAK's master's (2210) and doctoral (2211) education bursaries are **domestic (yurt içi)**. **Finding: TÜBİTAK operates NO programme that funds a master's degree abroad** — a useful negative result, since it is often assumed otherwise.
- **YÖK (Yükseköğretim Kurulu)** — sends university research assistants abroad for lisansüstü study (master's + doctorate) under Law No. 2547 arts. 33/35 (each sent on behalf of a named university). YÖK's own funded schemes — the "100/2000 YÖK Doktora Bursu" and the Yurtdışı Araştırma Destekleri — are doctoral/post-doctoral research and restricted to academic staff, not a general public master's route.
- **Türkiye Diyanet Vakfı (state-linked foundation)** — its "Diyanet Bursları" are principally **inbound** (bringing foreign students to Turkey) and religious-studies specific; not an outbound master's route for Turkish citizens.
- **Universities under Law 1416 / Law 2547** — the sponsoring university is named at the point of application (the "adına öğrenim görülen kurum"); this is the vehicle through which most YLSY academic-track placements run.

**YLSY specifics:**
- **Status: OPEN.** Recent cycles: 2024 (668 places; intake December 2024) and **2025 (811 places)** — per the MEB 2025 kılavuz, "For the 811 places set for 2025, applications can be made from 477 different undergraduate graduation fields. Candidates who qualify will be able to study in 45 different countries." Applications for the 2025 programme opened 19 December 2025 via the REBUS (Resmî Burslu Öğrenci Sistemi) portal. There is no evidence of a full suspension, though YÖK has been cutting domestic university quotas broadly since 2023.
- **Return obligation (the defining feature):** compulsory service equal to **twice the funded period abroad** (plus one-for-one for any funded domestic language period), served at the named sponsoring institution; **repayment with interest (tazminat, faiziyle)** on non-completion/non-return; a notarised undertaking (yüklenme senedi) and **joint-and-several guarantors (müteselsil kefil / kefalet senedi)** are required at acceptance. This is a bonded award, not an unconditional grant.
- **Published per cycle:** the başvuru/tercih kılavuzu plus tercih tabloları are published as PDFs on yyegm.meb.gov.tr, with separate tables for higher-education institutions (Table 1) and other public bodies (Table 2), plus undergraduate-field and grade-conversion tables.
- **Offer-status distinction:** candidates are selected centrally by ALES exam + oral exam and placed ("resmî burslu" status) to a named institution; they then must secure their own admission from a qualifying foreign university (institutions/rankings specified in the kılavuz). Those who already hold a place cannot change the sponsoring institution after placement.

### Part C — Comparison and Encoding
- **Azerbaijan State Programme: DIRECT** — the student applies electronically, but only after already holding admission to an approved-list university.
- **Türkiye YLSY: NOMINATED / state-selected** — ÖSYM/ALES exam + oral exam + placement to a named institution; the student subsequently obtains foreign admission.
- **Heydar Aliyev inbound grant: NOMINATED** — by the home-country government.
- **Combination with a destination scholarship (Chevening/DAAD/Fulbright/university awards):** **not clearly published** for either outbound scheme. Azerbaijan's rules reimburse tuition the student paid from the current academic year onward, implying a single-funder model, but no explicit exclusivity clause was located. YLSY's structure (full state funding against a bond) is not framed as combinable. **Confirm directly with the administering body before advising stacking.**
- **Single most decision-relevant fact a counsellor must convey:**
  - Azerbaijan — you must already hold an offer from a university on the current-year approved list, and you will owe Azerbaijan 5 years of in-country work (all costs repayable on breach).
  - Türkiye — YLSY is a bonded public-service contract: expect roughly double your funded time abroad in compulsory service to a named institution, with guarantors on the hook and repayment-with-interest if you default.
- **What is NOT published (must be asked directly):** (1) whether either award may be combined with a foreign destination scholarship; (2) YLSY's per-cycle split of master's vs doctoral places (the kılavuz lists institution-level kontenjan, not a clean master's total); (3) the precise text of Azerbaijani decree 3956 (corroborated by multiple official sources but the full gazette text was not retrieved).

## Details — verbatim operative provisions

**Azerbaijan return obligation — Qayda approved by Cabinet decision No. 218 of 1 June 2022, §1.4:**
> "Proqram iştirakçılarının təhsilinin maliyyələşdirilməsi məzun olduqdan sonra ölkəyə qayıdaraq 5 (beş) il müddətinə əmək fəaliyyəti ilə məşğul olmaq şərtilə həyata keçirilir."

Translation: "Financing of participants' education is carried out on the condition that, after graduation, they return to the country and engage in work activity for a period of 5 (five) years."

**Azerbaijan application rule — official FAQ (dp.edu.az), master's section:**
> "'2022-2026 Dövlət Proqramı' çərçivəsində xarici ölkələrin ali təhsil müəssisələrinə qəbul həyata keçirilmir. Yalnız xarici ali təhsil müəssisələrinə qəbul olunmuş və yaxud artıq orada təhsil alan tələbələr təhsil xərclərinin … qarşılanması məqsədilə … müraciət edə bilərlər."

Translation: "Admission to foreign higher education institutions is not carried out under the 2022–2026 State Programme. Only students already admitted to, or already studying at, a foreign institution may apply for their study costs to be covered."

**Türkiye YLSY return obligation — Ödemeler Tebliği, Madde 6(2):**
> "Yurt dışı öğrenimde mecburi hizmet süresi; yurt dışı aylık burs veya öğrenim ücreti veya sağlık gideri verilen ayların iki katı olarak hesaplanır."

Translation: "For overseas study, the compulsory-service period is calculated as twice the number of months for which the overseas monthly stipend, tuition or health expenses were paid."

**Türkiye YLSY undertaking (yüklenme ve kefalet senedi) — MEB template:**
> "…yurt dışında okul ödemeleri ve/veya aylık burs ödemelerimin yapıldığı öğrenim süresinin iki katı kadar mecburî hizmet yapmak üzere görev almayı … şimdiden kabul ve taahhüt ederim. Bu yüklenme senedini zincirleme kefil ve ortak zincirleme borçlu sıfatıyla MEB tarafından muteber sayılacak iki şahsın kefaletine…"

Translation: "…I hereby accept and undertake to take up duty and perform compulsory service equal to twice the study period for which my overseas tuition and/or monthly stipend payments were made. This undertaking is secured by the guarantee of two persons deemed acceptable by MEB, as chain guarantor and joint chain debtor…"

**Türkiye interest on default — Law No. 1416 art. 21, as amended by Law No. 7326 (9 June 2021):**
> "Faiz borcunun hesaplanmasında, öğrencilerin taahhüdünü ihlal ettiği tarih faiz başlangıç tarihi olarak esas alınır."

Translation: "In calculating the interest debt, the date on which the student breached the undertaking is taken as the interest start date."

## Recommendations
1. **Azerbaijan applicants:** first confirm the target university/programme appears on the current-year approved list (republished by the Ministry each cycle). Without a qualifying admission letter the application is inadmissible. Plan for the 5-year in-country return bond; do not commit if you intend to work abroad after graduation.
2. **Türkiye applicants:** treat YLSY as a bonded public-service contract, not a free scholarship. A ~2-year funded master's abroad implies roughly 4 years of compulsory service to the named sponsoring institution; default triggers repayment with interest and pursues your guarantors. Take it only if committed to Turkish public-sector/academic service — for a mobile student who may not return, the bond can outweigh the funding.
3. **Foreign students eyeing Azerbaijan:** the Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant is the right (and obligation-free) inbound instrument, but it requires home-government nomination — start with your own ministry/embassy, not a direct application.
4. **Counsellors:** verify each cycle against the primary source every year — edu.gov.az / dp.edu.az for Azerbaijan; yyegm.meb.gov.tr for YLSY — because branding and quotas change (YLSY is now published as "1416 Bursları"; Azerbaijan's programme was extended from 2026 to 2028 with a reset 500-place quota).
5. **Thresholds that change the advice:** (a) a missing current-year kılavuz/elan signals a paused cycle and removes the instrument; (b) a published exclusivity rule barring combination with Chevening/DAAD/Fulbright would change stacking strategy — confirm directly; (c) any published narrowing of the approved-university list is a hard constraint on where a student may be funded.

## Caveats
- **Do NOT confuse the inbound "Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant" (decree 3956, 8 July 2023, for foreign students, no bond) with the domestic "Heydər Əliyev adına təqaüd" (decree of 24 June 2023, a one-time 3,000 AZN award to 100 Azerbaijani bachelor-level students).** Both carry the "Heydar Aliyev" name and both were created in 2023; they are unrelated to each other and to the outbound State Programme.
- The date of the 2026–2027 Heydar Aliyev nomination window differs between official-adjacent sources (one gives 16 Feb–15 April 2026; another 15 March–15 May 2026); treat the exact dates as spring 2026 and confirm on studyinazerbaijan.edu.az.
- Whether either outbound award can be held alongside a destination scholarship is **not clearly published**; establish directly with the administering body.
- Master's-specific place counts for YLSY require the current-year kılavuz; the headline figures (811 in 2025, 668 in 2024) combine master's and doctorate.
- The full gazette text of Azerbaijani decree No. 3956 was not directly retrieved; its existence and terms are corroborated by multiple first-party government sources (edu.gov.az, the MFA/MSE call document, and the State Agency for Science and Higher Education).
