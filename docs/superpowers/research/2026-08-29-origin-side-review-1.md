# Government-Published Channels on Funding a Master's Abroad — Azerbaijan, Türkiye, and Seven Post-Soviet States

## TL;DR
- **In every country studied except Turkmenistan, the government runs at least one web channel that publishes foreign-funded master's scholarships — but the operative fact for a counsellor is that in Azerbaijan, Türkiye, Armenia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan and Tajikistan the origin state *nominates or pre-selects* citizens for most foreign-state schemes.** A "safety" listed on the funder's own page (e.g. Stipendium Hungaricum, a China Government Scholarship) is, for these nationalities, a NOMINATED route that can only be entered through the ministry — so any portfolio treating it as directly-enterable on the strength of the funder's page is wrong.
- **The strongest, most fetchable state surfaces are Azerbaijan's `htp.edu.az` / `eatda.edu.az` / `edu.gov.az` (dated, deeply archived), Georgia's `iec.gov.ge`, Armenia's `escs.am/am/category/interprograms`, and Kyrgyzstan's `student.edu.gov.kg` (a genuine flat application portal).** Two surfaces are hostile to automation — Türkiye's `abdigm.meb.gov.tr` (robots-blocked) and Tajikistan's channel, which is effectively **Facebook** and defeats every web-fetching design.
- **Turkmenistan is confirmed near-silence:** its ministry sites (`education.gov.tm`, `bilim.gov.tm`) publish diploma-recognition rules and news but no outbound scholarship-competition announcements; there is no evidenced live state channel advertising foreign master's funding to citizens.

## Key Findings
- **Origin-state role converts DIRECT to NOMINATED.** Azerbaijan's Stipendium Hungaricum channel states only students approved by the Ministry as the "Authorised Sending Party" may participate; Türkiye's MEB explicitly selects candidates by interview and forwards principal/reserve name lists to the funder. These sentences (quoted in Part B) govern over the funder's "open to all" framing.
- **A sending partner publishes the per-country quota the funder hides.** Azerbaijan publishes "up to 200 per year" for Stipendium Hungaricum; Türkiye's MEB historically published Hungary's Türkiye sub-quota (150 for 2021, split by level). Hungary itself publishes no per-country number. WHO publishes the quota is itself the finding, and it generalises across Hungary, China and Kuwait.
- **Live outbound state programmes exist in Georgia (IEC), Kazakhstan (Bolashak), Uzbekistan (El-Yurt Umidi), Tajikistan (Durakhshandagon) and Kyrgyzstan (the China-quota portal).** These belong partly to the separate outbound prompt but are named here because they *are* the origin-state's primary announcement surface and co-host the foreign/bilateral schemes.
- **Bilateral quotas are frequently invisible from the destination side.** Azerbaijan's intergovernmental programme (HTP) publishes a per-country realised-intake table for 2024–2025 that no destination country publishes.
- **Messaging-platform dependence in Tajikistan.** The Center for International Programs posts admissions "daily via this body's Facebook page," which defeats website-fetching designs.

## Part B — Schemes Found (one row per scheme)

**1. Stipendium Hungaricum (Azerbaijan channel) / «Stipendium Hungaricum» təqaüd proqramı**
- **Announcing body:** EATDA (via htp.edu.az / edu.gov.az). **Funder:** Hungary (Ministry of Foreign Affairs & Trade / Tempus Public Foundation).
- **Origin-state role — NOMINATES / SELECTS.** *"Qeyd edək ki, proqramda yalnız Səlahiyyətli Göndərən Tərəf qismində Azərbaycan Respublikasının Təhsil Nazirliyi tərəfindən ərizələri təsdiq olunmuş tələbələr və ya ərizəçilər iştirak edə bilərlər."* — "Only students or applicants whose applications have been approved by the Ministry of Education of the Republic of Azerbaijan as the Authorised Sending Party may participate."
- **Route (Azerbaijani citizen): HYBRID** — apply on `apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu` AND simultaneously to the Ministry/portal.edu.az.
- **Eligible:** *"Azərbaycan Respublikası vətəndaşları."* **Destination:** Hungary. **Level:** Master's included (bakalavr, magistratura, doktorantura).
- **Covers:** tuition waiver, monthly stipend, dormitory or HUF 40,000/month housing allowance, medical care.
- **Quota:** confirmed from EATDA's own text on the 2024–2026 MoU — *"Proqram çərçivəsində Macarıstan tərəfindən hər il 200 nəfərədək Azərbaycan vətəndaşı üçün ... təqaüd ayrılacağı nəzərdə tutulmuşdur"* ("up to 200 Azerbaijani citizens per year"), split e.g. engineering/IT/technology 75, natural sciences/sustainability 20, agriculture/water 10, tourism 10, health 5, international relations 5.
- **Cycle:** closes mid-January (e.g. 15 Jan 2023 for 2023-24). **URLs:** `edu.gov.az/az/news-and-updates/22567-1`; `htp.edu.az/uploads/fileuploads/2024/11/…pdf`; funder `stipendiumhungaricum.hu`. **Source language:** Azerbaijani.

**4. Jean Monnet Scholarship Programme (Türkiye) / Jean Monnet Burs Programı**
- **Announcing body:** AB Başkanlığı (Directorate for EU Affairs) — found through this channel and nothing else. **Funder:** European Union (via the Central Finance and Contracts Unit).
- **Origin-state role — RUNS / administers.** *"1989 yılında Türkiye Cumhuriyeti ile Avrupa Komisyonu arasında imzalanan bir anlaşma çerçevesinde Jean Monnet Burs Programı oluşturulmuştur."* — "created under an agreement signed in 1989 between the Republic of Türkiye and the European Commission."
- **Route: DIRECT** (open, competitive). *"AB müktesebatı konusunda uzmanlaşmak isteyen herkese açık"* — "open to everyone wishing to specialise in the EU acquis" (public/private-sector staff and university final-year/graduate students).
- **Destination:** EU member states. **Level:** Master's (yüksek lisans) or doctoral. **Covers:** tuition and living costs, paid in tranches (not monthly); no family allowance.
- **Quota:** confirmed from AB Başkanlığı (announcement dated 4 Sept 2025) — *"2026-2027 akademik yılında yaklaşık 150 kişiye burs verilmesi planlanmaktadır"* (~150 for 2026-27; was ~190 for 2024-25 and 2025-26). **Cycle:** opens early September, closes early November (2026-27: opened 4 Sept 2025, closed 4 Nov 2025). **URLs:** `ab.gov.tr/jean-monnet-burs-programi_45645.html`; `jeanmonnet.org.tr`. **Source language:** Turkish + English.

## Part D — The Sending-Partner Asymmetry

The generalisation holds strongly: **the sending state publishes numbers and per-country quotas the funder does not.**
- **Hungary / Stipendium Hungaricum:** Hungary publishes no per-country quota. Azerbaijan publishes "up to 200/year" with a field breakdown; Türkiye's MEB historically published Hungary's Türkiye sub-quota (150 for 2021, split by level). Both are sending-partner disclosures absent from the funder side.
- **China (CSC):** China's per-country allocation to Kyrgyzstan (15–17) and to Azerbaijan (28 in 2024-25) is visible only from the origin-state channel.
- **Azerbaijan HTP report** (`edu.gov.az/az/qisa-xulase/22236-1`) is the strongest single example — a per-destination realised-intake table no destination country publishes.
- **Kuwait:** confirmed verbatim from the Azerbaijan Ministry report — *"Küveyt dövlətinin Azərbaycan Respublikası vətəndaşları üçün ayırdığı kvota hesabına 7 nəfər Küveyt Universitetində təqaüdlə təlim proqramında təhsil almaq hüququ qazanıb"* (7 citizens at Kuwait University on Kuwait's quota for Azerbaijani citizens, 2024-25) — a quota invisible from Kuwait's side.

## Part E — Confirmed Silence

- **Turkmenistan: confirmed near-silence.** `education.gov.tm` and `bilim.gov.tm` (Ministry of Education) publish diploma-recognition procedures, the ministry's remit and general news — but no outbound scholarship-competition announcements advertising foreign master's funding to citizens. The `bilim.gov.tm` homepage as captured shows only diploma-recognition document lists and news; `iicmet.gov.tm` (Innovation Information Center) advertises language and mental-arithmetic courses, not scholarships. No evidenced live state channel publishes foreign master's funding opportunities. An evidenced "nothing is published" is the finding — not padded.
- **Tajikistan Ministry (`maorif.tj`): channel effectively dead for this purpose.** The site carries graduation news and civil-service vacancy competitions, not scholarship announcements; scholarship traffic has migrated to the Center for International Programs' Facebook page. `edu-maorif.tj` shows posts dating to 2023.
- **Armenia is NOT silent.** The `escs.am` international-programmes category is live and current (Feb 2026 posts). ## Recommendations

1. **Treat as NOMINATED-only for these nationalities** (do not list as direct safeties): Stipendium Hungaricum for Azerbaijan and Türkiye; all Azerbaijan HTP bilaterals; Türkiye's foreign-government bursaries; the China CSC route for Kyrgyzstan; the Armenia–Romania programme.
