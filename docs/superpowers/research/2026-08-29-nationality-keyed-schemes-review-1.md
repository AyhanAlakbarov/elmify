# Nationality-Keyed Master's Funding Schemes for the South Caucasus, Central Asia and Turkic States

## TL;DR
- The strongest DIRECT, student-initiated master's options for the priority nationalities are the **International Visegrad Fund Scholarship (Eastern Partnership strand)** — open to citizens of Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia to apply directly online — and the **Czech Government Scholarships for Developing Countries**, which name Georgia as eligible; the **Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) Scholarship** is DIRECT and full-cost and covers Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkey, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan as member countries (but not Georgia or Armenia).
- Several high-profile schemes that name our countries are NOMINATED, not direct — **Stipendium Hungaricum** requires nomination by a national Sending Partner authority ("Applications will only be considered if the applicant is nominated. No other applications will be considered."), and the **Heydar Aliyev Turkic World Scholarship** and **Orhun Exchange Program** are selected through a joint commission / home university — so they cannot serve as student-initiated safety options however generous.
- **No dedicated public index of nationality-keyed master's schemes exists**; general aggregators (Mastersportal/ScholarshipPortal, Campus Bourses, DAAD database) filter by nationality but are secondary and omit operative clauses. The single most decision-relevant structural finding is that the OTS/Turkic bloc runs **no full-degree master's scheme** — only a semester exchange (Orhun) and one university-hosted award (Heydar Aliyev).

## Key Findings

1. **A truly DIRECT + full-cost floor-maker exists for most Central Asian and Turkic nationalities: the IsDB Scholarship.** Member states include Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkey, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan. It covers full tuition, monthly stipend, medical insurance and round-trip travel, and "IsDB does not charge any application fee at any stage." It is structured as an interest-free loan with a service/community component, and it does NOT cover Georgia or Armenia (non-member, and not among the listed Muslim-community non-member countries).

2. **The Visegrad Scholarship Program (Eastern Partnership strand) is DIRECT for Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia** — the student applies through the My Visegrad online portal, no nomination required. It funds only 1–2 semesters of master's mobility ("The scholarship amounts to €3,500 per semester and is paid to each scholar's bank account," up to €7,000 for two semesters), not a full degree, so it is a mobility top-up, not a full-cost floor.

3. **Stipendium Hungaricum names all four priority countries but is NOMINATED.** Azerbaijan, Georgia, Armenia and Türkiye are all Sending Partners covering full-degree master's "in any field of interest," but nomination by the national authority is mandatory. Per-country numeric quotas are NOT published by the Hungarian funder.

4. **The Turkic bloc (OTS/TÜRKSOY/Turkic Academy) runs no full-degree master's scholarship.** The only degree-level offering is the university-hosted **Heydar Aliyev Turkic World Scholarship** at Ibn Haldun University (one master's student per member/observer state, selected by a joint commission). The **Orhun Exchange Program** is a one-semester exchange, not a degree.

5. **Diaspora/community funding is real but ancestry-keyed, not citizenship-keyed.** AGBU funds master's study for students "of Armenian descent" worldwide (excluding citizens of Armenia studying in Armenia). The Luys Foundation (Armenia) is dormant/defunct.

## Details

### Part A — Scheme Table

**1. International Visegrad Fund — Visegrad Scholarship Program (Eastern Partnership strand)**
- **Funder:** International Visegrad Fund (bloc/IGO; funded by Czechia, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia)
- **Eligible nationalities (verbatim):** "citizens of Albania, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Kosovo*, Moldova, Montenegro, Northern Macedonia, Serbia and Ukraine to study in Visegrad countries." The EaP chapter targets "Armenia, Azerbaijan, Belarus, Georgia, Moldova, and Ukraine."
- **Destination(s):** Czechia, Hungary, Poland, Slovakia
- **Degree level:** Master's and post-master's. Verbatim: "Citizens of the Eastern Partnership or Western Balkans countries can only cover studies at master's (MA or equivalent) or post-master's level (doctoral studies programs or independent research)."
- **Application route:** DIRECT. "Applying for the Visegrad Scholarship Program is possible only through the on-line application system." Applications via My Visegrad portal; no third-party nomination.
- **What it covers:** "The amount of the support is €3,500/semester for scholar + €2,000/semester for the host institution"; scholar money "is at the scholar's full disposal and the Fund cannot cover any additional costs"; 1–2 semesters (up to €7,000 to the scholar).
- **Annual cycle:** Deadline April 15, 12:00 noon; online system opens ~2 months prior. The program page lists the next scholarship mobility cycle as "Opens Jan 1, 2027."
- **Official URL:** visegradfund.org/scholarships (and /apply/mobilities/visegrad-scholarships/)
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** Site publishes a Privacy Statement and Copyright Information page; a Power BI "Project Database" is published (machine-viewable, not a documented API); no explicit machine-reuse/API terms stated.

**2. CEEPUS (Central European Exchange Program for University Studies)**
- **Funder:** Intergovernmental; each member state funds incoming grantees, coordinated via national offices (OeAD Austria, NAWA Poland, SAIA Slovakia) (bloc/IGO)
- **Eligible nationalities (verbatim):** signatories are "Albania, Austria, Bulgaria, Croatia, Montenegro, Czech Republic, Hungary, Macedonia, Moldova, Romania, Poland, Slovakia, Slovenia, Serbia, Bosnia and Herzegovina and Kosovo." **None of the priority countries (Azerbaijan, Turkey, Georgia, Armenia, Central Asia) are members** — out of scope for our population except as a confirmed absence.
- **Degree level:** BA/MA/PhD mobility (not full degree)
- **Application route:** Network or Freemover; "Before submitting an application you have to contact the relevant CEEPUS coordinator at your home institution."
- **Note:** Recorded to document that CEEPUS does NOT cover our priority nationalities.

**3. Czech Government Scholarships for Developing Countries**
- **Funder:** Czech Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports (MŠMT) — government
- **Eligible nationalities (verbatim, 2027/28):** Czech-taught follow-up master's route open to "Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Ukraine and Belarus (democratic forces)"; English-taught master's open to "all eligible countries" including "Bosnia and Herzegovina, Georgia, Ukraine, Belarus (democratic forces), Ethiopia, Nigeria, Rwanda, Zambia, Cambodia, Sri Lanka, Guatemala, Jordan." **Georgia is the only priority country named.** In the 2026/27 call, bachelor's study was available only to citizens of Georgia.
- **Destination(s):** Czech public universities
- **Degree level:** Follow-up (2-year) master's and doctoral. "The condition for granting a scholarship for studies in a follow-up master's study program is the completion of a bachelor's study program."
- **Application route:** DIRECT. "You apply directly for the government scholarship rather than enrolling first."
- **What it covers:** Tuition, living subsidy, health insurance, and (for Czech-taught route) a one-year Czech-language preparatory course at Charles University's Institute for Language and Preparatory Studies.
- **Annual cycle:** 2027/28 applications open 21 August–30 September 2026.
- **Official URL:** msmt.gov.cz (archiv.msmt.gov.cz); studyin.cz
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** No reuse/API terms stated.

**4. Stipendium Hungaricum (per-country / Sending Partner track)**
- **Funder:** Government of Hungary (Tempus Public Foundation) — government
- **Eligible nationalities:** Azerbaijan, Georgia, Armenia and Türkiye are all Sending Partners (also Kazakhstan, Uzbekistan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan). Verbatim (per-country, e.g. Azerbaijan/Georgia/Armenia): "Full degree master studies: in any field of interest." Azerbaijan: full-degree only (partial/exchange "not available"); Georgia and Armenia also eligible for partial/exchange.
- **Destination(s):** Hungary
- **Degree level:** "Stipendium Hungaricum scholarships are available for bachelor's, master's, one-tier master's, doctoral, non-degree programmes (preparatory courses) and postgraduate specialist training courses."
- **Application route:** NOMINATED (hybrid — dual submission). Verbatim: "Applications will be considered eligible only if the responsible authorities of the Sending Partner nominate the applicant"; "Applications will only be considered if the applicant is nominated. No other applications will be considered"; "Sending Partners can only nominate applicants with the same citizenship as the Sending Partner." National authorities: Azerbaijan — State Agency for Science and Higher Education (portal.edu.az); Georgia — International Education Center (app.iec.gov.ge); Armenia — Ministry of Education, Science, Culture and Sport.
- **What it covers:** Tuition-free; monthly stipend "master's level: monthly amount of HUF 43,700 (cca EUR 110) contribution to the living expenses in Hungary, for 12 months a year, until the completion of studies" (HUF/EUR rate as of 13 Oct 2025); free dormitory place or HUF 40,000/month accommodation contribution; medical insurance up to HUF 65,000/year. No airfare. Stipend drops to HUF 30,000/month if the mandatory Hungarian-language exam is failed.
- **Annual cycle (2026/27):** Deadline 15 January 2026, 14:00 CET; Sending Partner nomination lists due to Tempus Public Foundation by 28 February 2026.
- **Official URL:** stipendiumhungaricum.hu (Call for Applications 2026/27 PDF; Partners page)
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** Country/Partners pages are HTML; Call published as PDF; no reuse/API terms stated. **Per-country quotas NOT published.** (An Azerbaijani university source, UFAZ, reported 130 Azerbaijani awards for 2025-26 of which 106 master's — secondary, not official; do not encode as an official quota.)

**5. Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) Scholarship Programme**
- **Funder:** Islamic Development Bank (IGO / multilateral development bank)
- **Eligible nationalities (verbatim, member states):** "Afghanistan, Albania, Algeria, Azerbaijan, Bahrain, ... Kazakhstan, Kuwait, Kyrgyzstan, ... Tajikistan, ... Turkey, Turkmenistan, Uganda, United Arab Emirates, Uzbekistan, Yemen." Priority countries covered: **Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkey, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan.** NOT Georgia or Armenia. Also open to "members of Muslim communities residing in non-member countries" (that list does not include our priority states).
- **Destination(s):** Universities in IsDB member countries (and partner universities)
- **Degree level:** "Bachelor, Master, PhD, and Postdoc." Master of Science Scholarship Programme launched 1998.
- **Application route:** DIRECT. "Applications are received online through the online application system, free of charge"; "IsDB does not charge any application fee at any stage. The IsDB Scholarship is open to citizens of IsDB member countries and to members of Muslim communities residing in non-member countries." (A YTB–IsDB joint programme also exists, routed through Türkiye's YTB.)
- **What it covers:** Full tuition, monthly stipend, medical insurance, round-trip air travel, book/clothing allowance. Structured as an interest-free loan with a service/community component.
- **Annual cycle:** Booklet published annually (2026-27 cycle); dates vary by track.
- **Official URL:** isdb.org (scholarship portal)
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** Booklet published as downloadable PDF; no reuse/API terms stated.

**6. Heydar Aliyev Turkic World Scholarship**
- **Funder:** Ibn Haldun University (Türkiye), jointly with the Organization of Turkic States and the Consulate General of Azerbaijan in Istanbul (foundation/university + bloc)
- **Eligible nationalities (verbatim):** "Applicants must be from full member or observer member states of the Turkic States Organization" — i.e. Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Turkey, Uzbekistan (members); Hungary, Turkmenistan, Northern Cyprus (observers). One student selected per state.
- **Destination(s):** Ibn Haldun University, Istanbul, Türkiye
- **Degree level:** Master's (and doctoral). "Achievement Scholarship (BAB1) will be given to master's degree students." Social Sciences fields.
- **Application route:** NOMINATED / selection-based. "a commission formed by our university and the Turkic States Organization will select one student each from the five full members and three observer members of the Organization of Turkic States. These students will pursue graduate education at Ibn Haldun University." Applicants must "come from universities that are members of the Turkish Universities Association."
- **What it covers:** "Our university will waive tuition fees for scholarship recipients, pay stipends and provide accommodation facilities."
- **Annual cycle:** "Applications for the Haydar Aliyev Turkic World Scholarship will be accepted between April and June, along with other candidates applying for our university's graduate programs."
- **Official URL:** avrasya.ihu.edu.tr/en/heydar-aliyev-turkic-world-scholarship-for-graduate-students
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** Site carries a cookies notice; no reuse/API terms stated.

**7. Orhun Exchange Program (Turkic Universities Union / TÜRKÜNİB, under OTS)**
- **Funder:** Turkic Universities Union (TÜRKÜNİB), under the Organization of Turkic States (bloc/IGO)
- **Eligible nationalities:** enrolled students at TÜRKÜNİB member universities in "Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Türkiye, Uzbekistan, the Turkish Republic of Northern Cyprus (TRNC) and Hungary."
- **Destination(s):** Member universities in the above states
- **Degree level:** Semester exchange only (NOT a degree). "The program is available to Associate Degree, Undergraduate, Master's, and Doctoral students who wish to pursue one semester of study."
- **Application route:** NOMINATED via home university. Applicant must be enrolled at a member university and apply through its Orhun coordinator; "Only one university will be chosen for applications."
- **What it covers:** Host tuition waiver only. "Students do not pay any tuition fees to the host institution during the programme, but they continue to pay the obligatory tuition fees to their home institutions." No stipend amount published.
- **Annual cycle (2026/27):** Departments/quotas notified 08–16 July 2026 (Fall term); later steps undated on the page.
- **Official URL:** turkunib.org/en/orhun
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** Online application forms; no reuse/API terms stated. NOT a master's degree scheme — record as exchange only.

**8. AGBU Scholarship Program (graduate)**
- **Funder:** Armenian General Benevolent Union (diaspora foundation)
- **Eligible nationalities:** ancestry-keyed, not citizenship-keyed. "AGBU offers scholarships to students of Armenian descent" (excluding citizens of Armenia studying in Armenia). Covers Armenians worldwide, including from Armenia studying abroad.
- **Destination(s):** US and non-US highly selective universities worldwide
- **Degree level:** "full-time graduate degree programs (Master's, Doctoral, Professional)."
- **Application route:** DIRECT. Applicants take an eligibility quiz and apply online; "Applicants must secure and demonstrate a major portion of tuition from secondary sources."
- **What it covers:** For 2026-27, "AGBU scholarship awards will range up to $10,000 per student, with a limited number of highly selective grants of up to $20,000 available to exceptional candidates" (US graduate ~$7,500 average); international grants up to $2,000 (£4,000 UK / €2,000 France). Raised minimums: "3.7 GPA for U.S. based graduate study and a 3.5 GPA for all other categories."
- **Annual cycle:** Opens Jan 03, closes May 01.
- **Official URL:** agbu.org/scholarships
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** No reuse/API terms stated.

**9. Bolashak International Scholarship (Kazakhstan outbound)**
- **Funder:** Government of Kazakhstan (Center for International Programs) — government
- **Eligible nationalities:** "Bolashak is exclusively for Kazakhstani citizens" (nationality-keyed to Kazakhstan). "Refugees with official documentation are also eligible."
- **Destination(s):** Leading foreign universities on Kazakhstan's List of Recommended Universities (~33 countries)
- **Degree level:** Master's covered. "Within the framework of the Bolashak Scholarship, citizens of the Republic of Kazakhstan may apply for: ... Master's degree programmes."
- **Application route:** HYBRID. "You may either ask the CIP to locate a university place for you, or you may obtain an offer of admission to a university and then apply for the Bolashak funding." A collateral/return-service pledge (5 years' work in Kazakhstan) applies.
- **What it covers:** Full tuition, living costs, airfare and insurance. For the 2026 cycle, 550 awards reported (490 Bachelor's, 50 Master's, 10 PhD), applications 30 March–31 May 2026.
- **Official URL:** bolashak.gov.kz
- **Terms for automated retrieval:** Government portal; no reuse/API terms stated.

**10. Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (confirmed absence for our population)**
- **Funder:** UK FCDO / Commonwealth Scholarship Commission (government)
- **Eligible nationalities:** citizens of "developing Commonwealth countries." **None of the priority countries are Commonwealth members** — Azerbaijan, Turkey, Georgia, Armenia and the Central Asian states are not eligible. Record as confirmed absence.

**11. OIF / FRQ Bourses de formation à la maîtrise pour la Francophonie (marginal)**
- **Funder:** Fonds de recherche du Québec (government) — OIF-membership-keyed
- **Eligible nationalities:** "il faut détenir la citoyenneté d'un pays membre de l'Organisation internationale de la francophonie (OIF)." Armenia is an OIF member; Georgia is an OIF observer. However the FRQ master's bourse requires that the country "y avoir le français comme statut de langue officielle selon l'OIF," which excludes Armenia and Georgia. Record as marginal/likely-ineligible for the priority population.

### Part B — Which schemes are indexed anywhere

- **No dedicated public index of nationality-keyed master's schemes exists.** General aggregators filter by nationality but are secondary and omit operative clauses:
  - **Mastersportal / ScholarshipPortal (Studyportals)** — filters scholarships by home country; commercial; one competitor description cites coverage of ~50 nationalities and 18 host countries; not authoritative and omits application-route detail.
  - **Campus Bourses (Campus France)** — lists aid "filterable by nationality, field and level"; French government tool, destination-oriented.
  - **DAAD scholarship database** — filters by nationality (Germany destination).
  - **ARMACAD** — indexes opportunities for the Armenian/Caucasus academic community; useful for discovery but secondary/aggregator; not a primary source for facts.
- **Visegrad Fund** publishes a Power BI "Project Database" / results archive — a machine-viewable feed, though not a documented API with reuse terms.
- Most schemes (IsDB, Stipendium Hungaricum, Czech Government, Heydar Aliyev, Orhun, Bolashak) appear only on their own sites plus scholarship blogs; none publishes a documented machine-readable feed or reuse terms. **This is a flagged gap: there is no bloc scheme with a documented machine-readable feed.**

### Part C — Checked, no confirmable master's scheme

- **CEEPUS** — real master's mobility scheme but membership excludes all priority countries. Confirmed absence for our population.
- **Commonwealth Scholarships** — priority countries not Commonwealth members. Confirmed absence.
- **Black Sea Economic Cooperation (BSEC)** — members include Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Turkey, but BSEC runs only an internship program (Istanbul PERMIS: "Applicants are required to be citizens of the BSEC Member States, Observers or Sectoral Dialogue Partners"), not a master's scholarship. No master's scheme confirmed from a primary source.
- **Organization of Turkic States / TÜRKSOY / Turkic Academy** — no full-degree master's scholarship. The International Turkic Academy is a Turkology research-coordination body ("facilitate the integration of the Turkic World in science and education"); TÜRKSOY is cultural; no degree scholarship published by either. The only OTS-linked master's-level offerings are the university-hosted Heydar Aliyev award and the Orhun semester exchange (both above).
- **Colombo Plan** — priority countries are not members (membership is Asia-Pacific); occasional master's scholarships are routed through member governments (e.g., KDI School, Korea; Chulabhorn Graduate Institute, Thailand). Not applicable to our population.
- **GUAM (Georgia, Ukraine, Azerbaijan, Moldova)** — no master's scholarship scheme confirmed from a primary source.
- **Luys Foundation (Armenia)** — funded Armenian and diaspora students for top-university master's/PhD (up to 50–75% of costs with return-service conditions) but the program was reported suspended/defunct after 2018; not currently confirmed live.

## Recommendations

1. **For a student who needs a student-initiated (DIRECT) master's option now:**
   - Citizens of **Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkey, Turkmenistan or Uzbekistan** should prioritise the **IsDB Scholarship** — it is DIRECT and full-cost (the only scheme in this run that is both). Read the interest-free-loan/service terms before committing.
   - **Armenian, Azerbaijani or Georgian** citizens can apply DIRECT to the **Visegrad EaP scholarship**, but treat it as a 1–2 semester mobility top-up, not a full-degree solution.
   - A **Georgian** citizen can additionally apply DIRECT to the **Czech Government Scholarships** for a full follow-up master's.
   - A **Kazakhstani** citizen who wants to study abroad has **Bolashak** (HYBRID, full-cost, but with a 5-year return-service collateral pledge).
2. **Treat Stipendium Hungaricum, Heydar Aliyev and Orhun as nomination-dependent.** Begin the national nomination process early — contact the Azerbaijani State Agency for Science and Higher Education, the Georgian International Education Center, or the Armenian Ministry of Education well before the 15 January 2026 SH deadline and 28 February 2026 nomination cut-off. Do not treat these as fallbacks a student can trigger unilaterally.
3. **For Armenian-heritage students, add AGBU** as a DIRECT top-up regardless of citizenship (note the raised GPA thresholds: 3.7 for US graduate study, 3.5 elsewhere).
4. **Benchmarks that change the plan:**
   - If IsDB's loan/service structure is unacceptable, fall back to destination-inbound schemes (covered by the separate government-inbound run).
   - If a national Sending Partner publishes a Stipendium Hungaricum quota for a given country, re-rank SH as a primary option for that nationality.
   - If the Luys Foundation is confirmed revived, add it as a DIRECT full-cost option for Armenians.
   - If the Heydar Aliyev scholarship is confirmed running for a current cycle, it becomes a viable (if single-seat, nominated) full-cost Türkiye option for Turkic-state citizens.

## Caveats
- Per-country Stipendium Hungaricum quotas are not published by the funder; the only numeric figure found (130 Azerbaijani awards, 106 master's, 2025-26) is from a secondary university source (UFAZ) and should not be encoded as an official quota.
- The Heydar Aliyev scholarship's most recent confirmed cohort on the primary Ibn Haldun University site is the 2024-25 / 2025-26 intake; continuation for later years is not confirmed from the funder.
- The IsDB scheme is not a pure grant — it is structured as an interest-free loan with a community-service obligation; read the annual booklet's terms.
- Luys Foundation status is inferred from 2017-18 reporting of suspension; there is no current primary confirmation of revival or formal closure.
- OIF/Francophonie master's routes are marginal for Armenia and Georgia because of the French-official-language condition in the FRQ bourse; treat as likely ineligible pending direct confirmation.
- The Türkiye-specific Stipendium Hungaricum Partners entry (exact per-level fields, national portal and route sentence) could not be captured verbatim from the primary site in this pass; Türkiye's status as an eligible Sending Partner covering master's is confirmed from the official Call, but its national application portal should be verified on the Türkiye Partners page / MEB announcement before relying on it.
- Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye Scholarships), GKS, DAAD, Chevening and the Stipendium Hungaricum open track are out of scope for this run (general destination-inbound, no nationality restriction) and belong to the separate government-inbound sweep — their omission here is division of labour, not a claim they are already documented.
