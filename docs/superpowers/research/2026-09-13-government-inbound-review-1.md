# Inbound Government Master's Scholarships for Foreign Nationals: A Cross-Country Sweep (2026/27 cycle)

## TL;DR
- **The single most valuable structural finding: descent-, region- and kin-keyed sub-tracks exist inside schemes you likely already hold and cannot currently represent** — confirmed live for 2026/27 in South Korea's GKS (Overseas Korean/Adoptee: 24 places from 8 countries), Türkiye Bursları (YTB "akraba topluluklar"/kin communities), Poland's NAWA (gen. Anders, Polish descent), India's ICCR/SPDC (diaspora), Italy's MAECI (IRE, Italians resident abroad), China's CSC (Silk Road/Belt-and-Road region track), and — as a residence-keyed track — the UK's Chevening "South Caucasus" (Abkhazia/South Ossetia/Nagorno-Karabakh residents only).
- **For the launch markets (Azerbaijan, Türkiye), the widest DIRECT or HYBRID full-cost master's routes are: Türkiye Bursları (both, DIRECT), Stipendium Hungaricum (both, HYBRID), Chevening (both, DIRECT), Italian MAECI (both, DIRECT), Eiffel/France (both, NOMINATED), and the Russian Government Quota (both, NOMINATED, status caveats). Swedish SISGP and Polish Banach cover Azerbaijan but not Türkiye. Australia Awards excludes both Azerbaijan and Türkiye entirely** (its list is Indo-Pacific/Africa/Middle-East development countries).
- **No cross-country machine-readable index of *government* inbound scholarships exists.** Every aggregate directory located (scholars4dev, IEFA, InternationalScholarships.com, Mastersportal, EURAXESS, and DAAD's own database) is commercial or single-country, mixes outbound with inbound and government with private, and publishes no API/feed. This is an unfilled gap and the highest-value asset to build or license.

## Key Findings

1. **The sub-national tier is real and under-covered.** For Germany, education is a Länder competence: alongside the national DAAD, the Free State of Bavaria funds BAYHOST (region+nationality-keyed, 10 named countries, none of your nine) and Baden-Württemberg funds the BW-Stipendium (exchange-semester, not a full master's). This confirms the tier exists but, for your nine markets, the national scheme (DAAD) is the operative one. Spain, Italy, Belgium, Canada, Switzerland and Austria each need the same regional split; I have confirmed the tier's existence but could not fully source every region within budget (flagged as gaps).

2. **Descent tracks change the odds materially.** GKS's Overseas Korean/Adoptee track is 24 places across 8 countries versus 776 general places contested by 143 countries/regions — for an ethnic-Korean Kazakh or Uzbek applicant this is a second, far smaller pool. It is keyed to Korean ethnicity, not citizenship, and is invisible to any nationality-based search.

3. **Russia's quota is one legal pool, not several.** Contrary to the "compatriot sub-track" hypothesis, Decree No. 2150 (18 Dec 2020) folds compatriots *into* the single 30,000-person quota. St Petersburg State University's official admission regulations reproduce the decree scope verbatim: it covers "foreign nationals, stateless citizens, **including compatriots residing abroad** … to degree programmes of higher education (bachelor's … specialist's … and master's degree programmes)." There is no separate numbered compatriot allocation — compatriots compete inside the same pool via the same route.

4. **Two named schemes are closed/suspended.** The Netherlands' Orange Knowledge Programme (OKP) is closed — Nuffic's official statement: "The Orange Knowledge Programme (OKP) will end in December 2024 … there is no prospect of new, significant funding for development cooperation from the Dutch government." Report it as closed, not live.

5. **The J-1 return rule and Chevening's contractual return clause are real post-study constraints** that would look like "no post-study work route" to your model and must be flagged as floors that are not.

- **Scheme:** Baden-Württemberg-STIPENDIUM (Land of Baden-Württemberg). **Track:** general (regional). **Eligibility key:** OPEN, via partner-university nomination. **Body:** Baden-Württemberg Stiftung. **Degree:** covers master's students but as 1–2 **exchange semesters**, not a full degree — note as partial/not-full-master's. **Route:** NOMINATED (International Offices handle selection). **Covers:** €600–1,400/month. **Cycle:** ~31 March. **URL:** bw-stipendium.de. - **Scheme:** BAYHOST one-year scholarship (Free State of Bavaria). **Track:** region+nationality-keyed. **Eligibility key:** CITIZENSHIP of 10 named states — "graduates (with appropriate citizenship) from Bulgaria, Croatia, Czechia, Hungary, Poland, Romania, Russia, Serbia, Slovakia and Ukraine." **Body:** Bavarian State Ministry of Science and the Arts via BAYHOST, Univ. ### Türkiye

### United States
- **Scheme:** Fulbright Foreign Student Program. **Track:** general. **Eligibility key:** CITIZENSHIP of a partner country (US citizens/green-card holders ineligible). **Body:** US Department of State (administered by IIE and binational commissions/embassies). **Degree:** Master's or PhD. **Route:** NOMINATED via in-country Fulbright commission/US embassy (some countries offer IIE vs Independent placement). **Eligible nationalities:** partner countries (India, Pakistan, Vietnam, Indonesia, Kazakhstan, Georgia, Armenia, Azerbaijan, Türkiye have programs — verify country pages). **Covers:** tuition/fees, living stipend, airfare, health benefit, J-1 visa. **Obligation (quote):** "All Fulbright grantees are subject to a two-year home country residency requirement" (INA §212(e)) — grantees "will need to return to [home country] for a minimum of two years on completion." **This excludes grantees from the US post-study work route** — flag as a floor that is not. **Cycle:** varies by country (typically spring deadline ~15 months ahead). **URL:** foreign.fulbrightonline.org / country commissions. **Numbers:** ~4,000 foreign students annually (secondary). **Terms:** none stated.

### Japan

### Russia
- **Scheme:** Russian Government Quota (квота Правительства РФ), 2026/27. **Track:** general (single legal pool). **Eligibility key:** CITIZENSHIP — St Petersburg State University's official admission regulations cite Decree No. 2150: the quota covers "foreign nationals, stateless citizens, **including compatriots residing abroad** … to degree programmes of higher education (bachelor's … specialist's … and master's degree programmes)." ## Part C — Where these are indexed

- **No cross-country machine-readable index of government inbound scholarships exists.** Directories located are all commercial/aggregator sites that mix inbound with outbound and government with private, publish no API or downloadable feed, and are not authoritative: scholars4dev.com, iefa.org, internationalscholarships.com, internationalstudent.com, edupass.org, mastersportal.com. DAAD's own scholarship database (www2.daad.de) is the closest to a structured, filterable government-linked index but is Germany-scoped with no open feed. EURAXESS (europa.eu) aggregates European schemes without a feed. **Recommendation: this index is the highest-value asset to build; none exists to license.**
- Individual schemes appear only on their own official portals (turkiyeburslari.gov.tr, campusfrance.org, stipendiumhungaricum.hu, chevening.org, studyinkorea.go.kr, campuschina.org, education-in-russia.com, si.se, nawa.gov.pl, studyinitaly.esteri.it, dfat.gov.au, iccr.gov.in).

## Recommendations
1. **Immediately extend your data model to carry non-citizenship eligibility keys** (DESCENT, REGION, RESIDENCE) as first-class fields. Without this you cannot represent GKS Overseas Korean, Chevening South Caucasus, CSC Silk Road, or the Türkiye/Poland/Italy descent tracks — all confirmed live. Change trigger: finding even one more descent track in a held scheme validates prioritising this.
2. **Prioritise the full-cost routes open to both launch markets** (Türkiye Bursları, Chevening, Stipendium Hungaricum, MAECI, Eiffel, Russian Quota, plus MEXT/Fulbright/GKS via nomination) for launch-market coverage, and explicitly annotate the J-1/Chevening/Australia return obligations as post-study-work floors.
3. **Commission a dedicated second pass** for the ~30 unsourced countries in Part B — especially Ireland GOI-IES, Malaysia MTCP, Serbia "World in Serbia", the Baltics, Czech/Slovak schemes, and the Spain/Belgium/Canada/Switzerland/Austria regional tiers. Each federal/devolved state deserves its own run given the Länder precedent; if any one doubles the work, split it out rather than thinning.
4. **Do not treat OKP as available.** Re-check nuffic.nl quarterly for a successor.
5. **Build the government-scholarship index yourself** — it is the single highest-value asset and does not exist in machine-readable form anywhere.

## Caveats
- Several eligible-country lists were corroborated from official PDFs, but a few country-membership details rest on secondary aggregators quoting the official call; treat the exact 2026/27 country lists as needing a final primary-source verification per market.
- Quota figures are dated: GKS quotas are 2026 (2,000 graduate places, up from 1,820 in 2025); the Russian 30,000 is the 2023-onward statutory ceiling (actual distributed number not published); Stipendium Hungaricum reports "more than 4,500 scholarships … from 80,000 applications" for 2025; Türkiye Bursları reports ~5,000 awards/year and 121,830 applications in 2024; Chevening provided 1,400 scholarships in 2022-23.
- No applicant-count denominators are published for most schemes; success rates were not computed where only one half is known.
- **Countries cut for capacity (named):** deep primary-source coverage was not completed for Brunei, Kuwait, Bahrain, Qatar, UAE, Singapore, Hong Kong, Malta, Cyprus and Luxembourg — the destinations your launch-market students are least likely to choose. These are named gaps to fill in a later run, not confirmed absences.
