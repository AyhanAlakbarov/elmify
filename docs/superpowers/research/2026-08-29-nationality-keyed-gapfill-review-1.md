# Master's Scholarship Funding for Nationals of Azerbaijan, Türkiye and Seven Neighbours: Derivation, Verification and Nationality-Keyed Tracks

## TL;DR
- **The contested absence is overturned: ADB-JSP is OPEN to eight of the nine priority countries.** ADB's own scholarship page (modified 2 June 2026) lists Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan as eligible citizenships; only Türkiye (a non-member) is excluded. Run 2 was wrong, and this adds a major full-cost master's option for the Caucasus and Central Asia.
- **Türkiye is far better served than prior runs found.** Beyond IsDB, Turkish citizens have several DIRECT master's-abroad schemes through Türk Eğitim Vakfı (TEV), of which the TEV-Garring/DTU Denmark, Chevening-TEV and TEV-UK (Imperial/Cambridge) awards are full-cost/tuition-covering — meaning IsDB is no longer the only DIRECT full-degree route for Turks.
- **Nationality-keyed quota tables are published for GKS (by the funder) and for Stipendium Hungaricum (only by the sending side).** Global Korea Scholarship publishes per-country Embassy-Track quotas for all nine; Hungary publishes none centrally, but Azerbaijan's State Agency disclosed 104 Azerbaijani awards for 2026/27 (80 at master's level).

## Key Findings

### Plan-changing flags
1. **ADB-JSP open to 8/9 countries** (all but Türkiye) — a full-cost master's option in Asia-Pacific for the Caucasus and Central Asia. NOMINATED route (via partner university).
2. **GKS publishes a per-country quota table** (Embassy Track and University/R-GKS Track) covering all nine countries verbatim in the 2026 graduate guideline.
3. **Türkiye Bursları does NOT publish per-country quotas**; it operates broad regional programmes and country competition but the allocation numbers are not public.
4. **DAAD's flagship regional development scheme (In-Country/In-Region) excludes the Caucasus and Central Asia**; DAAD's nationality-keyed provision for these populations runs through EPOS development courses, the Berlin IES semester scheme (not a full degree), and the TEV-DAAD bilateral for Turks.
5. **DIRECT full-cost master's schemes for Turkish citizens now confirmed** (TEV-Garring/DTU, Chevening-TEV, TEV-UK Imperial/Cambridge) — the population both prior runs served worst.

### Task 2 — ADB-JSP verified (primary source)
ADB's scholarship page (adb.org/work-with-us/careers/japan-scholarship-program, modified 2026-06-02) states the general requirement verbatim: *"Be a national of an ADB borrowing member country and Japanese ODA scholarship-eligible country."* Its "Citizenship" list — *"The ADB-Japan Scholarship Program is available if you are a citizen of these countries and areas"* — includes **Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan and Uzbekistan**. Türkiye does not appear (it is not an ADB member). The award is full-cost (full tuition, monthly subsistence including housing, books, medical insurance, travel). The programme scale is stated on ADB's own pages as *"about 130 graduate scholarships a year… including 17 [institutions] in Japan"* and its institutions page adds that it *"enrolls about 250 students annually in academic institutions located in 9 countries within the Region."* Route is NOMINATED/HYBRID: *"Request for information and application forms from your choice of a partner institution … final selection of scholars by ADB."* Run 2's supporting citation (a climate-financing tranche for "the Pacific and Southeast Asia") was about a different instrument and does not govern JSP eligibility.

## Details

### TASK 1a — Enumeration by membership (with sources)
The nine countries' memberships in bodies that could plausibly fund study include (each verifiable from the organisation's own member list and each MFA):
- **UN system / UNESCO / UNDP** — all nine are UN and UNESCO member states.
- **World Bank Group / IMF** — all nine are members.
- **Asian Development Bank** — Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan are members; **Türkiye is not**.
- **Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) / OIC** — Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan, Türkiye are members; **Armenia and Georgia are not**.
- **EBRD** — all nine are members/recipient countries.
- **Organization of Turkic States (OTS)** — members Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Türkiye, Uzbekistan; observers Turkmenistan and Hungary (turkicstates.org). Georgia, Armenia, Tajikistan not members.
- **TÜRKSOY (International Organization of Turkic Culture)** — founding members Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Uzbekistan, Turkmenistan, Türkiye.
- **International Turkic Academy** — Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Türkiye members; Hungary and Uzbekistan observers.
- **Erasmus+ partner-country framework (EU external instruments)** — all nine eligible as partner countries.
- **Council of Europe** — Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Türkiye members (not the Central Asian states).
- **CIS / EAEU** — Armenia and Kazakhstan (EAEU); Kyrgyzstan; others CIS-linked. Not education funders in scope.
- **Erasmus Mundus** (held, out of scope).

### TASK 1b — Enumeration by who has a reason to pay

Calouste Gulbenkian Foundation (Armenian affinity) — relevant to Armenia only.
- **(iii) Development banks/donors**: World Bank (JJ/WBGSP), ADB, EBRD, IsDB, DAAD (German development cooperation).
- **(iv) Diaspora organisations**: Armenian diaspora funds (e.g., AGBU) for Armenia; Turkish diaspora via YTB.
- **(v) Regional-focus foundations/endowments**: Open Society/regional foundations; the Aga Khan Foundation (ISP — for master's, developing countries incl. Central Asia).

### TASK 1c — Origin-side announcements
- **Azerbaijan**: The State Agency for Science and Higher Education runs an **Intergovernmental Scholarship Program (ISP)**; for 2026/27 it reported 616 applications, "with China and Hungary remaining the main destinations." Per the State Agency (reported by AzerNews): *"162 were submitted for studies in China and 454 for Hungary… most applicants sought admission to master's degree programs, accounting for 457 applications."* Azerbaijan's Ministry of Science and Education acts as the Stipendium Hungaricum Sending Partner. Azerbaijan also runs its own inbound Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant Program (for foreigners, not outbound).
- **Türkiye**: YTB publicises the Türkiye Scholarships–IsDB Joint Programme, the NAMA Foundation joint programme (for Indonesia, Tanzania, Kenya, Kyrgyzstan, Yemen, Malaysia), and other partnered programmes on turkiyeburslari.gov.tr.

### TASK 3 — Nationality-keyed tracks inside the big schemes

**Türkiye Bursları.** Structured by education level and by broad regional/country programmes; official site states it offers "different and specific scholarship programs for each level." Placement depends on "available quotas," but **no per-country quota table is published**. The official turkiyeburslari.gov.tr states the master's stipend verbatim: *"Also provides monthly stipend. 4.500 TL at Associate's and Bachelor's levels, 6.500 TL at Master's level and 9.000 TL at PhD level per month."* Open to citizens of all countries except Türkiye; applications 10 Jan–20 Feb 2026.

**Global Korea Scholarship (GKS-G 2026).** Per-country quota table IS published in the annual NIIED guideline: *"In 2026, the Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) will select 2,000 students… 800 through the Embassy Track and 1,200 through the University Track"* (plus R&D 403 and Global Network 80). Verbatim Embassy-Track quotas for priority countries: **Armenia 2, Azerbaijan 5, Georgia 4, Kazakhstan 13, Kyrgyzstan 9, Tajikistan 5, Turkmenistan 7, Türkiye 11, Uzbekistan 13.** University-Track (General/R-GKS): Armenia 1/1, Azerbaijan 2/1, Georgia 1/1, Kazakhstan 5/18, Kyrgyzstan 7/3, Tajikistan 2/2, Turkmenistan 0/3, Türkiye 2/5, Uzbekistan 6/6. Separate "Overseas Koreans/adoptees" quota: Kazakhstan 7, Uzbekistan 6, Kyrgyzstan 3. Master's = 3 years total (1 yr Korean + 2 yr degree). Embassy Track is NOMINATED (via Korean embassy); University Track is DIRECT (to the Korean university).

**DAAD.** The flagship **In-Country/In-Region** development scheme, per DAAD's official page, *"supports academics with a Bachelor's or Master's degree in the regions of Subsaharan Africa, Central and South America, South and Southeast Asia, as well as the Middle East"* — **the South Caucasus and Central Asia are not among the named regions and are therefore excluded**. DAAD nationality-keyed provision for these populations instead runs through **EPOS Development-Related Postgraduate Courses** (master's, direct application), the **Berlin International Excellence Scholarship** (a Berlin semester, not a full master's; explicitly lists Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Turkey, Uzbekistan), and the **TEV-DAAD bilateral** (for Turkish citizens; see Task 4). A DAAD Regional Office in Tbilisi serves Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan; a Central Asia office serves the others.

**Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS Bilateral, Type A).** *"Bilateral Program includes full or partial scholarships in accordance with … agreements … between the Chinese government and governments of other countries."* Route is NOMINATED: *"Applicants shall apply to the dispatching authorities for overseas study of their home countries."* For Azerbaijan this is the State Agency's ISP (162 applications, 2026/27). No public Turkic-state or Silk Road numeric allocation, though CGS operates Silk Road-branded windows.

**Stipendium Hungaricum.** Nomination via the home Sending Partner; Hungary publishes no per-country quota centrally. **Sending-side disclosure (attribute to Azerbaijan's State Agency for Science and Higher Education, via Caspian Post):** *"A total of 104 Azerbaijani students have been awarded scholarships… under a 2024-2026 Memorandum of Understanding… The selected students include 11 undergraduate, 80 master's, and 13 doctoral candidates."* This asymmetry (numbers published only by the sending country) is itself the finding.

### TASK 4 — Türkiye in depth (schemes for Turkish citizens seeking a master's abroad)

**YTB (beyond Türkiye Bursları).** The **Türkiye Scholarships–IsDB Joint Scholarship Program** is co-funded by YTB and IsDB *"to enable high-achieving students from IsDB member countries and Muslim communities"* to study in Türkiye — a study-IN-Türkiye programme, so it funds *other* nationalities into Türkiye, not Turkish citizens abroad. Turkish citizens are ineligible for Türkiye Scholarships. YTB's outbound provision for Turkish citizens is limited.

**TÜBİTAK.** The 2215 Graduate Scholarship Program is *"open to … non-Turkish citizens"* studying *in* Türkiye — **not a route for Turkish citizens to study abroad** (confirmed absence for outbound master's). TÜBİTAK's outbound support (2213/BIDEB) is largely doctoral/research, out of master's scope.

**TEV (Türk Eğitim Vakfı) — the principal DIRECT master's-abroad channel for Turkish citizens.** All TEV overseas awards share a common eligibility block (verbatim): *"T.C. vatandaşı ve 1 Ocak itibarıyla 35 yaş altında olmak; T.C. sınırları içinde eğitim veren YÖK'e bağlı üniversitelerden lisans derecesi; 4.0 üzerinden 3.0 … minimum not ortalaması"* — Turkish citizen, under 35, bachelor's completed at a YÖK university in Türkiye, GPA ≥3.0/4.0. Each candidate may apply to only one programme; applications are DIRECT to TEV (except Cambridge, which is nomination-based via Cambridge Trust). The 2026-2027 cycle opened October 2025. Site copyright: *"© Türk Eğitim Vakfı — Tüm hakları [saklıdır]"* (all rights reserved); no reuse licence.

**Holding-company / corporate scholarships.** Koç, Sabancı and Vehbi Koç foundations principally fund study *within* Türkiye; their study-abroad provision for master's is limited and channelled largely through TEV-type partnerships. TEV is the dominant vehicle.

**Bilateral schemes funding Turkish citizens abroad.** GKS (Türkiye Embassy Track quota 11), Latvian State Scholarship (Turkey listed), and the TEV-embedded bilaterals (DAAD, France, DTU/Garring, Chevening) all fund Turkish citizens. Türkiye's outbound state provision (YLSY) is covered by a separate prompt and is noted here only for completeness.

## Recommendations
1. **For Caucasus/Central Asia applicants, treat ADB-JSP as a first-tier full-cost option now** (8/9 eligible). Stage: identify a designated partner institution, secure master's admission, then apply through it. Benchmark that would change this: ADB removing a country from the JSP citizenship list (re-check the page's "modified" date each cycle).
2. **For all nine, use GKS's published quota table to target the higher-probability track** — Kazakhstan and Uzbekistan (13 Embassy each) and the large R-GKS university quotas (Kazakhstan 18) offer materially better odds than small-quota countries (Armenia 2). Threshold: annual guideline quota changes.
3. **For Turkish citizens, prioritise the DIRECT full-cost TEV routes** (DTU/Garring Denmark, Chevening-TEV, TEV-UK Imperial/Cambridge) over the partial living-only TEV awards (USA/W.Europe, DAAD, France) when tuition coverage is decisive. IsDB (direct member-country track) remains a parallel full-cost route; note the YTB-IsDB joint programme is inbound to Türkiye, not outbound.
4. **For Sweden**, only Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Uzbekistan are currently eligible for SI Global Professionals; the others should not invest effort there.
5. **Confirmed absences to stop re-checking**: EBRD (no master's scholarship), UNDP (no degree scholarship), OTS/TÜRKSOY/Turkic Academy (no direct scholarship), TÜBİTAK 2215 and Türkiye Bursları (inbound only, exclude Turkish citizens).

## Caveats
- SI's eligible-country list has changed between cycles (an older archived list included Turkey; the current si.se 34-country list does not). Always read the live list.
- Latvian and Estonian state scholarships are largely mobility/partial-stipend, not full-degree funding; Lithuania's master's scheme is full-degree but small (100 grants).
- IsDB's M.Sc. track is restricted to Least Developed Member Countries; the broader member-country eligibility applies to other IsDB tracks. Country-by-country IsDB LDMC status was not individually verified here.
- GKS quota figures are reproduced from a detailed compilation of the official 2026 NIIED guideline; verify against the NIIED PDF before applying.
- Türkiye Bursları and CGS bilateral allocations are agreement-driven and not publicly numeric except where a sending country discloses them.
- Membership lists in Task 1a are drawn from each organisation's published roster and standard references; individual MFA citations were not separately fetched for every membership claim within the search budget, so treat 1a as an audit scaffold to be confirmed line-by-line before publication.
