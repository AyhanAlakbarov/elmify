# Scholarship Landscape for International Master's Students: A Data-Model Validation Sample

## TL;DR
- The 12 schemes fall into three structural archetypes a data model must accommodate: **funder-country study** (DAAD, Eiffel, MEXT, GKS, CSC, Türkiye Bursları, Stipendium Hungaricum, Azerbaijan's Heydar Aliyev grant, Russia's quota), **portable-within-funder-country** (Chevening, Commonwealth — UK only, but student chooses the university), and **consortium/multi-country** (Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters). Two requested countries do not fit at all: **Georgia** has no open national inbound scholarship, and **Russia's** scheme is a decentralized quota run by overseas Rossotrudnichestvo offices.
- Initiation mechanics vary sharply and are the highest-value field for the model: **direct student application** (Türkiye Bursları, Chevening, CSC Type A, GKS Embassy, EMJM), **institution/consortium nomination** (Eiffel — university applies for you; DAAD EPOS — apply to the university; MEXT University Recommendation; Commonwealth — national nominating agency; Stipendium Hungaricum — Sending Partner), and **home-government nomination** (Azerbaijan, Russia). Some schemes span two archetypes (GKS and MEXT both have embassy + university tracks with joint selection).
- For the crawler, the durable seed pages are the **national study-in-X portals and ministry directories**, not individual scheme pages: studyinjapan.go.jp, studyinkorea.go.kr, campusfrance.org, funding-guide.de (DAAD database), cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk, chevening.org, turkiyeburslari.gov.tr, stipendiumhungaricum.hu, studyinazerbaijan.edu.az, campuschina.org, education-in-russia.com, and the EACEA/Erasmus+ EMJM catalogue.

## Key Findings
- **Most schemes fund study only in the funder's own country.** The only genuinely portable schemes are the UK's Chevening and Commonwealth (any UK university, student's choice) and Erasmus Mundus (multi-country by design). None of the Asian government schemes are portable.
- **Academic bars are almost always comparative, not absolute** — published minima (e.g., Türkiye 75% for master's, Commonwealth 2:1 honours) act as floors, with final selection competitive.
- **Selectivity data is now published officially for a few schemes but not most.** YTB (Türkiye) and Chevening publish award counts and application volumes; the CSC portal and most others do not.
- **Two requested countries are structural mismatches:** Georgia has no open national inbound master's scholarship; Russia's "scheme" is a quota administered entirely by overseas Rossotrudnichestvo offices with no single central application calendar.

## Details

### 1. Germany — DAAD (EPOS / Development-Related Postgraduate Courses; and Study Scholarships for Master's)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** EPOS is for graduates from a published list of developing/newly-industrialised countries. The eligible-country and eligible-course lists are on the official DAAD scholarship database entry (https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50076777). The generic "Study Scholarships – Master Studies for All Academic Disciplines" is open more broadly by country (https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50026200). No fixed per-country numeric quota is published.
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Monthly stipend of €992 for graduates (master's level), plus health/accident/liability insurance, a travel allowance (unless covered by the home country), and possible rent/family allowances; EPOS recipients are exempted from tuition at participating universities (https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50076777). Funds study **only in Germany**. Not portable.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Bachelor's (usually 4-year) in an appropriate subject; at least two years' relevant professional experience (EPOS); an above-average degree (upper third); resided in Germany no more than 15 months before applying (https://www.meg.uni-freiburg.de/admissions/Scholarships/daad%20scholarship). Threshold plus comparative selection.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Direct application to the university offering the EPOS course (up to three courses), NOT to DAAD directly; DAAD makes the final selection. Admission letter must be submitted before funding begins if not held at application (https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=57742130).
5. **TIMELINE:** Decentralized — deadlines depend on the chosen course; the EPOS course list carries deadlines for 2027/2028 (https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/stipendium/datenbank/en/21148-scholarship-database/?detail=50076777). No single scheme-wide deadline. **Mismatch: no fixed central dates.**
6. **SELECTIVITY:** DAAD states that since 1987 more than 7,000 EPOS scholarships have been awarded, ~50% to women, and over 90% complete their degrees (https://www.daad.de/en/information-services-for-higher-education-institutions/further-information-on-daad-programmes/epos/). Annual applications-received / award-rate not published.
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** The DAAD scholarship database at **www.funding-guide.de** and https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/scholarships/daad-scholarships/ is the official enumerating index; each programme has a stable `datenbank/...?detail=` URL. Excellent crawler seed — it lists all DAAD programmes plus selected third-party funders.

### 2. France — Eiffel Excellence Scholarship (and Campus France-administered schemes)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** All nationalities except France; dual nationals holding French nationality are excluded; master's candidates cannot already be studying in France (https://euraxess.ec.europa.eu/worldwide/lac/news/france-eiffel-scholarships-phd-and-masters-degrees-2026). No published per-country quota.
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Monthly allowance of €1,200 at master's level (effective January 2026), plus international and national transport, insurance, housing-search assistance, cultural activities; **tuition is NOT covered** by the Eiffel programme itself (though French-government scholars are exempt from national-diploma tuition at public institutions) (https://www.campusfrance.org/en/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-implementation). Funds study **only in France**.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Age limit — master's candidates must be no more than 29 on the selection-committee date (born after 31 March 1996 for the 2026 cycle) (https://u-paris.fr/language/en/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-2026/). Selection is by academic excellence; some institutions require ~15/20 average (https://www.sorbonne-universite.fr/en/news/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-programme-2026/2027). Comparative/excellence-based.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Students **cannot apply directly**; a French higher-education institution must accept, support and submit the application to Campus France (https://www.sciencespo.fr/students/en/fees-funding/bursaries-financial-aid/eiffel-scholarship/). Institution-nomination model.
5. **TIMELINE (2026 cycle):** Call opened 1 October 2025; deadline for receipt of applications by Campus France 8 January 2026; results published from 30 March 2026 (https://www.campusfrance.org/en/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-program). Note institutions set earlier internal deadlines (e.g., Sorbonne 28 November 2025).
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Number of applications/awards not published on the Campus France scheme page. **Not published.**
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **campusfrance.org** is the official index — the Eiffel pages (program, implementation) plus the broader Campus France scholarship directory enumerate French government schemes. Good crawler seed.

### 3. Japan — MEXT (Monbukagakusho) Scholarship
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Nationals of countries with diplomatic relations with Japan; for some tracks 75%+ of nominees come from MEXT-designated countries (appendix list) (https://www.titech.ac.jp/english/international-student-exchange/prospective-students/scholarships/mext-university-general). The University Recommendation general program is limited to nationals of MEXT-designated countries/regions (https://www.kyoto-u.ac.jp/en/education-campus/procedures/scholarships/mext).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Monthly stipend ~¥144,000 (master's) / ¥145,000 (doctoral) for Embassy Recommendation, with +¥2,000–3,000 for designated regions; some University Recommendation tracks list ¥147,000 (master's). Tuition/enrollment fees waived; round-trip economy airfare provided (https://www.s.u-tokyo.ac.jp/en/admission/scholarship.html). Funds study **only in Japan**.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Master's/research applicants must be under 35; hold a bachelor's or 16 years of education; grantees must maintain a GPA of 2.30+ (out of 3.00) each year (https://www.titech.ac.jp/english/international-student-exchange/prospective-students/scholarships/mext-university-general). Threshold plus comparative screening/exams.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Two routes — **Embassy Recommendation** (apply via the Japanese embassy/consulate in your country of citizenship; embassy screens, then MEXT places) and **University Recommendation** (a Japanese university nominates you to MEXT). Joint two-body selection in both cases (https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/planning/scholarships/mext-scholarships/).
5. **TIMELINE:** Embassy Recommendation for 2026 arrival: first screening Jan–Feb by embassies; applications submitted late in the prior year (varies by country). For 2027 arrival, recruitment April–May 2026, first screening May–July 2026 (https://www.studyinjapan.go.jp/en/smap-stopj-applications-research.html). The US embassy set 12 Feb 2026 (Japanese Studies) / 21 May 2026 (research) deadlines (https://www.us.emb-japan.go.jp/itpr_en/mext-scholarship.html). **Dates are country-specific — partial mismatch.**
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Number of applications/awards not published centrally by MEXT. **Not published.**
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **studyinjapan.go.jp** is the official enumerating portal (scholarship overview page links each track); also mext.go.jp and each Japanese embassy site. studyinjapan.go.jp is the primary crawler seed.

### 4. South Korea — Global Korea Scholarship (GKS) / NIIED, Graduate track
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Citizens of NIIED-designated countries; neither the applicant nor their parents may hold Korean citizenship (https://www.applykite.com/blog/phd-guide-scholarship-korea). Per-country quotas are published each cycle: the 2026 Graduate total quota is **2,000 scholars** (Embassy Track 800; University Track 1,200); e.g., Georgia has 4 Embassy + 2 University places (2026 GKS-G Application Guidelines, NIIED, https://www.niied.go.kr/web/NIIED/contents/niiedEng/eng_gksDegree, posted via studyinkorea.go.kr).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS (2026 GKS-G Guidelines):** Monthly allowance **1,380,000 KRW** (degree program) / 1,200,000 KRW (Korean-language year); tuition up to **5,000,000 KRW per semester** (admission fee covered by the university, not NIIED); Korean language training fee 5,200,000 KRW; economy airfare (not for scholars selected in China/Czech Republic). A settlement allowance and Korean Proficiency Grant are referenced but **no KRW figure is published in the 2026 guideline** (2026 GKS-G Application Guidelines via studyinkorea.go.kr). Funds study **only in Korea**.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Graduate applicants under 40 (born after 1 Sep 1986 for 2026); CGPA ≥ 80% (or ~2.64/4.0); bachelor's required for master's; TOPIK 5–6 holders skip the language year (https://www.topikguide.com/global-korea-scholarship-gks-graduate/). Threshold plus comparative.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Choose ONE track — **Embassy Track** (apply via the Korean embassy; embassy 1st round, NIIED 2nd round, then universities review for admission) or **University Track** (apply via a NIIED-designated university). Joint multi-body selection; scholars must secure university admission (https://graduate2.korea.ac.kr/scholarship/gkscholarships.html).
5. **TIMELINE (2026):** Embassy Track online application opened ~12 Feb 2026; final scholar announcement late June 2026 (https://www.topikguide.com/global-korea-scholarship-gks-graduate/; 2026 GKS-G Guidelines).
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Overall award count published (2,000 graduate scholars for 2026); applications-received not published. Partial.
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **studyinkorea.go.kr** (Plan your studies → Scholarships → GKS Notice) is the official enumerating portal, with niied.go.kr. Primary crawler seed.

### 5. China — Chinese Government Scholarship (CGS) / China Scholarship Council (CSC)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Non-Chinese citizens in good health from any country; master's applicants generally under 35 (https://opencampushub.com/chinese-government-csc-scholarship — agency source). No published fixed per-country quota, though Type A is embassy-allocated.
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Tuition waiver; free/subsidized accommodation; monthly stipend of **3,000 RMB (master's)** / 3,500 RMB (doctoral); comprehensive medical insurance; one-off inter-city travel and settlement subsidies (https://greatyop.com/chinese-government-scholarship-program-csc/ — agency source). Funds study **only in China**.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Bachelor's for master's; language proof (HSK for Chinese-taught; IELTS 6.0+/TOEFL 80+ for English-taught, or a medium-of-instruction certificate). The CSCA test is required for undergraduate applicants only — **not** master's/PhD — from 2026/2027 (https://migaku.com/blog/language-fun/csc-scholarship-guide-studying-in-china-on-a-full-government-ride — agency source). Threshold plus comparative.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Type A (via Chinese embassy/consulate — bilateral); Type B (directly via a Chinese university); Type C (other programs). Applicants register in the CSC Information System and typically need a pre-admission letter from the university. May require both embassy/university and CSC approval.
5. **TIMELINE:** Country-specific deadlines mostly February–late April 2026 for the 2026/2027 intake (https://migaku.com/blog/language-fun/csc-scholarship-guide-studying-in-china-on-a-full-government-ride — agency source). **Decentralized dates.**
6. **SELECTIVITY:** The official CSC portal administers CGS on behalf of the Ministry of Education across 289 designated Chinese universities but does **not publish an annual award total** (China Scholarship Council / campuschina.org). The frequently-cited "~30,000 scholarships/year to 180+ countries" figure is **agency-sourced only** (https://www.study-abroad.org/blog/cn-scholarships-guide/ — flagged) and should not be treated as official.
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** The official CSC portal **campuschina.org** (CSC Information System) is the enumerating index. Flag: much of the detail above is from agency/blog sources rather than campuschina.org directly; the crawler should target the official portal.

### 6. UK — Chevening Scholarships
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Citizens of Chevening-eligible countries/territories (over 160); the eligible-country list is on chevening.org. Must agree to return home for two years after study (https://www.gov.uk/government/news/applications-open-for-2026-to-2027-chevening-scholarships-to-study-in-the-uk).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Full tuition, monthly stipend, return airfare, arrival allowance, homeward departure allowance, one visa-application cost, and a travel grant for Chevening events (https://scholarshiproar.com/chevening-scholarship/ — agency source, corroborated by gov.uk). Funds a one-year master's at **any UK university** — portable within the UK, student chooses the university.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Undergraduate degree qualifying for UK master's entry; **2,800 hours** (≈two years) of work experience; no minimum GPA specified; Chevening sets no English test, but the chosen university's requirements apply (https://scholarshiproar.com/chevening-scholarship/). Selection is comparative (leadership, networking, career plan, study choice).
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Direct online student application; successful candidates must submit at least one **unconditional UK university offer** by a stated July deadline (9 July 2026 for the 2026/27 cycle) (https://scholarshipsinstitute.com/chevening-scholarship-2027-application/ — agency source).
5. **TIMELINE (2027/28 cycle):** Applications opened 4 August 2026; deadline **6 October 2026 at 11:00 UTC**; reading/assessment Oct 2026–Jan 2027; shortlist mid-Feb 2027; interviews Mar–Apr 2027; results from mid-June 2027; unconditional offer by 8 July 2027 (https://www.chevening.org/scholarships/application-timeline/; https://www.careershelpdesk.com/chevening-scholarship/).
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Chevening awarded **1,545 scholarships for the 2023 academic year** to leaders from over 160 countries; the British Council/Study UK states "more than 1,000 scholarships on offer each year" with nearly 60,000 alumni since 1983. Agency estimates put the acceptance rate at ~2–3%, but Chevening does not publish a live selection-rate dashboard (https://study-uk.britishcouncil.org/scholarships-funding/commonwealth-scholarships; https://www.globaladmissions.com/scholarship/chevening-scholarship — acceptance rate flagged as agency-sourced).
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **chevening.org** (and the gov.uk announcement pages) are the official enumerating sources. Primary crawler seed.

### 7. UK — Commonwealth Master's Scholarships (CSC-UK / FCDO)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Citizens/refugees/British Protected Persons permanently resident in an eligible **low- and middle-income Commonwealth** country; eligible-country list on the CSC website (cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk). Must be unable to afford UK study without the scholarship (https://www.lboro.ac.uk/study/postgraduate/fees-funding/masters-funding/commonwealth-masters-scholarships/).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** All tuition and examination fees; return airfare; a living stipend (£1,378/month, or £1,690/month in the London metropolitan area); study travel grant; warm-clothing allowance; possible family allowances (https://www.scholars4dev.com/4900/commonwealth-scholarships-for-developing-commonwealth-countries/). Funds a full-time master's at a **UK university with a CSC part-funding agreement** — student-chosen among partner universities.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** By September of entry, a first degree of at least upper second-class (2:1) honours, or a 2:2 plus a relevant postgraduate qualification; CSC would not normally fund a second UK master's (https://www.qmul.ac.uk/scholarships/items/commonwealth-masters-scholarships--1.html). Threshold plus comparative.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** No direct applications — must be nominated by a **national nominating agency** (main route), selected NGOs/charities, or (for some awards) universities; applicants also submit via the CSC Electronic Application System (EAS) (https://www.scholars4dev.com/4900/commonwealth-scholarships-for-developing-commonwealth-countries/). Nomination model.
5. **TIMELINE (2026/27):** Applications opened ~2 September 2025; the 2026/27 round has closed; outcomes expected by July 2026; study begins Sept/Oct 2026 (https://registryservices.ed.ac.uk/student-funding/postgraduate/international/other-funding/commonwealth; https://www.scholarshipregion.com/commonwealth-masters-scholarship/). Nominating bodies set their own earlier deadlines.
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Not published on the CSC scheme summary. **Not published.**
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk** is the official enumerating index (lists Master's, PhD, Split-site, Professional Fellowships, with eligible-country subpages); Study UK/British Council also lists it. Primary crawler seed.

### 8. Turkey — Türkiye Bursları (Türkiye Scholarships)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Citizens of all countries except Turkey (Turkish citizens and former citizens excluded) (https://www.globaladmissions.com/scholarship/turkiye-burslari). Country-specific quotas exist but are **not publicly announced** (https://truescho.com/en/blog/turkiye-burslari-2026 — agency source, flagged).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Full tuition, monthly stipend (master's ~₺6,500), free accommodation, health insurance, a one-year Turkish preparatory course, one-off round-trip flight, plus university placement (https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/). Funds study **only in Turkey**.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Master's applicants need ≥75% prior academic average (90% for medical sciences); age limits per level; no language certificate required at application (one-year Turkish prep for all) (https://www.globaladmissions.com/scholarship/turkiye-burslari). Threshold plus comparative/holistic.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Direct online student application via the TBBS portal; **no prior admission letter needed** — YTB places the scholar into a university/department from up to 12 listed preferences (https://www.turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/applysteps).
5. **TIMELINE (2026):** Applications **10 January – 20 February 2026** (single period); interviews for shortlisted candidates; results typically June/July (https://turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/announcements/turkiye-scholarships-2026-applications-121).
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Now officially quantified by YTB: applications rose from ~42,000 in 2012 to **121,830 from 170 countries in 2024**, and YTB announced a **record of nearly 200,000 applications in 2026** (YTB / ytb.gov.tr). YTB states that **approximately 5,000 international students are given scholarships every year** — implying a low single-digit acceptance rate. (The often-quoted 165,500-applications figure was stated by YTB President Abdullah Eren; note press attributions to "Nuri Ersoy" are inaccurate.)
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **turkiyeburslari.gov.tr** is the official enumerating portal (scholarship-programs, criteria, application-steps pages); the parent body ytb.gov.tr publishes statistics. Primary crawler seed.

### 9. Hungary — Stipendium Hungaricum
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Applicants from Hungary's 90+ partner countries under bilateral educational-cooperation agreements; eligible study fields are country-specific and listed in the Call for Applications (https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BA_MA_OTM_Call_for_Applications_2026_27.pdf). Applicants must also apply through their home country's designated Sending Partner (https://catchthatscholarship.com/scholarships/hungary/how-to-apply-to-stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship/ — agency source).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Full tuition waiver; monthly stipend (HUF 43,700 for BA/MA/non-degree; higher for doctoral); free/subsidized dormitory or housing contribution; medical insurance (https://globalscholarships.com/external/stipendium-hungaricum/ — agency source). Funds study **only in Hungary** at ~30 institutions / ~900 foreign-language programmes.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Level-appropriate prior degree; language proficiency per programme (mostly English); specific requirements vary by Sending Partner and programme. Universities conduct entrance exams. Comparative, with nomination quotas per country.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Dual submission — online application to Tempus Public Foundation AND to the home-country **Sending Partner**, which submits nomination lists; universities then conduct entrance examinations (https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/BA_MA_OTM_Call_for_Applications_2026_27.pdf). Joint government-nomination + university-selection model.
5. **TIMELINE (2026/27):** Deadline **15 January 2026, 14:00 CET**; technical check Jan 2026; Sending Partner nominations Feb 2026; university entrance exams Mar–May 2026; final results late June/early July 2026; studies begin Sept 2026; next cycle (2027/28) opens ~November 2026 (https://stipendiumhungaricum.hu/news/application-is-on-for-the-2026-2027-stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship/; https://truescho.com/en/blog/stipendium-hungaricum-scholarship-2026 — agency source).
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Not published on the scheme site. **Not published.**
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **stipendiumhungaricum.hu** (news + Call for Applications PDFs + apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu) is the official enumerating index. Primary crawler seed.

### 10. Azerbaijan — Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant Program
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Citizens of eligible countries — members of the Organization of Islamic Cooperation, the Non-Aligned Movement, Small Island Developing States, and countries with shared cultural/ethnic/historical ties to Azerbaijan; Azerbaijani citizens excluded. Annually 100 students total (all levels) (https://studyinazerbaijan.edu.az/H.Aliyev_IEG_CALL_18.04.pdf).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Tuition; international flights to/from Baku annually; monthly allowance of 800 AZN (~US$470, excl. July/August); yearly medical-insurance allowance of 200 AZN; visa and residence permits (https://www.mastere.tn/en/azerbaijan-government-scholarships-for-international-students/). Funds study **only in Azerbaijan**. An official list of 435 eligible study programmes is published.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Minimum general GPA of 70/100 (or equivalent); language proficiency (IELTS/TOEFL or prior study in English/Russian/Turkish/Azerbaijani) (https://www.mastere.tn/en/azerbaijan-government-scholarships-for-international-students/ — agency source, flagged). Threshold plus comparative.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Requires **nomination by the applicant's national government**, plus an online application through **SIACAS** (Study in Azerbaijan Centralized Admission Service). Jointly administered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Science and Education (https://studyinazerbaijan.edu.az/financial-support). Government-nomination model.
5. **TIMELINE (2025/26 reference):** Government nomination deadline ~15 May; final applications ~1–15 June (https://www.mastere.tn/en/azerbaijan-government-scholarships-for-international-students/ — agency source). The grant runs annually for 5 years from 2026/27 (https://studyinazerbaijan.edu.az/H.Aliyev_IEG_CALL_18.04.pdf).
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Fixed **100 awards/year** published; applications-received not published (https://studyinazerbaijan.edu.az/H.Aliyev_IEG_CALL_18.04.pdf).
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** **studyinazerbaijan.edu.az** (financial-support and scholarships pages, plus the Call PDF) is the official enumerating portal; edu.gov.az (Ministry) is the parent body. Primary crawler seed.

### 11. Georgia — national inbound schemes (STRUCTURAL MISMATCH)
1–6. **Georgia has NO open national inbound scholarship for international master's students.** The **International Education Center (IEC, iec.gov.ge)** funds only **outbound** Georgian citizens studying abroad (https://educationusa.state.gov/scholarships/georgian-government-scholarship-fund-administered-international-education-center). The Ministry of Education (mes.gov.ge) lists a formally-named "State program of state grants for foreign citizens" (Decree №786) and a "State master's grant within the framework of a unique program for a foreign citizen," but the general State educational/master's grant is legally **restricted to Georgian citizens and neutral-ID holders** (https://www.stateless.ge/en/education), and the foreign-citizen category is a narrow special/bilateral allocation capped at **2,250 GEL/year** (EU Eurydice: https://eurydice.eacea.ec.europa.eu/eurypedia/georgia/higher-education-funding) — a tuition-offset voucher, not a stipend package, and not an open competition. Foreigners may enrol without the Unified National Exams (Decree №224/N, https://mes.gov.ge/content.php?id=1129&lang=eng), but that is an admission mechanism, not funding. Inbound international students therefore rely on **individual-university scholarships/tuition waivers**. As of 2026, Georgia is reportedly reducing state funding and admission of foreign students at public universities (news-sourced, minister Givi Mikanadze — should be corroborated against an official MES release).
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** For the model, Georgia should be flagged as "no national inbound scheme." The relevant official index pages are **mes.gov.ge** (state grant programs, https://mes.gov.ge/content.php?id=253&lang=eng) and **iec.gov.ge** (confirming outbound-only). Any inbound funding must be crawled at the university level, not a national portal.

### 12. Russia — Government Quota (Rossotrudnichestvo / education-in-russia)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Foreign citizens (and compatriots living abroad); non-Russian citizens only (https://en.misis.ru/applicants/scholarships/quota/). The quota is split among countries by bilateral agreement — reported at ~15,000 places/year (agency reporting; https://www.study-abroad.org/blog/rua-scholarships-guide/ — flagged).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Full tuition from the federal budget; a monthly stipend regardless of academic performance (nominal, ~2,000–4,500 RUB); dormitory place at low cost (https://en.misis.ru/applicants/scholarships/quota/). Airfare generally NOT included. Funds study **only in Russia**.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Master's requires a bachelor's / 16 years of education; medical certificates (incl. HIV/hepatitis/TB) required; entrance testing/olympiads may apply (https://en.hspu.org/news/3073/). Comparative selection managed abroad.
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Apply via **Rossotrudnichestvo** representatives / Russian diplomatic missions in the applicant's own country, through the education-in-russia portal; some universities (e.g., St Petersburg State) run Open International Olympiads as an entry route (https://english.spbu.ru/news-events/news/admission-russian-government-scholarship-opens-international-applicants). Home-country-office administration.
5. **TIMELINE (2025/26 reference):** Registration ran ~7 October 2024 – 15 January 2025 for 2025/26 (https://en.hspu.org/news/3073/); dates vary by country office. **Mismatch: decentralized, country-specific dates, no single global calendar.**
6. **SELECTIVITY:** ~15,000 places/year reported; official applications-received/rate not published. Flagged as agency-sourced.
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** The official **education-in-russia.com** (Rossotrudnichestvo) portal is the enumerating index, supplemented by Russian embassy pages and university sites. Primary crawler seed, but note selection is decentralized to embassies.

### 13. EU — Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters (EMJM)
1. **WHO CAN APPLY:** Students of any nationality worldwide, holding (or about to hold) a bachelor's; each consortium sets its own criteria. A mobility rule applies: students must study in at least two countries. The programme-vs-partner-country distinction was removed for masters selected from 2021 — all students get the same scholarship (https://www.eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships_en).
2. **WHAT IT FUNDS:** Scholarship covers participation costs (tuition/fees), a monthly living allowance, and travel/installation contributions. The standard EMJM allowance is **€1,400/month for up to 24 months (max €33,600 per student)**, confirmed against Erasmus+ programme rules and consortium pages (https://www.gla.ac.uk/postgraduate/erasmusmundus/edumah). Note some individual consortia set lower figures (e.g., EMHRPP lists €1,000/month). **Consortium-delivered, multi-country by design** — the opposite of single-country schemes.
3. **THE ACADEMIC BAR:** Bachelor's degree (or final-year, graduating before start); language and academic requirements set per consortium (https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters). Comparative — "best-ranked applicants worldwide."
4. **HOW IT IS INITIATED:** Direct application to the **consortium running the chosen programme** (not to the EU); up to three programmes per cycle (https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters).
5. **TIMELINE:** Decentralized — each programme sets its own deadline; most applications open Oct–Dec and close Dec–Feb for the following academic year (https://erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu/opportunities/individuals/students/erasmus-mundus-joint-masters). **Mismatch: no single scheme-wide deadline.**
6. **SELECTIVITY:** Per-programme scholarship quotas vary — some fund only 2–3 international scholars per intake, others around 15 (e.g., the Erasmus Mundus Joint Master in Imaging states it "offers around 15 scholarships per year," emjm-imaging.eu). No central applications/awards rate is published. **Not published centrally.**
7. **DISCOVERY PATH:** The official **EMJM catalogue** on the Erasmus+ / EACEA site (erasmus-plus.ec.europa.eu → Erasmus Mundus Joint Masters catalogue; eacea.ec.europa.eu/scholarships_en) is the enumerating index — searchable by field/country, each entry linking to the consortium site. Primary crawler seed for the multi-country archetype.

## Recommendations
1. **Model three initiation archetypes as a first-class, multi-valued field**, since it drives everything downstream: (a) direct student application (Türkiye, Chevening, CSC Type A, GKS Embassy, EMJM), (b) institution/consortium nomination (Eiffel, DAAD EPOS, MEXT University Recommendation, Commonwealth, Stipendium Sending Partner), (c) home-government nomination (Azerbaijan, Russia). A scheme can occupy more than one archetype (GKS, MEXT), so make it multi-valued rather than an enum.
2. **Add a "portability" enum** — {funder-country-only, portable-within-funder-country (student chooses university: Chevening, Commonwealth), multi-country-consortium (EMJM)}. Nine of twelve are funder-country-only.
3. **Treat "timeline" as optionally null / decentralized.** DAAD, CSC, MEXT (country-specific), Russia, and EMJM have no single global deadline; hard-code a "decentralized" flag rather than forcing a date. Only Eiffel, GKS, Türkiye, Stipendium, Chevening and Commonwealth have clean scheme-wide calendars.
4. **Treat "selectivity" as usually null but capture it where official.** Only Azerbaijan (100 awards), GKS (2,000 graduate), Türkiye (~5,000 awards vs 121,830 applications in 2024 / ~200,000 in 2026, per YTB), and Chevening (1,545 for 2023, per Chevening) give official award counts; almost none publish an official application-to-award rate, and the CSC "30,000" figure is agency-sourced only.
5. **Prioritize the national index portals as crawler seeds** (see below), not individual scheme pages — these are stable and enumerate multiple schemes.
6. **Flag Georgia as a negative case** in the model to validate that the schema can represent "country has no national inbound scheme," and Russia/DAAD/EMJM/MEXT/CSC as "decentralized deadline" cases.

**Benchmarks that would change these recommendations:** if the CSC portal begins publishing an official annual award total, promote its selectivity from null/flagged to populated; if Georgia launches an open inbound scheme (watch mes.gov.ge), reclassify it from negative case; if MEXT/CSC publish a single global calendar, remove their decentralized flags; if any scheme moves selection from embassies/nominating bodies to a central portal, update its initiation archetype.

## Caveats
- **Source quality:** Official scheme/ministry URLs are used wherever possible (DAAD, Campus France, studyinjapan, studyinkorea/NIIED, chevening.org, cscuk.fcdo.gov.uk, turkiyeburslari.gov.tr/ytb.gov.tr, stipendiumhungaricum.hu, studyinazerbaijan.edu.az, europa.eu/EACEA, misis.ru/spbu.ru, mes.gov.ge, Eurydice). Several data points — CSC stipend/deadlines/award total, Azerbaijan GPA/dates, Erasmus Mundus per-consortium figures, and some Chevening benefit details — were available only via agency/blog aggregators and are **explicitly flagged**; the crawler should re-fetch these from the official portals before relying on them.
- **GKS settlement allowance and degree-completion grant:** the 2026 GKS-G guideline references these but publishes **no KRW figure**; do not backfill prior-year numbers.
- **Türkiye selectivity attribution:** the widely-circulated 165,500-applications figure was stated by YTB President Abdullah Eren; some press wrongly attributes it to minister Nuri Ersoy. YTB's own published series (121,830 in 2024; nearly 200,000 in 2026; ~5,000 awards/year) is the authoritative source.
- **Russia:** administration and the education-in-russia portal reflect the current published system; geopolitical conditions may affect availability and payment mechanics.
- **Georgia 2026 policy shift** (phase-out of foreign-student funding/admission at public universities) is news-sourced and should be corroborated against an official MES release.
- This is a **validation sample, not an exhaustive census**; comparable national/state schemes not covered here include Italy's DSU / Invest Your Talent, the Netherlands' Orange Knowledge / Holland Scholarship, Sweden's Swedish Institute scholarships, Switzerland's Government Excellence Scholarships, and Taiwan's MOE Scholarship — each with its own official index portal that would extend the crawler seed list.
