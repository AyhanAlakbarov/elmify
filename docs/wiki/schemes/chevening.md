# Chevening

See [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Armenia](../countries/am.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- Chevening requires a commitment to return to the home country for at least two years and at least two years' post-undergraduate work experience equivalent to 2,800 hours. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §3]
- No per-country allocation or quota is published; the application form lists "South Caucasus" beside Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia, evidence of regional award structure but not a quota. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §3]
- Other unmodelled conditions: no prior UK-government-funded study, not resident in the UK, an unconditional offer from an eligible course by the deadline, and an employment-relationship exclusion. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §3]
- The 2,800-hour unit may need an hours unit in `comparable_value` or a recorded conversion. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §4]
- Run 1 found Chevening-TEV, a Türkiye-specific track, which the general exclusion would have missed. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §8]
- The inbound synthesis lists Chevening as `DIRECT` with a two-year return obligation for both Azerbaijani and Turkish citizens, run A only. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- The 1,400 figure (FCDO CP 981, run A) was not re-checked. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §4]
- Its offer-by-date pattern is real: an unconditional UK offer by 9 July 2026 for 2026/27 and 8 July 2027 for 2027/28; 1,545 awards were made for 2023. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §4]
- Precedence ("hold an offer by 8 July") has no result in the portfolio literature, so staging patterns are enumerated ([portfolio theory](../topics/portfolio-theory.md), [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md)). [src: 2026-08-27-portfolio-theory-synthesis.md §3]
- Both corridor runs agree it can substitute for visa financial proof ([United Kingdom](../countries/gb.md)). [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
