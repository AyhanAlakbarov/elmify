# Islamic Development Bank (IsDB) scholarships

See [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- The nationality-keyed synthesis lists IsDB as `DIRECT` and full-cost for 7 of 9 priority countries (not Georgia, Armenia). [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §1]
- The M.Sc. track is restricted to Least Developed Member Countries, and the earlier synthesis left open which of ours qualify. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §9]
- Corrected: the IsDB-ISFD booklet's list of eligible LDMCs names 21 countries and none of the nine, so membership does not imply LDMC status and IsDB is not a parallel full-cost route for Turkish citizens. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
- `isdb.org/scholarships` says "20" LDMCs while the booklet lists 21; do not encode a count of 20. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
- The LDMC scheme has an age cap of 24, usually studies in the student's own country, and placement abroad only in Malaysia, Morocco or Türkiye. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
- Separately, the YTB-IsDB joint programme funds other nationalities into Türkiye, not Turkish citizens out. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
