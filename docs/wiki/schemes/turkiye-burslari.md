# Türkiye Bursları

Türkiye's inbound government scholarship. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md] See [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md), [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md).

- It is the most accessible funding in the corridor sample: 75% bar, no language certificate, direct application, full package. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Turkey has no post-study work route, so the scholarship is a good floor and poor for mobility. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Both runs agree a scholarship can substitute for visa financial proof. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- It published 121,830 applications from 170 countries in 2024 against about 5,000 awards a year. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §4]
- It places students into Turkish-taught programmes with a mandatory Turkish year even for English-track placees. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §8]
- Its participating-university list is reachable only through a dynamic search tool populated during the application window. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4]
- Turkish citizens are ineligible; it is `DIRECT` and full-cost for Azerbaijanis. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
