# Fulbright Foreign Student Program

Nine national competitions for master's study in the US. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1] Country terms: [Armenia](../countries/am.md), [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [Kyrgyzstan](../countries/kg.md), [Tajikistan](../countries/tj.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Turkmenistan](../countries/tm.md), [Uzbekistan](../countries/uz.md). See [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md) and [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md).

- The programme "operates in more than 160 countries" and awards about 4,000 grants a year; applicants apply via the commission or embassy in their home country and must reside in the country of nomination. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §8]
- It is nationality-keyed by construction: about 160 national competitions with their own terms, not one global competition. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §8]
- `us.fulbrightonline.org` pages describe the US Student Program (Americans going abroad), the wrong programme for our population. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md]
- All nine competitions are master's grants (none research-only or FLTA-only); cycle 2027-28 unless noted. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1]
- Four of nine state a two-year post-graduation experience requirement (Azerbaijan, Kazakhstan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan); Kyrgyzstan states none, and Georgia, Armenia, Tajikistan do not state one in page text, which is not evidence of absence. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §3]
- Every award is bonded by visa law: INA §212(e) requires J-1 grantees to return home for at least two years, a destination-visa obligation rather than a funder one. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §2]
- The Türkiye commission states there is no mandatory service but a J-1 return-and-stay obligation of two years. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §5]
- Türkiye's award is capped at USD 60,000 per academic year, with renewal of the second year conditional on first-year performance. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §5]
- Nearly all use the portal `apply.iie.org/ffsp2027` and IIE places nominees, so the destination is unknown at application time. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §4]
- The inbound synthesis lists Fulbright among the schemes classed `NOMINATED`, "Runs A/B, partly verified". [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- Fulbright was missed by four runs because the prompts were organised by funder type; the inbound sweep's US row must capture the commission structure. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §8]
- Still open: Armenia's current cycle, Georgia and Tajikistan experience statements, Tajikistan's deadline. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §6]
