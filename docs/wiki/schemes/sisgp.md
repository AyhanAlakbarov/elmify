# SISGP (Swedish Institute Scholarship for Global Professionals)

See [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Armenia](../countries/am.md), [Uzbekistan](../countries/uz.md).

- It covers 34 countries including Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Indonesia, Uzbekistan and Vietnam, not Türkiye, Kazakhstan, India or Pakistan. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- It does not require residence at application and has no country quotas; a SEK 10,000 travel grant applies to Armenia, Azerbaijan and Georgia; the key is `CITIZENSHIP` (run B said `REGION`). [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- Route is `HYBRID` (admission, then SI), full cost, with work experience required. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- Among our nine, the Swedish Institute list is Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Uzbekistan only. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
