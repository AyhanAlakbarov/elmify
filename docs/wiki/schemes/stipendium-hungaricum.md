# Stipendium Hungaricum

Hungary's scholarship; the route depends on the sending state. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1] See [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Uzbekistan](../countries/uz.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Kyrgyzstan](../countries/kg.md), [Turkmenistan](../countries/tm.md), [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md), [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md).

- In 2025, 80,000 applications were received and more than 4,500 scholarships awarded; the 2026/27 call deadline was 15 Jan 2026. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- The route is `NOMINATED` for Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kyrgyzstan and Turkmenistan, and `DIRECT` for Uzbekistan on the Uzbek ministry's written instruction: the same scheme and cycle resolve differently by nationality. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Türkiye is `NOMINATED` via MEB; the scheme's key is `CITIZENSHIP`, not `REGION`. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §2]
- Georgia and Kyrgyzstan require dual registration. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Three Azerbaijani numbers are not one number: up to 200 a year (MoU ceiling), 104 awards of which 80 master's (2026/27), 129 realised intake in Hungary (2024-25). [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §2]
- Hungary publishes no per-country figure; the sending side does (Uzbekistan up to 200, Georgia up to 80). [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §4]
- Hungary lists the sending partner for all nine countries but not quotas or routes. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Hungary's historical Türkiye sub-quota was 150 for 2021 (40 BA, 65 MA, 40 PhD, 5 short-term). [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §4]
- Run B's 2023/24 and 2024/25 calls were stale. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §3]
- Instrument view: a Sending Partner nominates and both bodies select; it covers 90+ partner countries, publishes a clean calendar and no applicant counts ([Hungary](../countries/hu.md), [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md)). [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §4]
- Both corridor runs agree it can substitute for visa financial proof. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
