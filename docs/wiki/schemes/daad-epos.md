- The 2027/2028 course list lets applicants pick Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, India, Indonesia, Pakistan, Vietnam or Uzbekistan; Türkiye is not listed. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- "Country" means the country where the applicant lives and applies from, so the key is residence, not citizenship; run A's exclusion of AZ/GE/AM/KZ was wrong. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- The inbound table lists it as `DIRECT` to the course, full, with two years' work experience. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- A different scheme, DAAD In-Country/In-Region, excludes the South Caucasus and Central Asia. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
- Instrument view: the applicant applies to the university and DAAD selects; DAAD has no single global deadline, and eligibility is an explicit developing-country list ([instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md)).
