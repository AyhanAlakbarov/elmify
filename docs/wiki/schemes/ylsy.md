# YLSY (Türkiye's 1416 scholarships)

See [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [outbound launch](../topics/outbound-launch.md).

- It is open ("1416 Bursları"): 811 places for 2025 against 668 for 2024; service is twice the funded period to a named institution, secured by a notarised undertaking and two joint-and-several guarantors, with repayment with interest on default. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §1]
- Applications ran 19-30 December 2025, closing 17:00 on the 30th. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- Route: run B (`DIRECT`) is right over run A (`NOMINATED`); entry is open but placement binds the destination before the student holds a foreign offer. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §3]
- It is cited as a funder-imposed bond alongside ADB-JSP and Bolashak. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- Interest on default comes from Law 1416 art. 21 as amended by Law 7326; post-return duties include a three-month report and a half-stipend holding allowance. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]
- The handbook defines a "partially state-funded" status for holders of another non-repayable award or assistantship; medical withdrawal is forgiven. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- Open: the master's-versus-doctoral split per cycle, and how funded language months count toward service. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §7]
