# Aga Khan Foundation International Scholarship Programme (AKF ISP)

See [Kyrgyzstan](../countries/kg.md), [Tajikistan](../countries/tj.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- It was surfaced by the "who has a reason to pay" axis and left uncharacterised. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §6] [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §9]
- It is available in the Kyrgyz Republic and Tajikistan among our nine; the page also bars study in the United Kingdom or Russia. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §2]
- Awards are half grant and half loan, repayable at 50 percent with a service charge, with a guarantor required to co-sign. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §2]
- Other conditions: priority to master's, must be entering the first year, "resource of last resort", preference (not a cap) for under-30s. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §2]
- Every URL ending `international-scholarship-programme` serves an "OLD" page, a broken CMS entry, not a retired programme. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §2]
- `FundingBenefit` has no concept of repayable funding, so a half-loan recorded as coverage would overstate what the student has. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §4]
