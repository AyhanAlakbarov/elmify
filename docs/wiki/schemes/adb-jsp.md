# ADB-Japan Scholarship Program (ADB-JSP)

See [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- It is open to eight of the nine priority countries (Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan), not Türkiye, which is not an ADB borrowing member. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- Conditions run 1 omitted: at least two years' full-time professional experience after the first degree, age under 35, family income up to USD 50,000 and individual income up to USD 25,000, no dual citizenship with a developed country, and not already in a graduate programme. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- It carries a signed commitment to return home for at least two years, a bonded award. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- Route: no separate application to ADB; the student applies to the institution, which ranks and forwards, so it is practically self-initiated. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §4]
- It is one of several schemes with a professional-experience gate (JJ/WBGSP, Fulbright Uzbekistan also). [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §8]
- It is cited as a funder-imposed return obligation alongside Bolashak and YLSY. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §5] [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §6]
