# GKS (Global Korea Scholarship, graduate)

2026 NIIED graduate guideline quotas for the nine launch countries. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] Country pages: [Armenia](../countries/am.md), [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [Kyrgyzstan](../countries/kg.md), [Tajikistan](../countries/tj.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Turkmenistan](../countries/tm.md), [Uzbekistan](../countries/uz.md). See also [South Korea](../countries/kr.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- The earlier compiled figures were checked against the primary guideline (read as a mirror on the Korean Education Centre in the UK) and every figure checked out. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §1]
- Embassy Track General: 776 scholars from 143 countries or regions; Armenia 2, Azerbaijan 5, Georgia 4, Kazakhstan 13, Kyrgyzstan 9, Tajikistan 5, Turkmenistan 7, Türkiye 11, Uzbekistan 13 (nine-country total 69). [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2]
- The quota is a ceiling: a footnote lets the embassy choose not to recommend a candidate. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2]
- University Track: General 160 scholars from 54 countries, R-GKS 480 from 56. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3]
- General / R-GKS per country: Armenia 1/1, Azerbaijan 2/1, Georgia 1/1, Kazakhstan 5/18, Kyrgyzstan 7/3, Tajikistan 2/2, Turkmenistan —/3, Türkiye 2/5, Uzbekistan 6/6; the Turkmenistan General dash means R-GKS only, not zero. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3]
- Route split: Embassy Track is `NOMINATED`, University Track is `DIRECT`. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §3]
- Overseas Korean/Adoptee track: 24 scholars from 8 countries, including Kazakhstan 7, Kyrgyzstan 3, Uzbekistan 6. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §4]
- Those 24 are additional to the 776 (Embassy subtotal 800; University 1,200; total 2,000). [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §5]
- An Embassy applicant must apply to only one of General and Overseas Korean/Adoptee, so the quotas do not add; an applicant who failed round 1 may re-apply via a University Track. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §5]
- Descent also earns an evaluation preference in Embassy General. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §5]
- Proof of descent is document 16; the guideline does not say how the ascendant's Korean-ness is established, and dual citizenship of applicant or parent disqualifies. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §5]
- The synthesis calls University Track the first `DIRECT` scheme with a published per-country quota; the quota's year (2026) is to be recorded. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §7]
- Embassy and university tracks differ in quota, deadline and selection body, so a scheme is a set of tracks, and GKS's per-nationality quota is a nationality-level denominator. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §4]
- The 2026 graduate quota is 2,000, published with no applicant count; final lists by candidate number and nationality make per-nationality awards verifiable afterwards. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §4]
- NIIED pays tuition directly to the university, so GKS scholars register against a covered bill ([corridor mechanics round 2](../topics/corridor-mechanics-round2.md)). [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §1]
