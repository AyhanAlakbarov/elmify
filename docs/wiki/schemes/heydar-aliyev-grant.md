# Heydar Aliyev International Education Grant (inbound)

Azerbaijan's inbound grant. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] See [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md).

- Terms: MFA and Ministry of Science and Education; 100 grants a year for 5 years from 2026-27; open to OIC, NAM, SIDS and countries with common ties; includes Master's; covers tuition, flights, allowance, insurance and visa. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- Route is `NOMINATED` (run B's `DIRECT` is wrong): national-authority nomination then registration on SIACAS; Pakistan's HEC publishes 3 slots, a sending-side quota. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §2]
- Start-year conflict: the official page says 2026-27; SESRIC announced a 2025-26 round; run B cites an MFA page saying 2024-25. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- Türkiye qualifies as an OIC member and the route is `NOMINATED` by Turkish authorities. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- It is separate from the outbound State Programme, [HTP](htp.md) and a domestic one-off Heydar Aliyev award (3,000 AZN to 100 bachelor students). [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]
- Run 4 of the follow-up plan covers its start year and per-country slots. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §6]

Superseded: the four regional deep-research runs were replaced by a census of every country followed by two-model batches of 10-12 countries, and the Heydar Aliyev grant became known-answer check 11 — see [Inbound government search design](../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-inbound-government-search-design.md).
