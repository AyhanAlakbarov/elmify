# Outbound state provision, Azerbaijan and Türkiye

What Azerbaijan and Türkiye pay for their own citizens to study abroad. Pages: [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [YLSY](../schemes/ylsy.md), [HTP](../schemes/htp.md), [Heydar Aliyev grant](../schemes/heydar-aliyev-grant.md).

- Both programmes are live and bonded: Azerbaijan 5 years' service (employment contract, all costs repayable), Türkiye twice the funded period with a notarised undertaking and two guarantors, repayment with interest. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §1]
- Azerbaijan's 2026/2027 list covers 231 institutions in 33 countries and 4,121 programmes; run B's twenty-institution table was one field's section, not the list. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- Because it funds only an admission the student already holds, the Azerbaijani programme cannot be a portfolio floor. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §2]
- Quota composition from 2025: at most 25% bachelor's and 20% doctoral, so the 500 ceiling is majority master's. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]
- Co-holding another scholarship: permitted in both. Türkiye defines "Partially State-Funded Student"; Azerbaijan's contract has no exclusivity clause. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- Return: Azerbaijan requires return within 2 months and 5 years' work (§3.3.18), repayment on any inability to continue "irrespective of the reason"; Türkiye forgives medical withdrawal. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- Cabinet decisions: 218 (1 June 2022) approves the financing rule and 217 the selection criteria, so run A's citation of 218 for the return obligation is usable. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- The programme period reads "2022-2028" on directly retrieved pages; "2022-2026" survives as a stale string. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §3]
- Türkiye: YLSY route is `DIRECT` (run B) with binding placement. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §3]
- TÜBİTAK funds no outbound master's; TCMB funds its own staff (`EMPLOYER_ONLY`); YÖK sends research assistants. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]
- SOFAZ's financial series (AZN 92.729m for 2026, 13.2% executed by H1) is the likeliest source of per-cycle award counts and neither run reported it. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §2]
- Co-holding via a destination scholarship was first judged unpublished, then closed. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §7]
