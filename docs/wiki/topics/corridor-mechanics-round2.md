## Korea's tuition prepayment
- A Ministry of Education directive states the Certificate of Admission for the D-2 visa is issued only to those who have paid tuition in full after acceptance: the sequence is national, the amount is per university and college. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §1]
- The directive's wording is second-hand: the quoted clauses come from universities' admission notices, so pull it from `moe.go.kr` before citing it as primary. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §6]
- A self-funded student commits a full semester, typically ₩6-9m, before the visa document exists, so waiting for a scholarship is not free when it lands after the registration deadline. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §1]
- That is a cash-flow risk with a refund path, not a forfeiture; the post-payment treatment (감면고지 before billing, cash disbursement after) is published at SKKU only. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §3]
- Run 1's finding that 입학금 is largely folded into tuition is preferred, so no separate entrance-fee figure is encoded; the ban covers undergraduate entry only. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §4]
- GKS is the benign case: NIIED pays the university directly. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §1]

## The confirmed negative
- No university in Japan, Korea or China publishes a matched international applicant and admitted count for master's programmes. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §2]
- e-Stat, KEDI/대학알리미 and moe.gov.cn are enrolment stock series, the wrong kind of data rather than missing data. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §2]
- MEXT states its own rates: more than 20倍 for the embassy track (2026 self-inspection PDF), about 2-3倍 for master's courses (an FY2010 report) and a 10倍 FY2006 target; only the first is current, and all sit at rung 2 as publisher-stated. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §3]
- CSC: 63,041 enrolled in 2018 (12.81%), an enrolment stock; third-party "30,000-60,000 a year" figures are unofficial. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §3]
- GKS 2026 has 2,000 graduate awards with per-country quotas but no applicant count; it is a numerator with no denominator. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §4]
