# Synthesis cross-check

Four cheap searches that probed what each finished synthesis's own taxonomy excludes; three real gaps and one confirmed non-gap. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md] See [portfolio theory](portfolio-theory.md), [corridor mechanics](corridor-mechanics.md), [Germany](../countries/de.md), [United States](../countries/us.md), [United Kingdom](../countries/gb.md).

- Method: grep every synthesis for a concept; zero mentions across all of them is a taxonomy signal. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md]
- Work during study was never asked: Germany 20 h/week and 140 full days a year (about €1,112 a month at €13.90/h, HiWi posts exempt), Korea 20 h/week with unlimited vacation work, UK 20 h/week and none for a part-time postgraduate course sponsor. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §1]
- Work income is a property of destination and study mode that changes the affordability test, never a funding route. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §1]
- Sub-national schemes were missed: the Bavarian Scholarship Programme and BAYHOST, which is both sub-national and nationality-keyed. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §2]
- German political foundations fund master's students: Konrad-Adenauer €992 a month for 2 years, Heinrich-Böll €934, while Friedrich-Ebert and Ernst Ludwig Ehrlich restrict internationals. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §3]
- Some carry non-financial conditions such as endorsing Christian Democratic values or C1 German. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §3]
- Confirmed non-gap: OpenAlex is bibliographic and its funder side is research funding, so no action. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §4]
  Superseded (in part): OpenAlex was later adopted in a narrower role, as an R1 source for research institutes — see [Role-routed discovery design](../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-17-role-routed-discovery-design.md).
- German student-visa processing runs 4-12 weeks by post, appointment queues are a larger delay (Mumbai VFS 10-12 weeks for a slot), and some posts are faster for holders of a German public scholarship. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §5]
- Undermatching evidence: the Hoxby RCT found treated students admitted to 12% more colleges; the figures are unverified citations and a literature gap, not a source gap. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §6]
- Recommended: run this pass after every synthesis. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §7]
