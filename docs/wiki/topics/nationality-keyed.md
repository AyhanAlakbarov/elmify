# Nationality-keyed master's funding

Whether a nationality-keyed axis pays for the launch markets, and which schemes Azerbaijani and Turkish students can initiate. Schemes: [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md), [GKS](../schemes/gks.md), [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md), [IsDB](../schemes/isdb.md), [Aga Khan ISP](../schemes/aga-khan-isp.md), [Chevening](../schemes/chevening.md), [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md), [SISGP](../schemes/sisgp.md). Countries: [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md).

## Findings
- Four scheme groups were confirmed `DIRECT` or effectively self-initiated and full-cost for launch markets: ADB-Japan (8 of 9, not Türkiye), the TEV schemes and Jean Monnet (EUR 700 to 22,000) for Türkiye, and IsDB (7 of 9); before these runs Türkiye had no `DIRECT` full-cost route. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §1]
- Later verification found IsDB's M.Sc. track keyed on LDMC status with none of the nine listed; see [IsDB](../schemes/isdb.md). [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
- Jean Monnet was found by run 2 only (EU-funded, about 150 scholars, 2026-27 deadline 4 Nov 2025); run 1 supplied TEV and Estonia (Harno, EUR 350/month). [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §4]
- Türkiye Bursları quotas are not published by either run. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §4]
- The sending side may publish a quota the funder does not: Azerbaijan's State Agency disclosed 104 Azerbaijani Stipendium Hungaricum awards for 2026/27, 80 at master's. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §3]
- Türkiye's outbound landscape is TEV-shaped, not state-shaped. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §7]

## Confirmed absences
- EBRD (no master's scholarship), UNDP (internships only), OTS/TÜRKSOY/Turkic Academy, TÜBİTAK 2215 (inbound to Türkiye), Türkiye Bursları (inbound only), the YTB-IsDB programme (inbound). [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
- JJ/WBGSP is eligible only for Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan; the Swedish Institute only for Armenia, Azerbaijan, Georgia and Uzbekistan; DAAD In-Country/In-Region excludes the South Caucasus and Central Asia. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]

## Method
- The 1a membership enumeration was a scaffold: individual MFA citations were not fetched for every claim, so it is not yet auditable, though the method produced ADB-JSP, Jean Monnet and TEV. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §6]
- The Fulbright omission was found by a cheap cross-check, not by any of four runs. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §8]
- Design consequences stated: a return-service field on the award, applicant-attribute conditions beyond nationality, record GKS quotas with their year, ask both sides for a quota. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §7]
- Still open at the time: Fulbright per-country terms (now [verified](../schemes/fulbright.md)), the GKS quota figures (now [verified](../schemes/gks.md)), IsDB LDMC status, Aga Khan ISP and Chevening allocation (the last three closed in the gap-fill verification).
