# Corridor mechanics

Post-study work, visa money and commitment payments for eight destinations, from two research runs; the Korea follow-up is in [round 2](corridor-mechanics-round2.md). [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md] Pages: [China](../countries/cn.md), [Germany](../countries/de.md), [United Kingdom](../countries/gb.md), [Hungary](../countries/hu.md), [France](../countries/fr.md), [Japan](../countries/jp.md), [South Korea](../countries/kr.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md).

## Post-study work
- Routes: UK Graduate route (2 years now, 18 months for applications on or after 1 Jan 2027); Germany 18 months; Japan 12 months plus J-Find up to 2 years; France RECE 12 months; Hungary 9 months; China no national route; Turkey no dedicated route. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Korea's D-10 is 2 or 3 years, and the runs conflict. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §4]
- Türkiye Bursları makes Turkey the most accessible funding in the sample and Turkey has no post-study work route: a good floor, poor mobility ([Türkiye Bursları](../schemes/turkiye-burslari.md)). [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- It is a destination-level fact, so it belongs in a per-country destination policy record. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §6]

## Visa money and commitment payments
- Proof required: UK £1,529 a month in London and £1,171 outside, at most 9 months; Germany €992 a month (€11,904 blocked account); France €877.50 a month from 1 Aug 2026, indexed to gross SMIC. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- No statutory or published figure: Japan (about ¥2m is a university convention), China, Turkey, Hungary; Korea's ₩20m/₩16m is disputed. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- Both runs agree a scholarship can substitute for financial proof (DAAD, MEAE, GKS, CSC, Chevening, Türkiye Bursları, Stipendium Hungaricum); the UK exempts students with an official sponsor. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- "Deposit" is UK-shaped (£2,000-£4,000+ before the CAS); Japan has a ¥282,000 enrolment fee; Germany and France have no deposit culture at public universities. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §3]

## Admission, registers and recognition
- Admission difficulty for international applicants is unpublished essentially everywhere. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Regimes: rule-based (UK, Germany, Turkey, Hungary); capacity-competitive (France); exam-gated (Japan, often via a 研究生 year); holistic with quota (Korea); China exempts internationals from 考研. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Only the UK publishes visa refusals by nationality cleanly. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Run 1 is right that NC-style retrospective cutoffs generally do not apply to international master's applicants in Germany. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §4]
- Four recognition centres publish lists: Germany's anabin, China's jsj.moe.gov.cn, Ireland's QQI and Poland's NAWA KWALIFIKATOR. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
