## Recognition
- Neither launch market publishes a positive list of recognised foreign institutions; Azerbaijan's nostrification (TKTA) is case by case and resolved only after graduation, Türkiye has an e-Devlet query and a ranking rule. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §1]
- The derivation from ENIC-NARIC found no list publisher beyond Germany, China, Ireland and Poland; anabin (which rates institution and degree separately) is the only true accreditation-plus-recognition gate and China's MOE list a genuine second, each with a stated incompleteness caveat. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §2]
- India moved equivalence from AIU to UGC in 2025; Pakistan's HEC issues equivalence letters case by case. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §3]

## Contested facts, read against primary sources
- UK sponsor compliance thresholds come in three dated tiers: refusal under 10% to 1 June 2026 then under 5%; enrolment 90% then 95%; completion 85% until 31 May 2027 and 90% after. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- RWTH states no academic minimum; its language bars sit in the examination regulation per programme, so a screen reading programme pages alone records "no published bar" wrongly. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- Canada's PGWP excludes curriculum-licensing programmes begun after fixed dates; bachelor's, master's and doctoral graduates have no field-of-study requirement. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- Georgia's NCEQE is off EQAR after a rejected renewal on independence grounds, but DEQAR still lists 83 Georgian institutions, 60 with reports, and the screen is a filter rather than a blackout. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §4] [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §7]
- Run B's Caucasus International University entry is refuted by its own NCEQE finding. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §5]
- Run A is the better-sourced run; run B has a fabricated bar and a forbidden acceptance-rate estimate. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §6]

## Registers and floors
- Canada's DLI list and the UK's Licensed Student Sponsors CSV are the strongest destination-side registers; neither is a recognition list. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- Germany's tuition-free public sector is the floor: €0 tuition, a €300-340 semester contribution and a €11,904 blocked account for 2026. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §7]
- Screen post-study work separately from accreditation. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §7]
