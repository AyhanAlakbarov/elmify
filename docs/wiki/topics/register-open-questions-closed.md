Not a research run: the last pass over the register series, answering six open questions on 2026-08-31. - DAPIP: one POST to `ope.ed.gov` returns a zip of US accreditation data (44,954 campuses, 64,598 accreditation records, 89,532 actions) including terminations, without authentication. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §1]
- SEVP is a Drupal view with GET filters reachable only through a browser transport, certified per campus, with no bulk export. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §2]
- UAE: KHDA publishes 34 Dubai free-zone providers with UQAIB star ratings dated 2022, and 18 of 34 are unrated; the connection fix is a CA bundle, never `verify=False`. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §3]
- Ireland: 27 named authorised providers with no list file; the ILEP is frozen and ends at the end of 2026, so Ireland stays a consultant check. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §4]
- China: MOE publishes 3,167 institutions (2025-06-20) but no register of those authorised to enrol international students, so it is case by case on the destination side. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §5]
- Correction: only `jsj.moe.gov.cn` is IPv6-only; `moe.gov.cn` answers over IPv4. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §5]
- College Scorecard publishes no master's tuition; it gives selectivity, accreditor, earnings and debt, never price, which qualifies the US recon's strongest claim. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §6]
- Median master's debt (`DEBT_ALL_STGP_ANY_MDN`, `CREDLEV=5`) is a proxy and must never be shown as a cost. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §6]
