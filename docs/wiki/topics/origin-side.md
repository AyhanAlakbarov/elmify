# The origin side

What nine governments publish to their own citizens about funding a master's abroad. Pages: [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [Kyrgyzstan](../countries/kg.md), [Tajikistan](../countries/tj.md), [Turkmenistan](../countries/tm.md), [Uzbekistan](../countries/uz.md), [Armenia](../countries/am.md), [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md), [HTP](../schemes/htp.md).

- For most foreign schemes reaching these countries the origin state nominates or pre-selects, so the route is `NOMINATED` for that nationality whatever the funder's page says; the route belongs to the (scheme, nationality) pair. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1]
- Uzbekistan is the counter-instance: Hungarian scheme `DIRECT` by the ministry's written word. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Tajikistan is not silent (Center for International Programs, 314-place quota 2026) and Turkmenistan is not silent but bachelor's-level. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Kazakhstan's return-service clause is verbatim one year's continuous work; its GPA floor yields to a lower host requirement. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Azerbaijani Hungaricum figures: 200 ceiling, 104 awarded, 129 realised. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §2]
- Georgia's 5 bonus points, Kazakhstan's GPA and IELTS rules, Kyrgyzstan's HIV clearance and Turkmenistan's age band are nominating-state rules, not the funder's. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §5]
- Return service has a third source: the origin state binding a graduate on another state's money. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §5]
- Hostile surfaces: `abdigm.meb.gov.tr` robots-blocked, Tajikistan on Facebook; `escs.am` is WAF-blocked, and Armenia was parked by decision. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §6] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Both runs found no machine-readable feed from any origin body. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §8]
- Quality caveats: run B cites all seven Armenian schemes to one URL and its Kazakhstan return-service quote is the wrong sentence. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §7]
