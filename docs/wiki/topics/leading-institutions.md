# Leading institutions outside the high-income core

Whether a student from Azerbaijan or Türkiye has a funded master's route they can enter unaided, from two runs adjudicated against a pre-run recon. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md] Pages: [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [Pakistan](../countries/pk.md), [India](../countries/in.md), [Georgia](../countries/ge.md), [Türkiye Bursları](../schemes/turkiye-burslari.md), [Heydar Aliyev grant](../schemes/heydar-aliyev-grant.md).

## The route
- `AUTOMATIC_ON_ADMISSION` is real: an admitted international applicant is considered for funding with no separate competition at Sabancı, Bilkent, Nazarbayev, Khazar and ADA. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- Corrected 2026-08-30: it differs by nationality. Khazar and ADA citizens sit a State Examination Centre test, and Bilkent citizens take ALES, so the route is not automatic for an Azerbaijani applying at home. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- Route belongs to the (instrument, nationality) pair, not the instrument. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- Run A's detection rule: the phrases "automatically considered", "no separate application" and "considered by default" mark the best awards regardless of URL path. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- Khazar's Excellence Scholarship is tuition only, against Baku living costs of about $400-1,000 a month. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4]

## Adjudication
- The six Azerbaijani dual degrees are not shown to fund the student: the page says only that "some" costs are financed by BP and its partners, and run B's "free of charge" is unsupported. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §3]
- Run B found Pakistan's HEC Allama Iqbal scholarships (100% tuition, stipend, airfare) for Central Asian citizens, so run A's confirmed absence is wrong. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4]
- Run B's METU entry is METU Northern Cyprus, not Ankara; its Sabancı "full scholarships only" quote is from one department's guide. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4]
- Inbound versus outbound conflation (Georgian entries) is the most repeated failure across the six runs. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §5]
- No body enumerates funded international master's places; the exception is India's Study in India fee-waiver table. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §7]

## Changes for the model
- Tuition-only cover is a floor only where living costs are low; teaching language is a hard filter; discovery must not rely on path triage alone. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §8]
