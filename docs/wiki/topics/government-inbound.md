# Government inbound master's scholarships

Which foreign governments fund a master's an Azerbaijani or Turkish citizen can get. Pages: [Azerbaijan](../countries/az.md), [Türkiye](../countries/tr.md), [Russia](../countries/ru.md), [Japan](../countries/jp.md), [Kazakhstan](../countries/kz.md), [DAAD EPOS](../schemes/daad-epos.md), [SISGP](../schemes/sisgp.md), [Heydar Aliyev grant](../schemes/heydar-aliyev-grant.md), [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md), [Chevening](../schemes/chevening.md), [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md).

- About 36 of 58 jurisdictions have at least one row on an official source, far fewer are complete. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- Nearly all full-cost schemes are `NOMINATED`; the `DIRECT` full-cost set is Türkiye Bursları, Chevening, EPOS (with work experience) and for Azerbaijan SISGP. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- Verification grades are FETCHED, OFFICIAL SNIPPET and LEAD. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md]
- Not a master's: Canada Study in Canada (8 months), Israel MFA, Latvia, Austria Ernst Mach, Swiss research scholarships; not full-cost: Italian MAECI (9 months, EUR 10,800), Slovakia (EUR 5,000/yr). [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §4] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- Lithuania: 70 grants with per-country quotas, EUR 592/month plus tuition, for AZ, GE, AM, KZ, UZ among others. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- Serbia's World in Serbia: 100 scholarships for NAM members, nominated; latest evidence 2025. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- Czech development scholarships for 2027/28 exclude Azerbaijan and Armenia. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- Singapore, Hong Kong, Luxembourg, Denmark, Cyprus, Portugal were wrongly reported empty by multi-country queries; one search per country corrected it. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §7] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §4]
- Finland: "there are no governmental scholarships available". [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §7]
- Design lessons: eligibility needs a set of keys (residence, descent, citizenship); some routes sit outside the quota; sending sides publish the per-country number. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §5]
- No cross-country index of government scholarships exists. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §4]
- Further deep-research runs were planned: Asia and Gulf, Central/Eastern Europe, sub-national tier, origin region. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §6]

Superseded: the four regional deep-research runs were replaced by a census of every country followed by two-model batches of 10-12 countries, and the Heydar Aliyev grant became known-answer check 11 — see [Inbound government search design](../../superpowers/specs/2026-09-13-inbound-government-search-design.md).
