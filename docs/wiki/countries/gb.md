# United Kingdom

The UK as a study destination: work rights, visa money, sponsor rules and registers. See [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md), [synthesis cross-check](../topics/synthesis-crosscheck.md), [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md), [three remaining gaps](../topics/three-remaining-gaps.md), [Chevening](../schemes/chevening.md).

- The Graduate route is unsponsored with no salary floor: 2 years now, 18 months for applications on or after 1 Jan 2027. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Proof: £1,529 a month in London, £1,171 outside, at most 9 months, held 28 days; a recognised official sponsor is exempt. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- A tuition deposit, commonly £2,000-£4,000+, is due before the CAS is issued. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §3]
- Only the UK publishes visa refusals by nationality cleanly (Home Office Vis_D02 and Vis_04); the OfS Register is machine-readable. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Work is limited to 20 h/week (10 for some courses), and a student sponsored for a part-time postgraduate course may not work at all. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §1]
- Sponsor compliance thresholds are dated tiers: from 1 June 2026 refusal under 5% and enrolment 95%, completion 85% until 31 May 2027 then 90%. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- The Licensed Student Sponsors register is a CSV updated nearly every business day with each sponsor's rating; UK ENIC is Ecctis, with no public institution list. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- The OfS register gives each provider's degree-awarding powers, with bulk availability unconfirmed. [src: 2026-08-31-three-remaining-gaps.md §1]
