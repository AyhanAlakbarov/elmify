# India

India as a host of funded places and a recognition market. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §7] [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §3] See [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md), [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md), [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md), [synthesis cross-check](../topics/synthesis-crosscheck.md).

- India is in the Non-Aligned Movement, so eligible in principle for Azerbaijan's Heydar Aliyev grant, but a student cannot simply apply. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §1]
- Study in India publishes a browsable table of named institutions with per-institute fee-waiver counts, the only enumerable list in scope, though its figures are internally inconsistent. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §7] [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §6]
- The UGC 2025 regulations moved equivalence from AIU to UGC (`equivalence.ugc.ac.in`), decided case by case. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §3]
- A German student-visa appointment slot in Mumbai took 10-12 weeks. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §5]
