# Türkiye

Türkiye as a sending country and as a market. Outbound is foundation-shaped, not state-shaped. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §7] See [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md), [outbound launch](../topics/outbound-launch.md), [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md).

## Own outbound provision
- [YLSY](../schemes/ylsy.md) ("1416 Bursları") is open, with 811 places for 2025 against 668 for 2024; it is bonded at twice the funded period. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §1]
- TÜBİTAK funds no outbound master's: 2210 is domestic and 2213 is doctoral-only. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]
- YÖK sends research assistants abroad under Law 2547, restricted to university staff; the Central Bank (TCMB) funds its own employees (`EMPLOYER_ONLY`). [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]
- YTB and TÜBİTAK are confirmed dead ends for outbound master's study; a private foundation is the principal channel. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §7]
- Four routes are `DIRECT` or effectively self-initiated for Türkiye: TEV-Garring/DTU, Chevening-TEV, TEV-UK Imperial/Cambridge (Cambridge nominated), and the Jean Monnet Scholarship. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §1]
- Türkiye Bursları is inbound only and Turkish citizens are ineligible. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]

## Foreign schemes for Turkish citizens
- The Ministry of National Education (MEB) selects candidates by interview and reports them to the host country, which decides acceptance. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1]
- [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md) is `NOMINATED` via MEB; Hungary's historical Türkiye sub-quota was 150 for 2021. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §4]
- [GKS](../schemes/gks.md): Embassy Track quota 11; University Track General 2 and R-GKS 5. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3]
- [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md): master's, maximum 2 academic years, deadline 10 Apr 2026, award capped at USD 60,000 per academic year. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1] [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §5]
- [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md) excludes Türkiye, which is not an ADB borrowing member. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- IsDB's M.Sc. track keys on LDMC status and Türkiye is not on the list. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
- [Heydar Aliyev grant](../schemes/heydar-aliyev-grant.md): Türkiye qualifies as an OIC member, route `NOMINATED`. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- Excluded from DAAD EPOS, SISGP, the Lithuanian scheme, Czech development scholarships, Australia Awards and Manaaki. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- [Chevening](../schemes/chevening.md): `DIRECT`, run A only. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]

## Study in Türkiye and recognition
- Turkey has no dedicated post-study work route and publishes no fixed visa-money amount (Law 6458 Art. 38 requires "sufficient means"). [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- Sabancı and Bilkent consider admitted internationals for funding automatically, but a Turkish citizen at Bilkent takes ALES rather than the GRE. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- Koç bills tuition directly to Azerbaijan's state scholarship programme for state-sponsored Azerbaijanis. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §5]
- YÖK offers an e-Devlet query, a blacklist and a ranking-based equivalence rule, so recognition can be gated partially by ranking band. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §7]
- YÖK's list of 41 refused-equivalence institutions names units inside reputable universities; it has not been primary-sourced and must not be encoded. [src: 2026-08-31-three-remaining-gaps.md §2]
- See [Türkiye Bursları](../schemes/turkiye-burslari.md), [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md), [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md), [three remaining gaps](../topics/three-remaining-gaps.md).
