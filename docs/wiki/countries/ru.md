# Russia

Russian state-funded routes for foreign master's applicants. See [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md) and [Kazakhstan](kz.md).

- The verification found three routes, not one: citizens of Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan and Tajikistan compete for budget places on general grounds under the 29 March 1996 equal-rights agreement, outside the quota. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- Citizens of Azerbaijan, Armenia, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Kyrgyzstan, Moldova, Tajikistan, Turkmenistan, Uzbekistan and Ukraine with a Russian permanent residence permit get the same terms under the 1992 Tashkent agreement, a residence-keyed route. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- Compatriots living abroad compete on equal terms with citizens, proving status by documents; this is descent-keyed and outside the quota. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- The government quota itself (Decree 2150) says compatriots' interests are taken into account but publishes no separate allocation. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- The synthesis resolves run A versus run B this way: the quota is one pool, a compatriot route exists, and the CIS and compatriot routes sit outside the quota. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §2]
- The research calls the CIS route the single largest unlisted route for Kazakh students, and notes that a model walking quota documents cannot see these routes. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §6] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §5]
- "Russian quota" appears among the schemes classed `NOMINATED` for Azerbaijani and Turkish citizens, graded "Runs A/B, partly verified". [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- The Azerbaijani Ministry's 2024-2025 realised-intake report lists 5 Azerbaijani citizens placed in Russia (MGIMO). [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §2]
- The Russian government quota is by home-government nomination, administered entirely by overseas Rossotrudnichestvo offices with no central calendar. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §7]
- It has no single global deadline ([instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md)). [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §3]
