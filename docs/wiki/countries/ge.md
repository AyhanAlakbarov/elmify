# Georgia

Georgia as a sending country and a market. See [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md), [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md).

- [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md): master's with "full financial support", deadline 1 Jul 2026; the page requires J-1 eligibility, which binds the grantee to return to Georgia for at least two years. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1] [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §2]
- A writing sample is mandatory for a named list of master's fields. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §4]
- [GKS](../schemes/gks.md): Embassy Track quota 4; University Track General 1 and R-GKS 1. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3]
- [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md): up to 80 Georgians a year (a sending-partner quota Hungary does not publish), with up to 5 bonus points for IDPs, disability, social vulnerability or residence in high-mountain or occupied-territory-adjacent settlements. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §4]
- Georgia and Kyrgyzstan require dual registration for Hungaricum, and the route is `NOMINATED`. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1]
- [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md) lists Georgia; IsDB is not available to Georgia. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §1]
- Chevening's application form lists "South Caucasus" beside Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan, but no per-country allocation is published ([Chevening](../schemes/chevening.md)). [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §3]
- DAAD In-Country/In-Region excludes the South Caucasus; the Swedish Institute list includes Georgia. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
- Verified inbound: Czech development scholarships for 2027/28 are open to Georgia (window 21 Aug to 30 Sep 2026), Lithuania's 2026 list includes Georgia, [DAAD EPOS](../schemes/daad-epos.md) and [SISGP](../schemes/sisgp.md) include Georgia. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- Georgia's state study grant for foreigners (partial tuition, up to GEL 2,250) rests on a lead only. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- `iec.gov.ge` serves percent-encoded Georgian URLs long enough to defeat direct fetching in run A. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §6]

## Inbound and screening
- Georgia has no open national inbound scholarship, only bilateral allocations such as 10 places a year with Armenia. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §8]
- Georgia's national QA body NCEQE is off EQAR after a rejected renewal on independence grounds. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §4]
- DEQAR still lists 83 Georgian institutions, 60 with reports, but nearly all reports are NCEQE's, so the screen is a filter rather than a blackout. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §7]
- Run B's Georgian entries are largely outbound, not funded places for internationals. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §5]
- See [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md), [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md), [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md).
