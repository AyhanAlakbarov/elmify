# Japan

Japan as a data source and as a host of descent-keyed study. See [worldwide funding sources](../topics/worldwide-funding-sources.md) and [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md).

- No programme register exists for Japan: MEXT publishes only Japanese-only institution and faculty Excel directories, and e-Stat is aggregate statistics behind a registered app id. [src: 2026-08-28-worldwide-funding-sources-synthesis.md §3]
- KAKEN is usable on the grant side: OpenSearch API, free CiNii app id, English and Japanese records, terms "compatible with" CC BY 4.0. [src: 2026-08-28-worldwide-funding-sources-synthesis.md §3]
- The 2026-08-28 probe found KAKEN returns HTTP 403 without a CiNii application id. [src: 2026-08-28-worldwide-funding-sources-synthesis.md §7]
- Design consequence stated: Japan is tier-0-only, KAKEN for grants, and a registry should not be manufactured from MEXT Excel. [src: 2026-08-28-worldwide-funding-sources-synthesis.md §6]
- For descent-keyed master's study, the route that exists is JICA Nikkei Leaders (Japanese emigrants and descendants in Central and South America and the Caribbean, in principle up to third generation, 11 participating countries), not MEXT. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- The MEXT PDF that run B cited for a Nikkei route is a non-degree MEXT Teacher Training Student programme that does not mention Nikkei. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- The synthesis records the same resolution: JICA, Latin America only. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §2]

Superseded: KAKEN was later dropped as a funding source because it funds researchers, not master's training — see [Funding-Led Discovery design](../../superpowers/specs/2026-08-28-funding-led-discovery-design.md).

## As a destination
- Post-study: a 特定活動 job-hunting permit of 12 months plus J-Find up to 2 years; no statutory visa-money figure, only a university convention of about ¥2m. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- Entry is exam-gated per university, often through a non-degree 研究生 year, with a ¥282,000 non-refundable enrolment fee. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §3] [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- No Japanese university publishes a matched international applicant and admitted count for master's programmes. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §2]
- See [MEXT](../schemes/mext.md), [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md) and [round 2](../topics/corridor-mechanics-round2.md).
