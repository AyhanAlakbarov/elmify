# Azerbaijan

Azerbaijan as a sending country (its own outbound programmes, nominations and quotas) and as a market for foreign schemes. See [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md), [outbound launch](../topics/outbound-launch.md), [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md), [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md).

## Own outbound programmes
- The State Programme ran a 2025/2026 intake (10 April to 15 June 2025, 1,147 applicants) and its 2026/2027 list was approved on 12 January 2026. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §1]
- That list covers 231 institutions in 33 countries and 4,121 programmes across 15 priority fields, and the annual quota is 500. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- It is bonded: 5 years working in Azerbaijan, secured by an employment contract, with all costs repayable on breach. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §1]
- It funds only an admission the student already holds, so it cannot be a portfolio floor. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §2]
- Holding another scholarship is not prohibited by the contract; the ministry pays tuition only against an invoice. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §8]
- The [HTP](../schemes/htp.md) 2024-2025 realised intake was 192 citizens, 129 of them in Hungary. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §2]
- Do not confuse the State Programme, HTP and the inbound [Heydar Aliyev grant](../schemes/heydar-aliyev-grant.md); a fourth, domestic award bears the same name. [src: 2026-08-29-outbound-launch-synthesis.md §5]

## Foreign schemes for Azerbaijani citizens
- The origin ministry decides the route: for a scheme its funder markets as open, only applicants approved by the Ministry of Education as Authorised Sending Party may take part. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1]
- Türkiye Bursları is `DIRECT` and full-cost (5,000 TL/month at master's), graded official snippet; Polish Banach NAWA is `DIRECT` (stipend and fee exemption, run A only); the Malaysia International Scholarship is `DIRECT` and full, with its eligible-country list only a lead. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md): up to 200 a year (MoU ceiling, 2024-2026), 104 awarded for 2026/27 of which 80 master's. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §2]
- [GKS](../schemes/gks.md): Embassy Track quota 5; University Track General 2 and R-GKS 1. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3]
- [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md): master's, deadline 28 Jul 2026, minimum 2 years' professional experience. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1]
- [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md) lists Azerbaijan on its citizenship list. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- Inbound verification: [DAAD EPOS](../schemes/daad-epos.md) is open (residence-keyed), [SISGP](../schemes/sisgp.md) is open, Lithuania has a state scheme with country quotas, Serbia's World in Serbia is `NOMINATED` via NAM, and Czech development scholarships are closed for 2027/28. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]
- JJ/WBGSP is not open to Azerbaijan (2027 list), and DAAD In-Country/In-Region excludes the South Caucasus. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
- [Chevening](../schemes/chevening.md) appears as `DIRECT` for Azerbaijanis, run A only. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1]

## Funded places, recognition and the dual degrees
- Khazar and ADA consider admitted foreign applicants for funding automatically, but an Azerbaijani citizen sits a State Examination Centre test, and at Khazar is placed rather than choosing. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- The six dual degrees are not shown to fund the student: the page says only "some" costs come from BP and its partners; entry is by national exam and joint selection. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §3]
- Recognition (nostrification) runs through TKTA case by case and resolves only after graduation, so it cannot be gated. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §1]
  Superseded: TKTA publishes two negative lists (not-recognised and high-refusal universities), still no positive list — see [Institution-safety screen design](../../superpowers/specs/2026-08-31-institution-safety-screen-design.md).
- Azerbaijan's Heydar Aliyev grant needs nomination by the applicant's own government (100 awards a year). [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §1]
- [Türkiye Bursları](../schemes/turkiye-burslari.md) is `DIRECT` for Azerbaijanis. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §1] See [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md) and [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md).
