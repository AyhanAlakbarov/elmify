# Kazakhstan

Kazakhstan as a sending country, an inbound destination and a Russian-route beneficiary. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1] See [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md), [government inbound](../topics/government-inbound.md), [Russia](ru.md).

- [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md): master's, deadline 15 Jul 2026, at least 2 years' substantive experience after graduation. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1]
- [GKS](../schemes/gks.md): Embassy Track quota 13; University Track General 5 and R-GKS 18; Overseas Korean/Adoptee track 7. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §4]
- The Koryo-saram, ethnic Koreans deported to Central Asia in 1937, are concentrated in the countries holding 16 of the 24 Overseas Korean places. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §4]
- Kazakhstan's intergovernmental grants (via `oq.gov.kz` and `bolashak.gov.kz`) carry per-country tables, a GPA 3.0 floor and IELTS 5.5 or TOEFL iBT 50 set by Kazakhstan, tie-break priorities, and a one-year return-and-work obligation. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §4]
- The GPA floor (3.0 of 4.0/4.33) yields to a lower host requirement; the return-service clause (one year of continuous work) does not. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Tie-break order: orphans, disabled since childhood, large families, then rural residents. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Kazakh citizens can take Russian budget places with no nomination under an equal-rights agreement ([Russia](ru.md)). [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- Pakistan's HEC Allama Iqbal scholarship names "central Asian and friendly countries" with 350 places and no master's split or country list. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- Inbound "Study in Kazakhstan" (2024): 550 places, 11,293 applications from 83 countries, 50 at master's; ethnic Kazakhs form an eligibility class with no separate quota published. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2]
- [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md) lists Kazakhstan; the Swedish Institute list does not; [DAAD EPOS](../schemes/daad-epos.md) is open to Kazakhstan. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- IsDB's M.Sc. LDMC list does not include Kazakhstan. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
- Lithuania's 2026 state scholarships include Kazakhstan. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- Nazarbayev University considers all qualified international applicants for funding by default, with no separate application. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §1]
- Pakistan's HEC scholarship is a funded inbound route for Central Asian citizens ([Pakistan](pk.md), [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md)). [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4]
