# France

France as a study destination. See [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md), [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md).

- Post-study: RECE (replaced APS), 12 months, non-renewable. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Proof: €877.50 a month from 1 Aug 2026, up from €615, indexed to 47% of gross SMIC, so it must be re-fetched on a schedule. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- No deposit culture at public universities; a CVEC is paid after enrolment. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §3]
- Admission is competitive against published capacity on monmaster.gouv.fr, but non-EU applicants abroad use Études en France; RNCP on data.gouv.fr is machine-readable. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Eiffel is nominated by the university and has no applicant-facing portal. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §7]
- The seed index is `campusfrance.org`'s scholarship directory. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §6]
