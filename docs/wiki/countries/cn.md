# China

China as a study destination: work rights, visa money, admission data and registers. See [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md), [round 2](../topics/corridor-mechanics-round2.md), [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md), [register questions closed](../topics/register-open-questions-closed.md), [CSC](../schemes/csc.md).

- No national post-study work route exists; only Shanghai and free-trade-zone pilots. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- No published financial threshold for the X1 visa. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- Internationals are exempt from 考研 and apply directly to universities under 自主招生; China publishes no visa-refusal data. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- No university publishes a matched international applicant and admitted count for master's programmes; moe.gov.cn's 来华留学统计 is an enrolment stock series. [src: 2026-08-28-corridor-mechanics-round2-synthesis.md §2]
- `jsj.moe.gov.cn` lists about 10,000 institutions across 44 countries as "a reference list for the public": presence is no guarantee, absence is no refusal. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §2]
- MOE publishes 3,167 Chinese higher education institutions (2025-06-20) but no register of those authorised to enrol international students, which is case by case. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §5]
- Only `jsj.moe.gov.cn` is IPv6-only; `moe.gov.cn` answers over IPv4. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §5]
