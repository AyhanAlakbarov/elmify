# Kyrgyzstan

See [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md) and [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md): master's with a stipend up to 24 months, deadline 10 Jun 2026, no work-experience requirement stated. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1]
- Nominees are placed through IIE only and "are only informed of the final choice"; financial need is not a selection factor; holders of a US master's are ineligible. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §4]
- Grantees are expected to return home for at least two years in compliance with J-visa requirements. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §2]
- [GKS](../schemes/gks.md): Embassy Track quota 9; University Track General 7 and R-GKS 3; Overseas Korean/Adoptee track 3. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §4]
- [Aga Khan ISP](../schemes/aga-khan-isp.md) is available in the Kyrgyz Republic. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §2]
- JJ/WBGSP is open to the Kyrgyz Republic, Tajikistan and Uzbekistan; [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md) lists the Kyrgyz Republic. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5] [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2]
- Kyrgyzstan requires HIV clearance and a police certificate for nominations; `student.edu.gov.kg` is flat HTML, the cleanest of the nine surfaces. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §5] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §6]
- Stipendium Hungaricum is `NOMINATED` for Kyrgyzstan, with dual registration. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §1] [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- Kyrgyz citizens are among those who compete for Russian budget places outside the quota ([Russia](ru.md)). [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- IsDB's M.Sc. LDMC list does not include Kyrgyzstan. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
