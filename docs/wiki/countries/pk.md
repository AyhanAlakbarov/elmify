# Pakistan

Pakistan as a funder of inbound study and a recognition market. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4] [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §3] See [leading institutions](../topics/leading-institutions.md), [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md), [Kazakhstan](kz.md).

- HEC's Allama Muhammad Iqbal Scholarships are open to Central Asian and other friendly countries: 100% tuition, stipend, accommodation, return airfare, applied through `eportal.hec.gov.pk`. [src: 2026-08-29-leading-institutions-synthesis.md §4]
- The scheme names "central Asian and friendly countries" with 350 places and no master's split or country list. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- HEC publishes a list of recognised Pakistani universities and a foreign-university list for its own scholarship, neither a recognition list for foreign degrees; equivalence letters are case by case via `eservices.hec.gov.pk`. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §3]
