# Hungary

Hungary as a study destination. See [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md), [instrument landscape](../topics/instrument-landscape.md), [Stipendium Hungaricum](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md).

- The job-seeking permit (Act XC of 2023) is 9 months and non-extendable. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Subsistence proof is required, with no single national figure on oif.gov.hu. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2]
- Admission is rule-based with the bar published in advance; the register is the Oktatási Hivatal FIR, human-readable. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Visa refusal data is only Eurostat-level. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §5]
- Stipendium Hungaricum is nominated by a Sending Partner and jointly selected, covers 90+ partner countries and publishes a clean calendar. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §3]
- Hungary publishes no applicant counts for the scheme; the seed portal is `stipendiumhungaricum.hu`. [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §4] [src: 2026-08-27-instrument-landscape-synthesis.md §6]
