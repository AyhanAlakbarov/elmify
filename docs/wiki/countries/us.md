# United States

The US as a study destination, from the register series and the cross-check. See [register questions closed](../topics/register-open-questions-closed.md), [synthesis cross-check](../topics/synthesis-crosscheck.md).

- Work: 20 h/week in session and full-time in breaks, with on-campus, CPT and OPT rules. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §1]
- Undermatching evidence (Hoxby and Avery; Hoxby and Turner's randomised trial) supports the intervention; the citations are to be verified. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §6]
- DAPIP returns US accreditation history, terminations included, from one unauthenticated POST. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §1]
- SEVP certification is per campus, from a Drupal view with no bulk export. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §2]
- College Scorecard has selectivity, earnings and debt but no master's tuition, so the price half of the US finding does not hold. [src: 2026-08-31-register-open-questions-closed.md §6]
