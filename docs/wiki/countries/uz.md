# Uzbekistan

See [origin side](../topics/origin-side.md) and [nationality-keyed](../topics/nationality-keyed.md).

- Stipendium Hungaricum is `DIRECT` for Uzbek citizens: the Ministry of Higher Education, Science and Innovation says documents go straight to the Tempus Public Foundation platform, and it publishes "up to 200 places" with master's named among the levels ([scheme](../schemes/stipendium-hungaricum.md)). [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §9]
- [Fulbright](../schemes/fulbright.md): master's, deadline 30 Jun 2026, minimum 2 years' professional experience. [src: 2026-08-29-fulbright-per-country-verification.md §1]
- [GKS](../schemes/gks.md): Embassy Track quota 13; University Track General 6 and R-GKS 6; Overseas Korean/Adoptee track 6. [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §3] [src: 2026-08-29-gks-quota-primary-verification.md §4]
- [ADB-JSP](../schemes/adb-jsp.md) lists Uzbekistan; JJ/WBGSP and the Swedish Institute list include it. [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-nationality-keyed-synthesis.md §5]
- [SISGP](../schemes/sisgp.md) and [DAAD EPOS](../schemes/daad-epos.md) include Uzbekistan; Lithuania's 2026 list includes it. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §2] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1] [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §3]
- Uzbek citizens with a Russian residence permit get the Russian budget-place route ([Russia](ru.md)). [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-verification.md §1]
- The ICESCO-Uzbekistan Scholarship Programme is inbound (deadline 15 May 2026, ICESCO member states); the "New Uzbekistan Scholarship" is a lead. [src: 2026-09-13-government-inbound-synthesis.md §7]
- `el-yurt.uz` is a monitored surface. [src: 2026-08-29-origin-side-synthesis.md §6]
- IsDB's M.Sc. LDMC list does not include Uzbekistan. [src: 2026-08-29-gapfill-openitem-verification.md §1]
