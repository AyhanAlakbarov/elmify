# Germany

Germany as a study destination: work rights, visa money, schemes, foundations and the free-tuition floor. See [corridor mechanics](../topics/corridor-mechanics.md), [synthesis cross-check](../topics/synthesis-crosscheck.md), [open admission](../topics/open-admission.md), [three remaining gaps](../topics/three-remaining-gaps.md), [DAAD EPOS](../schemes/daad-epos.md).

- Post-study: a §20 AufenthG job-seeking permit of 18 months, non-renewable. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §1]
- Visa proof: €992 a month, €11,904 in a blocked account; no deposit culture at public universities. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §2] [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §3]
- NC-style retrospective cutoffs generally do not apply to international master's applicants, so Germany is rule-based at that level. [src: 2026-08-27-corridor-mechanics-synthesis.md §4]
- Term-time work is 20 h/week and 140 full days a year (about €1,112 a month), with on-campus HiWi posts exempt from the cap. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §1]
- Visa processing runs 4-12 weeks by post, with appointment queues a larger delay. [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §5]
- Sub-national and foundation funders sit outside the national frame: the Bavarian Scholarship Programme, BAYHOST, Konrad-Adenauer (€992 a month) and Heinrich-Böll (€934). [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §2] [src: 2026-08-29-synthesis-crosscheck.md §3]
- anabin is the only true accreditation-plus-recognition gate found, and it does not claim to be complete. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §2]
- Tuition-free public universities are the floor, with a €300-340 semester contribution. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §7]
- RWTH publishes no academic minimum, only per-programme language bars in its examination regulation. [src: 2026-08-31-open-admission-synthesis.md §9]
- Germany is the only tuition-free public floor either run found; Norway's study-permit requirement alone exceeds the €11,904. [src: 2026-08-31-three-remaining-gaps.md §3]
