# Privacy Notice

## Who we are

Tehsil runs a service that helps students find, prepare and submit applications to universities and funders abroad. Your agency, {{ agency }}, is the owner (controller) of your personal data. Tehsil is its operator (processor) and handles your data only on the agency's instructions.

Contact for this service: privacy@tehsil.example. For questions about the agency's own use of your data, ask {{ agency }}.

Company name and address: to be completed before launch

## What we process

- Identity basics: your name, date of birth and nationality.
- Contact details: your e-mail address, phone number and country.
- Education history, transcripts and grades.
- Your CV, your motivation letters and the facts they are built from.
- Programme and funding choices.
- Application status and outcomes.
- Messages you choose to connect through the mailbox feature.
- Technical logs, such as the time and device of a sign-in.

## Why we process it

- To run the service you signed up for.
- To send your application data to the universities and funders you choose.
- To improve recommendations, produce aggregate statistics and test our models, using anonymised data only.
- To check that our AI features work properly on real documents, such as your CV and letters, while your account is active. Your documents and letters are never included in the anonymised data we keep after your personal data is deleted, and we do not train or test models on them after that.
- To send you service e-mails about your applications, as part of the service.

## Sensitive information

Letters and CVs can reveal health, family or religious facts. We use such facts only to produce your own documents. They stay in your documents until your personal data is deleted (see How long we keep it), and they are never included in the anonymised data we keep after that. By accepting this notice you consent to this use.

## Who receives your data

- The universities and funders you apply to, in the countries where they are.
- AI model providers that process the text of your documents for us: OpenAI (United States), Anthropic (United States), OpenRouter (United States) and providers of AI language models in the People's Republic of China (China), such as Zhipu AI, Moonshot AI, DeepSeek, Alibaba Cloud and MiniMax. The text of a CV or letter can identify you.
- Hosting and e-mail providers: Amazon Web Services (United States) for hosting, and our e-mail delivery provider.
- Your agency, {{ agency }}, which sees your application data.

## Transfers abroad

Your data is sent to recipients in other countries, including countries without the data protection law of your home country. These countries include the United States, China and the countries of the universities and funders you choose. By accepting this notice you consent to these transfers, and this consent is what permits them.

## How long we keep it

We keep personal data for as long as your account is active plus 12 months, then delete it, or earlier if you ask us to. The extra 12 months lets a late admission result still be recorded. Anonymised data, which cannot identify you or be linked back to you by anyone, including us, is kept indefinitely. It holds only banded figures such as field of study, degree level and outcome, never your name, your documents or any free text.

## Your rights

You may ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, to stop us processing it, and you may object to processing without giving reasons. You may withdraw your consent at any time. Write to us by e-mail to privacy@tehsil.example; we reply within 7 working days. We answer requests sent from the e-mail address of your account; if you write from another address we will ask you to write from your own. If we need more time we tell you and take up to 7 more working days. If we refuse, we tell you why within 5 working days.

When you withdraw your consent or object to processing, we stop processing your data at once. This pauses your account until you ask us to resume.

## Changes

When this notice changes in a way that matters, the new version is shown at your next login in your language. You must accept it to continue. Until you do, your account is paused: you can see only the new documents, sign out and use the account deletion page, and we start no new work for you.
